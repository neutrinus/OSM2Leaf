## Question

Does `tools/coord.py` map WGS84 latitude/longitude to the correct US
`BxxRyyR` tile and local cell on the US grid (`R ≥ 0xA0`, `lon_max = 108 −
int(R,16)` negative), and which longitude origin (52742 coord / 52736 tile)
applies to US `COORD001` and `MAPAL001` stream keys (M4.8)?

## Hypothesis

The same formulas hold for US: `x = 32768 + lat*192`, `y = origin + lon*128`,
cell `B = ⌊lat*3/(2*8)⌋*8`, `R = (⌈(108−lon)/8⌉−1)*8` (hex), with 3-digit R
values `0x0A0..0x110` producing negative `lon_max`. `COORD001`/`POINT`/`ADDRE`
use origin 52742 and `MAPAL001` stream key `B` uses origin 52736, as on EU
(C-048, C-111, C-053).

## Method

1. List US `MAPAL001` tiles and confirm `lon_max = 108 − int(R,16)` yields the
   expected negative longitudes (e.g. `B38R0B0R` → −76..−68).
2. Pick ≥ 5 well-known US landmarks with coordinates from Wikipedia (not OSM).
   For each: compute `coord.cell_for`, packed `x/y` (`latlon_to_xy`, origin
   52742), `latlon_to_local` (scale 2), verify the tile file exists in the US
   factory root, and compute haversine round-trip error (packed quantisation).
3. Decode US `COORD001` (same layout as EU: guard at `0x1000`, count at
   `0x1004`, 14-byte big-endian records at `0x1010`) with both origins and
   sanity-check geography.
4. Resolve origin for `MAPAL001` stream keys: decompress zlib streams from
   `B38R0B0R.DAT` (lon box −76..−68), read the 6-byte decompressed key
   `(u16 BE A, u16 BE B, u16 BE tag)` and test which origin places `B` inside
   the tile box.
5. Add US fixtures to `coord.py --selftest` (US cell boxes including negative
   lon/Hawaii/Aleutians, 5 landmarks → tile/cell/local with <350 m error, packed
   round-trip over negative longitudes). Adjust the EU-only lattice message to
   "EU shipped lattice".
6. Run `python3 tools/coord.py --selftest`, `python3 tools/selftest.py --only
   coord`, `python3 tools/selftest.py`, `python3 tools/claims_check.py`.

## Observations

### US tile grid

113 `MAPAL001/BxxRyyR.DAT` tiles (excluding `LEV3456R.DAT`), B `0x18..0x70`,
R `0x0A0..0x110` (3 hex digits). The formula `lon_max = 108 − int(R,16)` gives
negative longitudes throughout, e.g.:

| tile | B,R | lat box | lon box |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `B18R100R` | 24,256 | 16.00..21.33 | −156.00..−148.00 (Hawaii) |

The lattice is not a full rectangle (gaps; Hawaii/Alaska separate). All 5

### US `COORD001`

Decompressed 4352 bytes. Header: guard `0x56` at `0x1000`, count 11 at `0x1004`,
`val7` 7 at `0x1006` — same layout as EU (C-110). 11 region boxes, 14-byte
big-endian records at `0x1010`. Decoded with origin 52742 they cover plausible
N-America geography (e.g. region 1 lat 32.57..42.06, lon −124.44..−114.06;
region 10 Hawaii lat 18.91..28.41, lon −171.95..−154.81; region 9 reaches
lon −52.58). With origin 52736 every longitude shifts +0.046875° (6/128).

Decompressed stream headers (6-byte key `(u16 BE A, u16 BE B, u16 BE tag)`, no
payload bytes recorded):

| 0x5300 | 39951 | 43010 | 0x00fa | 37.4115 | −76.0312 | −75.9844 | yes |

holds on US).

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** `coord.py` maps 5 Wikipedia-sourced US landmarks into the correct
  US `BxxRyyR` tile (all present in the factory `MAPAL001`), inside the cell,
  with packed round-trip error 189–300 m (< a few hundred metres). The
  `lon_max = 108 − int(R,16)` formula produces correct negative longitudes for
  `R ≥ 0xA0` (including 3-digit R, Hawaii `B18R100R`, Aleutians `B48R110R`).
- **FACT:** US `COORD001` (11 regions, 14-byte big-endian records at `0x1010`,
  guard/count/val7 layout) decodes with origin 52742 to plausible N-America
  bounding boxes, consistent with EU (C-110/C-111).
- **FACT:** US `MAPAL001` stream key `B` values in `B38R0B0R.DAT` decode to
  longitudes inside the tile box with origin **52736** and outside with 52742;
  therefore stream key `B = 52736 + lon*128` on US (C-048 confirmed). The
  52742-vs-52736 split (C-053) holds on the US grid.
- **OBSERVED:** Tile/cell and `latlon_to_local` are origin-independent (anchored
  to the cell corner); only packed `y`/global decode of stream/COORD ids depends
  on the origin choice. The 52736/52742 longitude difference remains 6/128 =
  0.046875° (~3.5 km at 37°N).
- **HYPOTHESIS (carried, M4.8):** local scale factor 2 (`0..2047`) — not pinned
  by byte-level US samples here; US fixtures are consistent with it.

## Consequences

- M5.12 can target a US road/POI by lat/lon via `coord.py` (tile/cell known;
  `B38R0B0R` area is covered). Use origin 52742 for COORD001/POINT/ADDRE ids and
  52736 for `MAPAL001` stream keys.
- The US lattice is irregular (112 tiles, gaps); `coord.py` reports "EU shipped
  lattice" membership only — a US tile-set membership check is a possible
  follow-up if needed.
