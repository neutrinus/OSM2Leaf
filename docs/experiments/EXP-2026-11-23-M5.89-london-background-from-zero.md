## Question

Rung Z4: which background types does the London tile use at level 1, and
does the real `Navi.exe` draw OSM water, parks, forest and urban area written
as our own odd (background) records next to the Z3 roads, with no factory
record in the tile?

## Hypothesis

The level-1 odd records use the `clarion_bg` grammar (26-pair layer table,
`0001 0003` groups) with the class in the low byte of the shape type, as on
the Polish tiles (M5.74), and records built from that grammar draw.
Falsifier: a new error line, a failing L3 read, or no fill on the screen.

## Inputs

London bbox `-0.51,51.28,0.33,51.70`; start position 51.5098 N, 0.1342 W;
the M5.88 road records (names off).

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** Sample and hash: above.
2. **Commands.** Commands (`W` scratch, `FAC` factory tree, card build and L4 as in M5.87):

   ```bash
   python3 tools/osm_areas.py --selftest
   python3 tools/osm_areas.py $W/osm/London.osm.pbf --bbox=-0.51,51.28,0.33,51.70 -o $W/z4/areas.jsonl --json
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py --selftest
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py $W/z3/split.jsonl --template $FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT --cell B48R068R \
       --class-table $W/z3/class.json --country 826 --no-names --areas $W/z4/areas.jsonl \
       [--urban-fill 0.1] -o $W/z4/B48R068R.DAT --json
   python3 tools/tile_provenance.py $FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT $W/z4/B48R068R.DAT --require-zero
   python3 tools/s1_wide_dir.py census $W/z4/B48R068R.DAT
   (cd tools/ze0-viewer && python3 harness.py verify $W/z4/B48R068R.DAT)
   python3 tools/cardcheck.py --image $W/z4/card.img
   python3 tools/datask_l3.py mount archive/head256.img --card-image $W/z4/card.img --position 51.5098,-0.1342 \
       --request 0xa6accdef --bytes --json $W/z4/req.json
   ```

   The factory census decompresses every section-1 level-1 record of the
   factory tile, parses it with `clarion_bg.parse_record` (road records fail
   the layer check and are skipped) and counts `(layer, group kind, shape
   type)`; a scratch script, counts only.

3. **Controls.** Controls. Positive: `osm_areas --selftest` (5 checks: closed ways,
   a two-way multipolygon joined into one ring, river line kept, farmland
   dropped, nothing kept for a bbox elsewhere) and `tile_from_zero --selftest`
   (13 checks, new: a park and a water square become one background record
   that reads back with the same types; even then odd in the row block).
   Negative: the M5.88 tile (roads only) is the screen without fills; the M5.87
   empty tile is black.
4. **Scope.** Scope: level 1 odd records, area kinds only. Not written: river centre
   lines (2,002 ways, factory line types not identified), inner rings (3,098
   holes ignored; water drawn over parks covers lakes), labels, level 2.
5. **Falsifier.** Falsifier: as in Hypothesis.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** OSM parks, forest, heath, water and urban area written as our
  own level-1 background records are drawn by the real `Navi.exe` under the
  OSM roads, with no factory record in the tile. Z4 passed for areas.
- **FACT:** level-1 London uses layer 0 `0xc12b` urban, 1 `0xc112` sea,
  3 `0xc11a/b/d` forest/heath/park, 7 `0xc114/5` river/water; the drawing
  order follows the layer number.
- **OBSERVED:** the factory's urban fill is generalised (one ring over the
  whole start parcel) while OSM landuse covers blocks; summed urban rings
  cover 18 % of the start parcel. `--urban-fill 0.1` reproduces the
  factory look; the threshold is a HYPOTHESIS, not a rule from the data.
- **OBSERVED:** our park rings show a red outline the factory's St James's
  Park does not; the cause (type, ref, or overlapping `landuse=grass` rings)
  is UNKNOWN.
- **UNKNOWN:** types of layers 4, 5, 8–11 (river lines, railways, site
  areas); sea (`0xc112`) is not needed inside the London bbox.

## Conclusion

`tools/osm_areas.py` + `tile_from_zero.py --areas` give the from-zero London
tile its background: water, parks, forest, heath and urban area from OSM,
drawn at L4 with roads. Next: Z5 (names, classes) and the open items below.
