## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the harness paths, commands, statuses, target table and corpus
  counts in the rewritten page are the ones the tools execute today; the
  outputs above are the evidence.
- **FACT:** `targets.json` has six targets: one `synthetic` (PASS) and five
  `blocked`; no firmware reader is an L3 target yet.
- **FACT:** a located VA is not a runnable target. `runner.py` accepts only
  `entry.kind` `synthetic`/`blocked` and maps only `SYNTHETIC_PROGRAMS`; there
  is no firmware entry kind, so `tile_reader` and `drstr_parser` stay
  `BLOCKED` even though M3.5/M3.10 located their reader VAs. The page now says
  this explicitly and gives the extension as step 4 of "Adding an M3 reader".
- **FACT:** `l3check.py` exists and passes its self-test; its `--root`/`--image`
  are recorded-only until M9.12, and its reader rows are BLOCKED.
- **OBSERVED:** `UNVERIFIABLE` is not emitted anywhere in `tools/l3_harness/`;
  in this repo it belongs to `tools/zlibinfo.py`/`tools/lev3456.py` (exit 3).
  The page defines it as such instead of inventing an L3 meaning.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether any reader VA can actually execute under PySh4/Ghidra
  without unmodelled instructions; no reader has been attempted.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the concurrent M9.8 `l3check.py` interface is final; the
  page records the flags and statuses it has now.
