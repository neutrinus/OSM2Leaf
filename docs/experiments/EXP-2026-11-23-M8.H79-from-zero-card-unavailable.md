  not the decisive witness. TODO M3.43's recorded 330 s negative on the EU card

  second prerequisite.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** once a pinned from-zero card is available and M8.C1p lets the
  EU card reach map state, the paired run will find a concrete divergence in
  the file-open sequence or the first screen (the from-zero `B48R068R.DAT` is

  change" if no position is seeded.
- **UNKNOWN:** which files the from-zero card actually opens at L4, and the
  first screen hash/pixel difference; nothing in this record measures them.
