## Question

What are the "entries past `geo_len`" in the three B30R048R background records
that M9.22 found (dir `0x2ad00`, slots 6/8/11, `k = 1`) — layers, shared data or
padding — and can the bounded layer-table read accept and rebuild them with
`strict=True` without changing any control tile?

## Hypothesis

M9.22 (C-2639, HYPOTHESIS) framed the records as having a layer table longer
than the geometry region with used entries after the region end. The competing
explanation is that the bytes past the declared geometry region are *not* layer
table entries but belong to a following section, and the parser overruns
`geo_len` into them. **Falsifier:** if any entry past `geo_len` is a real layer
read by the header's geometry model (a used layer whose data lives inside the
geometry region and whose table slot is past `geo_len`), or if bounding the
scan changes the parse/rebuild of any record that parsed before M9.23, the
bounded read is wrong and the records must remain opaque/refused.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (VERIFIED):** the "entries past `geo_len`" are the leading bytes of the
  names section (`w11 = geo_off + geo_len`), misread because `parse_record`
  scanned the layer table without bounding by `geo_len`. They are not layer
  table entries and not layers (C-2860).
- **FACT (VERIFIED):** bounding the table scan to `geo_len` (and setting
  `nlayers = geo_len` when no used entry is found within the region) accepts the
  three records and lets `build_record` reproduce them byte-identically with
  `strict=True`; the bound does not bind on the records that parsed before
  M9.23 and leaves the three control tiles unchanged (C-2861–C-2863).
- **FACT (VERIFIED):** `harness.py verify` reports `parse refused 0` on B30R048R;
  the 3 records moved from refused/opaque to rebuilt, so B30R048R now has
  `background rebuild ok 2958 (opaque 62, parse refused 0)` vs 2955/65/3 before.
- **FACT (VERIFIED):** the B30R048R L2 tile checks and semantic digest (16,684)
  are unchanged; the golden is not regenerated because nothing the golden
  measures changed (C-2864).
- **RESOLVED:** C-2639's hypothesis (layer table longer than the geometry region,
  used entries after the region end) is resolved by C-2860: the apparent extra
  table was a names-section overread. C-2639 is kept as the historical record.
- **OBSERVED:** the 62 remaining opaque-by-design background records in B30R048R
  (and 6 in B30R060R) are still kept verbatim; the model now rebuilds 3 more
  records than M9.22 did, but tile-level round-trip already held.

## Conclusion

The three refused B30R048R records are not an unmodelled layer layout: their
declared geometry region is 26 empty table units and the bytes after it are the
names section, which the parser misread by scanning past `geo_len`.
`clarion_bg.parse_record` now bounds the table scan to `geo_len` (ZE0 delta in
`ze0-tools.patch`), so the records parse and rebuild byte-identically with
`strict=True`; `harness.py verify` reports `parse refused 0` on B30R048R and the
control tiles are unchanged. The L2 golden is unchanged (stated reason: tile
bytes and semantic features unchanged). Format impact is recorded in C-2860–
C-2866 and the bounded-read note in `docs/format/map-tiles.md`. Still open: the
62 opaque-by-design background records kept verbatim (not a regression; out of
scope).
