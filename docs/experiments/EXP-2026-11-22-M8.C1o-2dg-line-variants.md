## Question

M8.C1f left seven 2DG commands unexecuted in the London map lists
(`0xb3007602` ×5, `0xb10038c2` ×2, C-6936). What are their operand layouts and
colour sources, and can the interpreter draw them without losing map pixels?

## Hypothesis

Both are polyline variants emitted by the renderer (`XGLDLL.dll` in build A,
`auirtdll.dll` in build B) with a fixed header; their exact word counts can be
read from the emitters and checked against a `gfx-trace` dump. Falsifier: after
implementing them the L4 screenshot loses map pixels, or the trace still
reports skipped commands.

## Observations

**O1 — Emitter layouts (static).** In build B `auirtdll.dll` (uncompressed
section 0, file `0x04083200`, 369 KiB), fn `0x03d0b408` loads the literal
`0xb1000000` from `0x03d0b484` and writes six header words before the vertex
loop; word 4 is `(r6+r7)`, the number of 8-byte vertex records read from `r5`
and packed as `x<<16 | u16(y)`. Fn `0x03d0b502` loads `0xb3000000` from
`0x03d0b560` and branches on `flags & 0x400`: the pointer form writes
`[op, N, *r5]`, the inline form `[op, N1+N2, V0..]` (lines 291–337 of the
disassembly). The same two emitters are in build A `XGLDLL.dll` at
`0x031204ac` and `0x031205a4` (literals at `0x03120528`/`0x03120604`), so both
modules agree; the runtime TLB trace of the map run shows `r13 = 0xb10038c2`
built at `XGLDLL.dll 0x031204be`, i.e. the map renderer emits them.

**O2 — The dump.** With `gfx-trace=on`, the EU 2011 start screen logs
`ze0-2dl: ... b3007602 00000026 ffffff34 ...` after
`18000000 000000f0 00004004`, and two
`b10038c2 46317fff ... 00000024/00000028 00000000` commands. For the `0xb3`
at `0x0eddaf80`, `pc + 0xffffff34 = 0x0eddaeb4`, which is exactly the first
vertex of the preceding `d0007038 00000026 ...` polygon; the same holds for
the `0xb3` at `0x0eddebc8` (`pc + OFF = 0x0eddeafc`). `0xb1` is followed by
36 and 40 coordinate words respectively, matching `word 4 & 0xffff`.

**O3 — Colour state.** The `0xb3` commands are preceded by reg `0xf0` =
`0x00004004` / `0x001a4004` / `0x001f4004` (low half always `0x4004`); the
`0xb1` commands by reg `0x88` = `0x000000ff`, and their word 1 is the constant
`0x46317fff` (`0x4631` is dark grey, 17/17/17 in (A)RGB1555). These are the
colour sources used by `ze0-0016`; the exact meaning of the high half of reg
`0xf0` and of the `0x7fff` mask remains unknown.

**O4 — Verification boot.** With `ze0-0016` the same card boots to the same
position. The eight 2DG list summaries are
`1/0/1/1/1/2241/298/133 drawn, 0 skipped` = **2676 drawn, 0 skipped**;
C-6936 measured 2669 drawn + 7 skipped = 2676 commands on the same lists, so
the seven previously skipped commands are now executed and no command was
lost or invented. Both screenshots have 384 000 non-black pixels; a bytewise
RGB comparison of the two PNGs finds **342 of 384 000 pixels different
(0.09 %)** — small new outline strokes on area boundaries, no removed map
content. The new `0xb3` stroke is visible as a dark grey line along the top
edge of St James's Park; the rest of the map (roads, shields, labels) is
unchanged to the eye.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** both command families have fixed, module-independent layouts; the
  skipped count of C-6936 was a decoder gap, not unreadable firmware state.
- **FACT:** `0xb3` reuses the vertex array of the preceding `0xd0` polygon and
  is therefore an outline stroke over that area, not new geometry.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the colour sources in O3 are the stroke colours; the visual
  check in O4 tests whether the result matches the factory render.
- **UNKNOWN:** the high half of reg `0xf0`, the `0x7fff` mask of `0xb1`, and
  the `0xb2`/`0xb4`–`0xb7` variants, which have not appeared in these lists.

## Conclusion

`ze0-0016` adds the two remaining map line variants of the 2DG command set:
`0xb1` (6-word header, N inline vertices, colour from word 1) and `0xb3`
(pointer form, N vertices at `op + OFF`, colour from register `0xf0`). Both
are drawn with `polyline()` and advance by their exact word count, so the
resync of `ze0-0014` is no longer used for them. The L4 map run goes from
2669 drawn / 7 skipped to 2676 drawn / 0 skipped, and the screenshot changes
in 0.09 % of pixels. The falsifier is not met: no map pixels were lost. Claims
C-8390–C-8395 replace the open Next step in EXP-2026-11-22-M8.C1f; the UI
surface and the `0xb2`/`0xb4`–`0xb7` variants remain out of scope.
