## Interpretation

- **VERIFIED (precheck):** M4.30 `two->two` L1 miss clustering reproduced exactly (188: 103/101/157); L2 clustering reproduced (61: 38/37/54). (C-2990)
- **VERIFIED (same-way/tile identity):** In the matched `two->two` `no_*` corpus, OSM `from==to` (`feq`) and tile `F==T` are the *same* case set at both levels (L1 101/101, L2 37/37; no feq/F!=T, no !feq/F==T among misses; 0 in hits). Bearing matching maps a same-way-id prohibition to a single tile entry. All feq misses are `no_u_turn`. This makes the M4.23 0xff-on-T-naming-F test structurally inapplicable as written when F==T: it would require an ff word on T naming F, i.e. a self-naming prohibition on the same entry. (C-2991)
- **VERIFIED (u-turn representation, negative for tested self-form):** `no_u_turn` dominates `two->two` misses (103/188 L1, 38/61 L2). The feq/F==T subclass (101/37) shows **no** `F_names_F_ff` self-prohibition (0/101 L1, 0/37 L2), no `F_names_T_ff`, no `T_names_F` present; 64/17 have no ff words on T/F. The 14/6 `ff_naming_F_on_other_entries` cases place an ff-naming-F word on a different entry than T(=F) — a counted alternate-location candidate but minority (14/101, 6/37 of feq misses). Non-feq `no_u_turn` misses are rare (2/1) and `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere`. The 4/5 `no_u_turn` hits are all !feq/F!=T, consistent with the direction-explicit convention applying when from/to map to distinct tile entries. (C-2992, C-2993)
- **OBSERVED (OSM-vs-tile splitting, matched population):** Among matched misses/hits there is no duplicate OSM way bearing and no/low bearing ambiguity (`dup_way` 0; `amb_margin<15` 0/188 L1, 3/61 L2). The "splitting" relevant to this class is representational: OSM encodes u-turn as `from==to` (same way/member) and tile presents one matching entry (F==T). This is not fixed by bearing disambiguation in the matched set. Count-mismatch exclusions (492/737) are outside scope (M4.34). (C-2994)
- **Conclusion (mapping normalization / alternate encoding):** A simple mapping normalization cannot make F!=T when bearing matching yields the same tile entry for the same OSM way; the missing piece is an **encoding/representation rule for same-entry (feq/F==T) u-turns**. The tested self-form `ff naming F on F/T` is absent (0). Alternate possibilities remain: ff naming F on another entry (14/6 feq misses, counted), a different word position/byte1, a graph/edge representation not captured by the matched-node regulation words, or true absence — **firmware consumer VA required** (rule 13; C-2629/C-2805 context). The non-feq `two->two` remainder (87 L1: mostly left/right turns, `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere` dominant) aligns with M4.25's absent-encoding majority and is not explained by same-way identity.

## Conclusion

M4.33 reproduced M4.30's `two->two` clustering and performed a counted per-case audit (`tools/m433_twotwo_audit.py`). Key finding: in matched `two->two` `no_*` misses, `osm_from_eq_to` and `F==T` are identical sets (L1 101/188, L2 37/61), all feq are `no_u_turn`, and none show `F_names_F_ff` self-prohibition (0). `no_u_turn` hits occur only when !feq/F!=T (4/5). Matched-population bearing duplication/ambiguity is absent/low, so this is a same-way/same-entry representation+encoding class, not a bearing-split match failure. No mapping normalization that preserves bearing identity resolves F==T; the tested alternate self-encoding form is negative; alternate location/form/absence remains unknown pending firmware confirmation (rule 13). Claims C-2990–C-2994 added.
