## Interpretation

- **FACT (C-3400):** none of the six targets waits on M1.2f/M1.19, M9.28 or
  M9.24 any more: M9.6, M9.11, M9.13, M9.14 and M9.17 are done with runs, and
  M9.10's prerequisites (US VAs, accessor, byte order) are met.
- **FACT (C-3401):** `l3_precond` says `BLOCKED` for `tile_reader`,
  `drstr_parser` and `mmain_reader` because the gate only knows `runner.py`
  entries; those targets run through `datask_l3.py`/`vcard_l3.py`/
  `mmain_l3.py`, which the gate does not model.
- **OBSERVED (C-3402):** given the EU head, `l3_precond` classes the US
  target's VAs against EU sections (`INIT`); the verdict is unaffected, but
  the per-VA lines are wrong for US2011 unless `--image archive/us256.img`
  is passed.
- **FACT (C-3403):** M9.10 is the only one of the six that is still open, and
  it is `READY` (its own runner, then M9.55's `l3check` row).

## Conclusion

Every target M9.33 names now has a verdict. M9.6, M9.11, M9.13, M9.14 and
M9.17 are done, and each was re-run on the corrected engine (M9.36). M9.10 is
