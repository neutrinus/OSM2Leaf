## Question

Does the KIWI Background Data Frame (chapter 7.3 of `kiwiread`) describe the odd-partner background records of a ZE0 `MAPAL001` grid tile — specifically the 7.3.1 distribution header / element unit table, the 7.3.2.1 background type units and the 7.3.2.2.1 minimum graphics records? If it does not, at which byte does the layout stop fitting, and what layout should the writer use?

## Hypothesis

The two formats share a vocabulary (typed layers, 11-bit coordinate counts, `int8` deltas), so the shape idea may be KIWI-like; the prior from M4.63 (`C-8106`, `C-8109`) is that the container is different. Expected result: negative for the frame. **Falsifier:** any odd record whose `u16@0` yields a valid `SWS` header, whose element units and type units stay inside the geometry block, and whose graphics records satisfy `SWS(hdr) = 12 + 2*ncoord` for every shape — on two cards. Such a fit would make `kiwiread` a reader for our background records and refute the prior.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (tool, negative):** 0 of 17,659 odd background records of three tiles / two cards fit the KIWI 7.3 frame. The dominant first failure is `header_size` (17,032 records): ZE0 has no KIWI `SWS(u16@0)` header word at the start of the geometry block — `u16@0` is the first layer-table offset/length pair (`0` when the pair is empty, `26` at the sea example). In the remaining records the KIWI element-unit/type-unit reads point outside the declared block or give implausible type counts.
- **OBSERVED (positive sample):** the two hand-picked records are genuine ZE0 background: 26 layer pairs, 10/10 closed sea polygons (`0xc112`) in `B30R048R`, and a mix `0xc112`/`0xc115`/`0xc12b` with 4/4 closed polygons in `B30R060R`. The US card fails in the same way, so the negative is not an EU-build artefact.
- **FACT (narrower, refines C-8106):** a ZE0 shape is one word shorter than `mingr_t`: on `000e c112 ffff 00c4 0771 b691`, reading `count=14, type=0xc112, ref=0xffff, x0=0x00c4, y0=0x0771` gives a self-consistent 38-byte ZE0 shape, while the KIWI alignment gives `ncoord=274`, `code=0xffff` and an implied 560-byte record vs `SWS(hdr)=28`. The shared idea is the 11-bit count plus `int8` deltas, not the field layout.
- **HYPOTHESIS (not tested here):** the resemblance is a common design heritage (both use parcel/level/link vocabulary); no causal claim is made. No field in `docs/format/` was renamed and no firmware VA is attributed.
- **UNKNOWN:** a level-1 `0xc114` river record was not found in the scanned tiles; a real KIWI volume remains M4.77's positive control.

## Conclusion

M4.70's Done-when is met in its negative branch: the exact first mismatch is recorded (byte 0 of the geometry block / the KIWI header word; then element unit `poff=38, plen=90` on a 592-byte block), with a counted corpus, a synthetic positive control and a ZE0-side rebuild control. The KIWI 7.3 background frame is **not** a reader for our odd records; the layout a writer must use is the already documented `clarion_bg` 26-pair table + `0001 0003` groups + `count/type/ref/x0/y0 + int8` shapes. No new type-code table is added — the `0x..12/0x..14/0x..15/0x..1a` table in `docs/format/map-tiles.md` already carries those codes, with the count-byte wrap caveat. A short negative note is added to that page.
