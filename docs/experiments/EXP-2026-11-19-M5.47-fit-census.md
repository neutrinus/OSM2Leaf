## Question

For the M5.34 result tile and the manifests it rebuilds, does the new byte
length fit the FAT cluster chain `imgpatch` would reuse, and if not, by how much
and which file?

## Hypothesis

M5.45 measured the writer's block budgets but not the **image-side** chain: a
tile that grows by megabytes is unlikely to fit the slack of its factory chain,
so the expectation is `needs_relocation` for `B48R058R.DAT` and `fits` for the
manifests (whose sizes are unchanged or tiny). **Falsifier:** the census reports
`fits` for a file whose delta exceeds its chain slack, or reports
`needs_relocation` for a synthetic over-budget file that still has spare slack.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the M5.34 level-1 tile (delta +4,671,350 B) and the M5.44 level-2
  tile (delta +138,994 B) both exceed the factory chain slack of 22,272 B on a
  65,536 B cluster, so both are `needs_relocation` (71 and 2 extra clusters).
  M5.30's `Stop:` condition on the size axis **does** fire: the changed tile
  cannot be written in place.
- **FACT:** it is a **one-file** problem on the length axis. 479 of 480 files
  have delta 0 (and fit); the only file with a non-zero delta is the tile. The
  second file whose **bytes** differ, `DRSTR001.DAT`, keeps its 11,968 B length
  (delta 0, slack 53,568 B), so it needs no cluster either. A relocation of one
  chain is not "reconcile the whole tree".
- **FACT:** `imgpatch --allow-resize` cannot perform that relocation today even
  though the card has 35,851 contiguous free clusters starting at 29,687: its
  allocator starts at `last_chain_cluster + 1` = 94,807, walks to the FAT's
  last entry and picks 13 clusters (115,187–115,199) that lie past the
  partition's last valid cluster 115,186, so `_check_envelope` refuses with
  `internal-range`. A write-side fix (allocator bounded by the partition's
  cluster count) is a prerequisite of M5.30, not an optional improvement.
- **OBSERVED:** free space is not the constraint (35,851 free clusters in one
  run); the constraint is the allocator's candidate range.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the unit reads a relocated chain at all (rule 13) and
  whether the appended tail the writer adds is the right model of growth (M5.45
  bounded it; it does not change the fit question).

## Conclusion

The census answers M5.30's first question on real bytes: the level-1 changed
tile does **not** fit its factory chain (22,272 B slack against 4,671,350 B
growth, 71 extra clusters), the level-2 tile does not either (+138,994 B, 2
extra clusters), and both are a one-file relocation because the remaining 479
files fit. The write path's `--allow-resize` dry run currently refuses because
its allocator reaches past the partition end; that is recorded for M5.30 rather
than fixed here (this task is read-only, no image copy). No claim in
`docs/format/` changed; the reader matrix is untouched (rule 13).
