## Question

With the EU card prepared for the US unit and the unit's own kernel and
flash, does the navigation draw its map, and does it take a GPS position in
Wrocław (the M1.50 question: the US card keeps the car in Kentucky)?

## Hypothesis

The launcher passes the card (M8.C1m/C1r). On the EU map the Wrocław fix
lies on a tile, so the map-matching gate of M8.H78 passes and the car
follows the fix. Falsifier: the car stays at the backup position, or the
map does not draw.

## Method

1. Emulator-only guest patches (`--ipl-extra`, NK1 physical addresses; the
   NK1 `PowerMonitor.dll` is the card's image-B module at `+0x310000`,
   bytes compared equal):
   `0x081fb6f4 0b0000e0` (`AccOffProc` `0x03ce56f4` → `rts`) and
   `0x081fc428 0b0000e0` (`PowerOFF_Sequence` `0x03ce6428` → `rts`).
   Without them the guest halts in the OAL loop `0x8804808c`
   (`ldc r0,sr; bra .`) after `Pow Off 1st Detect` / `Acc Off Not Notify
   SystemEXE`, because there is no VCAN peer on NK1 (M8.H75).
2. Each run: GPS peer `fix 51.0756 17.0478`, `alt 137`, `sats 8`, `on`;
   `gyro0 = 4096`; wait for `NAVIMAP_CC_NTF_OWNFIRSTMAPDRAW_END` or a Navi.exe
   exception in `serial.log`; panel `tap 277 98` to unlock; screenshots.
3. For the moving case: `speed 30`, `heading 45`, `drive on`, `on`,
   `pulses-milli 5000` (the M8.H32 recipe).
4. Flash variants (VSram = flash `0x400000–0x4FFFFF`, the LeafSDTools "SRAM"
   dump, found byte-identical at that offset; VFlash at `0x500000`):

   | run | VSram |
   | --- | --- |
   | F1, F2 | unchanged (full dump) |
   | N | all `0xFF` |
   | A | `0x400000–0x47FFFF` `0xFF` |
   | B | `0x480000–0x4FFFFF` `0xFF` |
   | A1 | `0x400000–0x43FFFF` `0xFF` |
   | A2 | `0x440000–0x47FFFF` `0xFF` |

## Observations

**O1 — Launcher.** Every run logs `Target ID Code >> 10200212`,
`Launcher [Area:20 >> USA]`, no `Error Proc`, and
`Process Create!!! -> \windows\Navi.exe`.

**O2 — Full dump (F1, F2).** Navi.exe takes an exception before the first
map draw, ~2–3 min after start:
`Exception 0e0 … 'Navi.exe' AKY=00004001 PC=8800f048(NK.EXE+0x0000f048)`
with `RA=0353213a(osm_msgdll.dll+0x213a)` (F1) and
`RA=034943b0(objdll.dll+0x143b0)` (F2), then `ErrorAnalyzing_Exception`; the
guest ends in the OAL halt loop `0x8804808c` (reached from `0x88047a60`).
Screen black. No `OWNFIRSTMAPDRAW_END`.

**O3 — VSram erased (N).** `OWNFIRSTMAPDRAW_END` about 1 min after start.
The car starts at the card's default (London, Piccadilly Circus). Standing
with the Wrocław fix it stays in London (60 s). With the moving recipe
(step 3) the screen shows **Wrocław** within ~75 s: Ulica Bardzka, Aleja Armii
Krajowej, Hala Kupców, Ulica Ziębicka, with the car arrow on the map
(screenshot kept outside the repository: vendor map data).

**O4 — Bisection.**

| run | `OBJ_BackUp_Load Error` lines | `UnLoad BackUpInfo @OBJ_Carpos_initialize` | outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 7+ | yes | map drawn |
| B | 1 | no | no map, no exception within 7 min, screen black |
| A1 | 5 | **no** | **map drawn** |
| A2 | 4 | yes | **Navi.exe exception** (`RA=objdll.dll+0x1723a`) |

In A1 the car-position backup loads (no `Carpos` error) and the map still
draws; in A2 it does not load and Navi still crashes.

**O5 — Layout hint.** Counting non-`0xFF` bytes per 64 KiB block of VSram
gives a period of `0x30000` (`0x400000`≈`0x430000`, `0x410000`≈`0x440000`,
`0x420000`≈`0x450000`), i.e. probably two copies of a 3-block area. The
NK1 kernel prints backup area 2 at pages 79/80 (`0x480000`/`0x490000`).

## Interpretation

- **The EU map takes a Wrocław position on the unit's software.** With the
  map under the fix, the M8.H78 gate passes and the car moves to Wrocław
  (O3). This confirms M1.50 H2c: in the car, Kentucky holds because the US
  card has no map at Wrocław.
- **The unit's saved navigation data (VSram) stops the EU card from drawing
  the map** in the emulator (O2, 2 of 2). The trigger is in the first half of
  VSram but is **not** the car-position backup itself (A1 loads it and
  works); it lies in data that A1 removes and A2 keeps — most likely the
  first copy of the 3-block area at `0x400000–0x42FFFF` or the block at
  `0x430000` (HYPOTHESIS; which object it is was not isolated). Likely
  candidates are records that hold US-map references (stored route,
  destinations/history, map-specific caches) — HYPOTHESIS.
- **Risk for the car:** the same VSram is in the unit, so the patched EU
  card may crash `Navi.exe` there too (a navigation restart or a reset
  loop). The emulator differences (no VCAN/audio micom, power-off patched
  out) make this a HYPOTHESIS for the car, but the A/B/N controls tie the
  crash to the flash data, not to the emulator.
