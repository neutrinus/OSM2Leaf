## Question

Do the KIWI level, block-set and block-management record layouts (6.1.1, 6.1.2, 6.2.1 in `kiwiread`) describe the per-level totals of `MMAIN001.DAT` and the level tables of tiles on EU2011 and US2011?

## Hypothesis

After M4.63's "same concepts, different container" result, the expected verdict is `same idea, other layout` for the level counts and `absent` for the two block-management structures. **Falsifier:** a field passes the KIWI range test on both cards with non-degenerate values and its value behaves like the KIWI definition (e.g. display-scale flags in the defined bit range, parcel counts within KIWI's small count, geonums inside ±90/±180).

## Method

2. **Commands.**

   ```bash
   python3 tools/kiwi_probe.py header /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.29-factory-root/MMAIN001.DAT
   python3 tools/kiwi_probe.py header /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory/MMAIN001.DAT
   python3 tools/kiwi_probe.py header /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.29-factory-root/MAPAL001/B28R070R.DAT
   python3 tools/kiwi_probe.py header /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory/MAPAL001/B48R0C0R.DAT
   python3 tools/mesh_grid.py list /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.29-factory-root/MAPAL001
   python3 tools/parcel_census.py /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.29-factory-root/MAPAL001/B28R070R.DAT --sections 1 --levels 1
   python3 tools/mmain.py /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.29-factory-root/MMAIN001.DAT --list
   ```
3. **Controls.** `kiwi_probe.py --selftest` supplies synthetic KIWI-shaped positive controls for all four header checks; they must pass while the real files fail (M4.63, C-8100–C-8104).
4. **Scope.** The three KIWI record types only; every field at `MMAIN001 0x1ac` and the tile `0x1ac` table on two cards. Excluded: road/background frames (M4.69/M4.70), a real KIWI disc (M4.77) and firmware VAs.
5. **Falsifier.** Count fields that pass only because their value is 0 or 1 separately; a "match" made only of such fields is not a match.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the KIWI `lmr_t` reading of `0x1ac` gives `lvl=0`, a display-scale word `0x6800001` outside the KIWI field range and a parcel count of 145 (larger than KIWI's small count), on both cards. The `pdmdh_t` geonum of 143.922° is outside any latitude/longitude frame. No field passes the structural test with informative values: the only zeros/ones are `lvl=0` and `bsmr_off=2`, which are counted as degenerate and do not support a match.
- **FACT:** the `bsmr_t` and `bmt_t` structures have no counterpart at these offsets: `nbsmr=16191` and `nlmr=16191` are constant across both cards and are the ZE0 level/box word counts, not KIWI record counts.
- **OBSERVED:** the ZE0 pyramid is instead described by `MMAIN001` level totals plus each tile's 6-word directory groups; `mesh_grid.py` reproduces the 45-tile lattice and `parcel_census.py` the per-directory boxes, so our own structures are self-consistent.
- **UNKNOWN:** no KIWI version is available to broaden the range tests (M4.65); no firmware VA is attributed by this record.

## Conclusion

M4.67's Done-when is met with verdicts: `lmr_t` → `same idea, other layout`, `pdmdh_t` → `absent` as a geonum frame, `bsmr_t`/`bmt_t` → `absent`. No field passed with non-degenerate values, so no KIWI field name was added to `docs/format/*.md`.
