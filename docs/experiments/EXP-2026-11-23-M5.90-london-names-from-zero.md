## Question

Rung Z5: how does the factory store the London start screen's street names,
road numbers and place labels, and does the firmware draw OSM names written
the same way into our from-zero tile?

## Hypothesis

Street names are `1=NAME;2=REF` entries of the odd record's names section,
reached through the road shapes' `name_off` (M5.7); place labels are point
anchors in layer 25 with their own names entries. Falsifier: no label drawn,
a new error line, or labels at wrong roads.

## Inputs

As M5.89; London start position 51.5098 N, 0.1342 W.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** See the Sample bullet above.
2. **Commands.**

   ```bash
   python3 tools/osm_areas.py --selftest
   python3 tools/osm_areas.py $W/osm/London.osm.pbf --bbox=-0.51,51.28,0.33,51.70 -o $W/z5/areas.jsonl --json
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py --selftest
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py $W/z3/split.jsonl --template $FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT --cell B48R068R \
       --class-table $W/z3/class.json --country 826 --areas $W/z5/areas.jsonl --urban-fill 0.1 --places \

   (cd tools/ze0-viewer && python3 harness.py verify $W/z5/B48R068R.DAT)

   python3 tools/datask_l3.py mount archive/head256.img --card-image $W/z5/card.img --position 51.5098,-0.1342 \
       --request 0xa6accdef --bytes --json $W/z5/req.json
   ```

   section with `ze0_decode.parse_names_section` and a scratch walker (counts,
   entry layouts and the first label words only).

3. **Controls.**
   Negative: the same tile with names flags `0x2000` instead of `0x2800`
   (screen byte-identical, so the flag is not what draws the label);
   `tile_from_zero --selftest` checks that appended place entries keep the
   road offsets and move the sub-table intact (17 checks).
4. **Scope.**
   way; place kinds city/town/suburb/quarter/neighbourhood/village; no
   shields, no area labels (park/lake names), no POI labels.
5. **Falsifier.**

## Observations

**O1 — the factory names section (start parcel).** Header `0006 02f3`
(755 entries) and four `(offset, count)` pairs; the item sub-table is the
fourth pair (`0x308a`, 1,594 items) where `fa_encode.names_section` writes
the second. Entry layouts:

| entries | type | flags | layout | examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| area names | `0x018f`, `0x8194`, `0x0188` | `0x2800` | type, flags, x, y, len, text | BUCKINGHAM PALACE GARDENS, ST JAMES'S PARK LAKE |
| place / POI labels | `0xc068`, `0x8092`/`0xc092`, `0x80c9`/`0xc0c9`, `0x80ce`, `0x80cf`/`0xc0cf`, `0x80d1`/`0xc0d1` | `0x4800` | type, flags, x, y, style, len, text | MAYFAIR, SOHO, W1, LEICESTER SQUARE (`0xc068`); PICCADILLY CIRCUS, TROCADERO CENTRE (`0x80cf`) |
| road names | `0x00ff` | `0x2800` | type, flags, 0, 0, len, text | `1=SHAFTESBURY AVENUE;2=A401` (712) |

Layer 25 is one group of kind byte `3` with 82 points `0xc00c, 0xffff, x, y`;
the first two points have MAYFAIR's and SOHO's coordinates.

**O2 — the tile.** `osm_areas`: 1,520 place nodes (suburb 439, locality
338, neighbourhood 218, village 182, quarter 131, hamlet 125, town 84, city
3). `tile_from_zero`: 1,057 place labels written, 602 names sections, 1,237
records, tile SHA-256
`490250396ec57eefadeda0e515152948ec3844dd0fed98bb5b49b7e283e5d1f4`;
`factory_records=0/1237`; `harness verify`: `background rebuild ok 634 bad 0`,
`roundtrip OK`; census OK.

**O3 — names against the factory (start parcel).** Road names: factory 677
distinct, ours 569, 519 in both. Factory-only names are courts, mews and
passages (`ADAM AND EVE COURT`, `ANGEL COURT`, `BAKERS MEWS`): footways and
pedestrian ways the class table does not carry. Road numbers: factory 21,
ours 22, the 21 factory numbers all present (`A4380` extra).

**O4 — L1/L3/L4.** Card SHA-256 `92ce873d…8839470`: `cardcheck` PASS; L3
`--request 0xa6accdef` reply `sub 3`; L4 with the factory's 1,043
error/fail lines. Screenshot SHA-256
`a1b95d9a967baa35b08a62502c0b844ddda6472cb10c3086802f1de77b9ce8a7`
(OSM data only, given to Marek): street labels along their roads (OXFORD
STREET, SHAFTESBURY AVENUE, CHARING CROSS ROAD, PALL MALL, THE MALL,
BATEMAN STREET), brown on main roads and dark on minor ones, and place
labels SOHO, ST GILES, ST. JAMES'S, LONDON, SEVEN DIALS. No road-number
shield is drawn (the factory screen has A4, A400, A401, A3201). The names
built without `--places` (screen `0904d5d7…818b20b`) and with flags
`0x2000` gave the same screen.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** OSM street names written as `1=NAME;2=REF` entries in our own
  odd records, linked by `name_off`, are drawn on their roads; place labels
  written as `0xc068`/`0x4800` entries after the road names plus a layer-25
  point group at the same coordinates are drawn at their places.
- **FACT:** the `2=REF` part alone does not draw a shield; the shield needs
  something else (candidates: the factory's `points` section, w15/w16, whose
  tail holds `c000 0004 …` items, or a road-shape attribute; M4.90).
- **OBSERVED:** the names entry flags (`0x2000`/`0x2800`) and the sub-table
  pair position do not change the drawn screen.
- **OBSERVED:** 77 % of the factory's start-parcel street names come back
  from OSM with the current class table; the rest are pedestrian ways.

## Conclusion

The from-zero London tile now has street names and place labels from OSM,
drawn by the real firmware. Open: shields (M5.97) and the measured
`highway → class` table (M5.98).
