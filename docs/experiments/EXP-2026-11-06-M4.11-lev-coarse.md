## Question

What do overview levels 3–5 in `MAPAL001/LEV3456R.DAT` contain, and can one of
them be rebuilt from decoded records byte-identically, as a prerequisite for
regenerating the coarse views from OpenStreetMap?

## Hypothesis

The coarse file is the tile container with whole-database directories, so a
decoder that handles the directory/record layout, the background group
grammar, the road-shape grammar, the level-5 names section and stock zlib
should rebuild at least one level exactly. Levels 3 and 4 are expected to be
less complete because their names/point-index sections are not documented.

```bash
python3 harness.py verify \
  /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/root/MAPAL001/LEV3456R.DAT
```

The decoder reads the header level table at `0x1ac`, the section table at
`0x800`, the group table at its section address, then each level's directories
(`size/2, rows, cols, y, x, typ` head; per-row `u32` block address and
`(compressed words, plain words)` pairs). Records are zlib streams (`78 9c`);
level 6 is raw and is preserved. A background block is a 26-pair layer table
plus one group per layer; a road block/record is an 11-pair table plus shapes.
The level-5 rebuild re-encodes every record, recompresses with stock zlib level
6/windowBits 15/memLevel 8 and pads the stream to 4 bytes, then lays the
directory and row blocks out at 256-byte boundaries and replaces the factory
level region.

### Grammar facts observed while decoding

- The background layer table has **26** entries, not 11; its size is the first
  non-empty offset. The road table has 11.
- A group is `0001 0003`, kind byte, count byte, shapes. Its **count byte wraps
  mod 256**: the 604 islets of the level-5 Ireland/Scotland layer 8 are stored
  as count `0x5c` (92). Walking the self-delimiting shapes to the layer end
  reproduces all 62 level-5 backgrounds; trusting the count drops data.
- Level-5 road layers use classes 3–6 in layer 6, 1–2 in layer 7 and ferries in
  layer 0. Levels 3/4 put roads in layer 6 and carry one record per parcel
  (even slot) beside the background (odd slot).
- 19 level-5 records carry a 2–6-word segment at unit 11 (before the road
  block); its values are small but its meaning is unknown. It is preserved
  verbatim and does not block the rebuild.
- Streams are `78 9c` and re-compress exactly with stock zlib 1.3.1
  (level 6, windowBits 15, memLevel 8) plus zero padding to 4 bytes.
  Python 3.14's zlib-ng reproduces none of them.

## Interpretation

- **C-990** container/directory geometry: verified.
- **C-991 / C-992 / C-993** level content and counts: verified on the 2011
  file. The type semantics (sea, coast, forest, …) are observed from the
  shape type and the vendor `AREA_CLASSES` mapping; some types (`0x..08/09`,
  `0x..11`, `0x..26`) remain unknown.
- **C-994** background grammar and the wrapping count: verified by the
  byte-identical level-5 rebuild; this is the one finding that would silently
  truncate a rebuild if missed.
- **C-995** level-5 names: verified over all 48 names sections.
- **C-996** road-shape grammar: verified by decode/encode over every level-5
  road block and every level-3/4 road record.
- **C-997** stream framing: verified by 62/62 exact streams.
- **C-998** the rebuild proof: section 1 level 5 is byte-identical, and the
  whole file `cmp` is silent. Levels 3/4 and section 2 rebuild their regions
  identically, but 1,677 level-3 records (point index), 41 level-4 records
  (names subtable) and the section-2 graph records are preserved verbatim.
- **C-999** OSM mapping: a hypothesis. The observed classes and types give a
  concrete mapping, but no regenerated coarse level has been validated in a
  car, and levels 3/4 names/point indices are not regenerated.

**UNKNOWN:** meaning of the level-5 pre-road segment; semantics of background
types `0x..08/09`, `0x..11`, `0x..26`, `0x..49`; the level-3/4 names subtable
layout; whether the unit accepts a generated level-3/4 names section; the
meaning of w21; whether level-5 aux correlates with labels.

## Conclusion

`docs/format/map-tiles.md` now documents levels 3–5 (directories, content,
record/block/name grammars, stream framing, OSM regeneration and the rebuild
proof). `docs/research/claims.md` gained C-990–C-999. The tool
`tools/lev3456.py` is in `tools/` with a synthetic self-test and the commands
above. Level 5 is the decoded-record byte-identical proof: 62/62 records and
the whole 76,066,304-byte file. Levels 3/4 are region-identical with the
unmodelled sections preserved.
