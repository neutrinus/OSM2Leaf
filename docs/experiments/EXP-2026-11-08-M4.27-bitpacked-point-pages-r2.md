## Question

Do POINT "bit-packed / non-zlib" pages (the gap M4.8b reported: pages not counted by inflated `78 9c`/`0x60|0x68` name-record parsing) carry coordinate-bearing POI position records, and can their layout be decoded so `poi_point_check` (or a new tool) covers them with counts — enabling a wider POI position sample — or do they carry no coordinate-bearing records?

## Hypothesis

Exploratory. H1: some bit-packed/non-name zlib inner pages contain coordinate-bearing POI records in a decodable layout (same or different container/grammar), enabling wider position checks. H2: the dominant bit-packed pages are non-coordinate auxiliary data (index/phone/other) and carry no coordinate-bearing POI position records. **Falsifier (H2):** finding a decodable coordinate-bearing POI position record (lat/lon/packed A/B-equivalent) in a non-`0x60|0x68` bit-packed page on the counted corpus, with a control showing it is not a name-page record.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (observed, counted):** `poi_point_check`/`iter_records` count only `0x60|0x68` name records inside zlib inner streams found by `poi_icons.iter_streams` (`78 01/9c/da/5e`; observed `789c` in the POINT002 2k-400k range). Non-`0x60|0x68` zlib inner pages and non-zlib raw regions are not counted as POI position records (precheck/gap confirmed, consistent with M4.8b).
- **VERIFIED (this corpus):** The dominant non-`0x60|0x68` zlib inner page class in US POINT002/008 is a 16-byte fixed phone-record layout (`w0=8,w1=6`) carrying ASCII phone numbers (no coordinate-bearing POI positions). In EU POINT029 the dominant decoded class is 20-byte intl phone records (`w0=10,w1=8`). A new tool covers these classes with counts and `coord_bearing=no` evidence (C-2670, C-2671).
- **OBSERVED:** `w0=1` (US) / `w0=28` (EU) index-like families show 24B/sentinel structure with `0xffff` and low ASCII but are **not fully decoded** (fixed-prefix uniformity varies; field semantics unknown). No coordinate-bearing A/B records identified in sampled pages (heuristic 0). Header bytes recorded (C-2672). `w0=2` mixed and rare unknown `w0=11–21` (`04 01…ff…` headers) are **not decoded** — stop condition applies (record header bytes/counts recorded, layout not guessed) (C-2673).
- **OBSERVED:** 72.2% of decompressed POINT002 payload is non-zlib raw/gap regions (not `78xx` inner pages). Container is not zlib and not the known `0x60|0x68` name-record grammar; header/region bytes observed (large zero-filled gaps). Coordinate-bearing content there is **unknown** and not decoded here (stop condition for "page layout" decode; scanning raw payload for unknown grammars is out of scope) (C-2674).
- **UNKNOWN:** Full semantics of w0=1/w0=28 index records, w0=2 mixed, rare unknown classes, and any coordinate-bearing records in non-zlib raw payload regions. Whether any undecoded structure maps to POI positions via a non-A/B grammar (C-2675).
- **HYPOTHESIS refuted (scoped):** "Wider POI position sample possible from decoded phone bit-packed pages" — phone classes carry no position records (H2 supported for dominant phone classes; H1 not supported on decoded classes).

## Conclusion

M4.27 is **partially met and blocked (rule 6).** Decoded: the dominant bit-packed (non-`0x60|0x68`) zlib inner POINT pages are phone/contact record pages (US 16B `w0=8,w1=6`, 1598+2956 pages across POINT002/008; EU 20B `w0=10,w1=8`, 249 pages in POINT029) carrying ASCII phone data, **not coordinate-bearing POI position records** — shown with synthetic/selftest controls, decoded samples, and heuristic coord scans (0). `tools/point_bitpack_decode.py` covers these pages with counts. Not fully met: index (`w0=1`/`w0=28`), mixed (`w0=2`), rare unknown classes, and 72.2% non-zlib raw payload regions are not fully decoded (stop condition / header bytes recorded); therefore a wider POI *position* sample from bit-packed/non-zlib pages is **not** enabled. `poi_point_check` correctly counts `0x60|0x68` name records; its gap is now characterized by class. `docs/research/claims.md` gains C-2670–C-2675.
