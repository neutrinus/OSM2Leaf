## Interpretation

- **VERIFIED (baseline):** M4.23/M4.25 `no_*` numbers reproduced exactly on the pinned corpus (C-2890). The M4.30 `deepen` shuffle matches `match()` RNG order.
- **VERIFIED (strata):** The convention is strongly class-dependent. Direction-explicit `arr->lv`/`two->lv` carry the real signal (65–82% by stratum/level) far above shuffle, while `two->two` is 23–39% and dominates misses. `two->lv` L2 reaches PASS on the full stratum but fails held-out due to test n=13; no pre-defined stratum reaches PASS on held-out n>=20 (C-2891, C-2894).
- **VERIFIED (mapping):** The 851/1317 "bearing mismatch" exclusions decompose mostly into count mismatches (492/737, OSM incident-bearing count != tile non-edge entries) rather than pure angle failure (359/580). Duplicate OSM way bearings and ambiguous margins are rare among matched `no_*` (0 dup; 32/51 margin <15°) and explain only a small fraction of matched-case uncertainty. They are not the dominant cause of the matched-miss population (C-2892).
- **VERIFIED (miss causes):** M4.25's encoding partition holds: most misses lack any 0xff-naming-F word at the node (C-2893). `two->two` misses concentrate `no_u_turn` + `osm_from_eq_to` + absent encoding — a geometry/mapping/representation class (u-turn on two-way / same OSM way identity) distinct from direction-explicit prohibition storage. `arr->lv` misses are overwhelmingly "no ff word naming F anywhere" with `T_has_ff_naming_other`, suggesting alternate encoding form/position or residual mapping, not simple reversal.
- **CONCLUSION (b), no threshold lowered:** No single pre-defined 0xff-on-T-naming-F rule reaches pre-stated PASS on a held-out subset. The convention is plausible as a *direction-explicit* rule (`arr->lv`/`two->lv`) but not proven to threshold robustness; `two->two`/u-turn/from==to requires a separate hypothesis; count-mismatch exclusions need per-case audit. Split hypotheses filed (C-2895).

## Conclusion

M4.30 reproduced M4.23/M4.25 baselines and added `rd_restrictions.py deepen` (stratified hit/shuffle with held-out, mapping diagnostics, miss clustering). The 0xff-on-T-naming-F signal is real and class-dependent (`arr->lv` 65–71%, `two->lv` up to 82% full / 77% held-out L2 but n<20) but **no pre-defined stratum reaches PASS on held-out test (n>=20)** at L1 or L2. Mapping analysis shows most bearing exclusions are count mismatches (492/737), while matched-case dup/ambiguous signals are rare; dominant misses remain `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere` (M4.25), with `two->two` driven by `no_u_turn`/`osm_from_eq_to`. Result is (b): no single rule fits at pre-stated threshold; split hypotheses filed. No format claim changed to firmware-confirmed (rule 13). Claims C-2890–C-2895 added.
