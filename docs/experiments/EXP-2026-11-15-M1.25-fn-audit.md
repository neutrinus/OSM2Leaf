# EXP-2026-11-15-M1.25: `fn_audit` — cited `fn`/`sub_` VAs vs `.pdata`

- **Status:** done
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Sample:** `archive/head256.img`, SHA-256 `92aa525d0db2c167cf4288b82f9cb951865a53c3a9ab4ead84d11a6f19a007f9`
  (256 MiB firmware head; builds A `0x200200`, B `0x3e00200`, C `0x5200200`)
- **Tools:** `python3 3.15.0`; repo `tools/fn_audit.py` (new, M1.25),
  `tools/pdata.py` (table/locate/gaps, M1.23), `tools/cerom.py` (TOC),
  `tools/selftest.py`, `tools/claims_check.py`, `tools/todo_check.py`.
  No vendor bytes are stored in the repo; `archive/head256.img` is outside
  the checkout.
- **Claims:** C-3640–C-3642 (C-3643–C-3649 reserved for M1.26)
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Superseded by:** none

## Question

Can "is every VA we cite as `fn`/`sub_` actually a function start?" be a
command that fails, and what is the exact set of mid-function citations on
the current board and format docs?

## Hypothesis

H51/H52 already proved three VAs are mid-function by hand
(`0x0041c7e8` → `fn 0x0041c7dc`, `0x00412bee` → `fn 0x00412ac0`,
`0x00542256` → `fn 0x00542250`). If those are the only ones, a mechanical
audit over `docs/format/*.md`, `TODO.md`, `targets.json` firmware entries and
`tools/*_l3.py` constants yields exactly those three unique `INSIDE` VAs and
every other citation is `START`, `GAP(leaf)` or an unmapped constant.

**Falsifier:** a fourth unique `INSIDE` VA in the scoped corpora, or any of
the three not reported. Either proves the audit corpus or the classifier
wrong (it may not silently drop a citation).

| Item | Value |

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** `archive/head256.img`, first 256 MiB, SHA-256 above.
   Builds are selected by CE image start, not by a fitted delta.
2. **Commands.** Run from the repo root:

   ```bash
   sha256sum archive/head256.img
   python3 tools/fn_audit.py --selftest
   python3 tools/fn_audit.py archive/head256.img
   python3 tools/fn_audit.py archive/head256.img --fix-list
   python3 tools/pdata.py fn archive/head256.img --module Navi.exe \
       0x0041c7e8 0x00412bee 0x00542256 0x004125a8
   python3 tools/selftest.py
   python3 tools/claims_check.py
   python3 tools/todo_check.py
   ```

3. **Controls.**
   * Positive: `0x004125a8` (`fn 0x004125a8`, START) — a citation the docs
     cite 19 times and that `pdata.locate` must accept.
   * Positive (GAP leaf): `0x003f5194` (the POINT walker) has no pdata record
     but is a direct `jsr`/`bsr` target, so the tool must say `GAP(leaf)`,
     not `DATA/UNKNOWN`.
   * Negative (synthetic, in `--selftest`): a table where a gap has no caller
     must classify `GAP(DATA/UNKNOWN)`; two builds whose records give two
     different `INSIDE` starts must classify `AMBIGUOUS` and fail. The
     selftest needs no image, so CI without vendor data still runs it.
   * Falsifier above.

4. **Scope.** 17 files: `TODO.md`, 13 `docs/format/*.md`,
   `tools/l3_harness/targets.json`, `tools/chunk_decoder_l3.py`,
   `tools/point_walk_l3.py`. 293 citations, 112 unique VAs. Classifier: for a
   VA, the modules in builds A/B/C whose pdata span contains it are
   candidates; the strongest class wins (`START` > `INSIDE` > `GAP`). A
   citation whose best class is `INSIDE`/`GAP` in two builds with different
   starts is `AMBIGUOUS` (fails when an `INSIDE` is among the candidates).
   A bare VA on a line carrying a correction marker (mid-function, "not a
   function", `→ fn`) is included as a context citation so that
   `0x00542256 → fn 0x00542250` is audited; other bare addresses are not
   (data addresses, section starts). `tools/*_l3.py` constants that fall
   outside every pdata span are `UNMAPPED` and are not errors.

5. **Falsifier.** A unique `INSIDE` outside the known three, or a known one
   missing from the report.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** On `archive/head256.img` (`92aa525d…`), 12 unique VAs cited as
  functions in `TODO.md`, `docs/format/*.md`, `targets.json` firmware entries
  or `tools/*_l3.py` constants are inside a `.pdata` function, not at its
  start; `--fix-list` names each true start. H52's three are among them
  (`0x0041c7e8` → `0x0041c7dc`, `0x00412bee` → `0x00412ac0`,
  `0x00542256` → `0x00542250`).
- **OBSERVED:** Nine further unique `INSIDE` VAs exist in the scoped corpus
  (`0x003715a0`, `0x003f5fc2`, `0x0040e4b2`, `0x00413af4`, `0x0041c802`,
  `0x004e90c8`, `0x004ea402`, `0x005423e8`, `0x0054369e`). The hypothesis
  ("exactly H52's three") is therefore **refuted**; the falsifier fired.
- **OBSERVED:** 29 `GAP` citations are all leaves (direct callers), so "no
  record" in this corpus means "leaf function", not "data".
- **UNKNOWN / caveat:** a VA can land inside different modules in different
  builds (`0x03bd2ad8` is `AppLaunch.dll` B code and `fatutil.dll` A data at
  the same address; `0x880385e4` is `nk.exe` B code). The tool picks the
  strongest class; 27 citations have such a second holder and are listed
  with `--verbose`. For those the citation context (which module the text
  names) is the human's job — this is the M1.26 refinement, not a defect.
- **UNKNOWN:** citations in append-only EXPs (`EXP-*.md`) and the rest of the
  M9/M3 task blocks are out of this scope; `--scope l3` (M1.26) extends it.

## Conclusion

`tools/fn_audit.py` makes the H52 check reproducible and failing: it exits 1
on any `INSIDE` and prints the corrected starts with `--fix-list`, using
`pdata.locate`/`pdata.gaps` rather than a second implementation of `.pdata`.
On the real head it reports 12 unique `INSIDE` VAs in the scoped corpus,
including H52's three, so the "three mid-function labels" earlier count was
an undercount; the corrected full table is above. H52's own job (editing
each citation) and the wider `--scope l3` corpus stay open (M1.26), which
inherits the 12-VA table and the multi-build ambiguity caveat.
`docs/format/` gained no claim beyond the tool's contract; the claims ledger
registers C-3640–C-3642, and C-3643–C-3649 are reserved for M1.26.
