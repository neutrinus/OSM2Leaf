## Question

Do the M2.27 readings of the type label hold on another unit: unit ID =
`1001` + serial (C-10120), and the part-number suffix letter follows the model
letter (C-10121)?

## Hypothesis

Both hold. Falsifier for C-10120: a unit ID that is not `1001` followed by
the 8-digit serial. Falsifier for C-10121: a unit whose `N #` suffix ends in a
different letter than its model.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED (C-10140):** C-10120 holds on a second unit: the unit ID is
  `1001` + the 8-digit serial (2 of 2 labels).
- **OBSERVED (C-10141):** the `N #` suffix letter does **not** follow the
  model letter (`QY-7200N-G` ↔ `3NA1A`). C-10121's letter rule is refuted;
  what stays is that PROD `+0x0B0` holds the 5-character suffix of the
  Nissan part number `25915-xxxxx`. C-10121 is RETRACTED.
- **OBSERVED (C-10142):** a third unit sample: `QY-7200N-G`, part
  `25915-3NA1A`, manufactured May 2012, serial `0019744`, mark `ZA`, with a
  US/Canada map card, so a North American unit.
- **OBSERVED (C-10143):** across the two labels the Bluetooth module
  (`BTU-3588`, QD ID `B014872`) and FCC ID (`SJ2QY7200`) are unchanged
  between the March 2011 and May 2012 builds; the label part number moves
  from `276-0925-00` to `-06`.
- **HYPOTHESIS (C-10144):** serials are one counter for the `QY-7200N`
  family (0004058 in March 2011, 0019744 in May 2012, about 15.7 k units in
  14 months). Two points cannot tell a shared counter from per-model ones.
- **UNKNOWN:** meaning of the large mark (`Z7`, `ZA`) and of the model
  letters (`B`, `D`, `G`); still no PIN, so C-8304/C-8334 are unchanged.

## Conclusion

`docs/hardware/head-unit.md` shows both labels side by side; the
`+0x0B0` row of `unit-flash.md` now cites C-10141. The unit-ID rule
(C-10120) is confirmed on a second unit; the part-number letter rule
(C-10121) is retracted. The PIN question needs (serial, PIN) pairs as before.
