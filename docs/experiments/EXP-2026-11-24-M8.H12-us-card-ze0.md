## Question

Can the US 2011 card be brought to the same L4 launcher stage as the EU 2011 card on `-M ze0`, and which constants in the recipe are build-specific?

## Hypothesis

The EU recipe, with the US card's own `verinf`/`mapdb`/`ipl-patch`, reaches at least the launcher stage `Finish Launch Threads`. **Falsifier from the task:** the EU recipe works unchanged on the US card with EU constants, in which case nothing is build-specific. **Additional falsifier:** a wrong `verinf` does not change the launcher verdict (the check would not have teeth).

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the US 2011 card is accepted and mounted on `-M ze0`; the launcher passes both map-code and map-DB checks with the *card-derived* `QSD-301-100`/`16-16`, and no FailSafe or `Error Proc` is printed (O1, O4, O5, O7; C-7090, C-7091).
- **FACT:** the recipe is build-specific — verinf `QSD-301-100` vs EU `QSD-302-100`; image A 151 vs 149 modules; `Navi.exe` 5,858,816 vs 5,714,432 B and 5,257,126 differing code bytes; the US launcher prints `Launcher End !` but never the EU's `Finish Launch Threads` (O1, O2, O4–O6). The task's falsifier is not met (C-7092, C-7093).
- **FACT (first US-only blocker):** 17 `ACM::WaitModule Timeout` lines are consumed, then `CAuiMonitorCmn::ThreadProc::TIMEOUT!!!!!` and `Pow >> No Action at ACC-OFF? Timeout!! Force PowerOff!!!` are logged; `FinishThread Error TimeOut` (EU: 10) and `Finish Launch Threads` (EU: 1) are both 0 even at 900 s (O5, O6). This is the named US-only divergence, recorded rather than debugged per the Stop rule (C-7094, C-7095).
- **OBSERVED:** `launch_not_create=16` on US vs 0 on the EU patched run P (C1d O7) and 8 in this EU factory log; the 16 lines are relaunch attempts after `WaitModule Timeout`, not a card rejection (O4, O5, O6; C-7096).
- **OBSERVED:** VRAM is still zero and no map is rendered within 900 s; this task does not debug `Navi.exe` (O5; C-7097).
- **UNKNOWN:** which US launcher thread omits the `FinishThread Error TimeOut` wait and why `CAuiMonitorCmn` times out; left for a follow-up (C-7098).

## Conclusion

`python3 tools/fetch_image.py --image US256 -o archive/us256.img` verifies the US head and `tools/ze0_card.py describe` derives `verinf=QSD-301-100, mapdb=16-16`. With the US IPL patch (81/91 kernel and 113/151 image-A modules) and the derived `dbgmsg=0x880404f4`, the US 2011 card boots on `-M ze0` to `Launcher End !` with the launcher accepting the card (`applch_error=0`, no FailSafe, no `Error Proc`); a wrong verinf demonstrably flips those checks to 1. However the US build never reaches `Finish Launch Threads`: at 900 s its first US-only blocker is `CAuiMonitorCmn::ThreadProc::TIMEOUT!!!!!` followed by `Pow >> No Action at ACC-OFF? Timeout!! Force PowerOff!!!`, while the EU factory log prints `FinishThread Error TimeOut` 10× and `Finish Launch Threads` once. Both READMEs now carry a US row, and claims C-7090–C-7099 record the build-specific constants and the blocker. The Done-when's blocker branch is therefore met; the task is `done`, not `blocked` (naming the blocker *is* its Done-when), with the follow-up recorded as C-7098.
