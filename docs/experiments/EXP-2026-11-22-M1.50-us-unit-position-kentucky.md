## Question

Marek's US 2011 unit (map card QSD-301-103) is in Poland. The map screen shows
the car "somewhere in the US", and OpenCARWINGS reports 37.4058805556,
−84.4876444444 (between Somerset and Richmond, Kentucky), *At Home: No*, last
updated 2026-11-22 11:15. Is it the GPS receiver (region lock), or does the
WinCE side hold or replace the position? Four sub-questions: (1) where the
position comes from; (2) is the coordinate a constant in the firmware;
(3) who sends the position to OpenCARWINGS; (4) relation to the area code
(M2.7) and the SD lock.

## Hypothesis

The working hypothesis at the time of the measurements was H1 (the receiver
has no current fix and the unit shows its last stored position); H2 (a valid
fix is rejected by the navigation) was not excluded. **Falsifier:** the
coordinate is a factory constant in the firmware (H1 would be wrong), the
receiver is region-locked, or the receiver has no fix while the car is outside
the US — any of these would change the verdict. The receiver rollover and the
stored Kentucky positions were then measured (U1–U3), falsifying H1/H3 and
leaving H2 (cause H2a/H2b/H2c not separated). M1.56 changes only the
*reproducibility* of those measurements, not this verdict.

### 3. Controls

- **Positive (`vflash`).** The `--selftest` plants a little-endian int32 pair
  (37.4058805556, −84.4876444444 → raw `68946519, -155727626`, bytes
  `570a1c04 f6c8b7f6`) plus a date `0x07d7-04-04 15:34` at the derived offset
  `pair+8` into a 1024-byte synthetic dump. The printed output finds the pair
  at offset 64, the bounding box containing the point, and the year `07d7`
  (2007, not 2026). The offset is quoted from `position_dump_scan.py
  --selftest` (`planted pair offset: got 64 want 64`).
- **Positive (`rom`).** The same selftest plants a coordinate constant in a
  synthetic blob in both byte orders (`deg*1e6 latlon little` at 128,
  `deg*1e7 lonlat big` at 256) and as float64 little-endian at 512. The tool
  prints `rom exact hits >= 3`, and names each matched form. A planted
  LE 1/512 pair is found in the tolerance pass at offset 128.
- **Negative (`vflash`).** The `--selftest` swaps lat/lon (pair outside the
  US box) → `0` pairs; shifts the date to the alternative `pair+4` offset →
  no date span; plants a wrong year `0x07ea` → no `07d7-04-04`; empty dump →
  `0` pairs. The date offset is derived from the §U3 record description, not
  fitted to the selftest; the `pair+4` negative control proves this.
- **Negative (`rom`).** A byte-swapped plant loses its LE form; an empty blob
  gives `exact_hits=0, tolerance_pairs=0`. The task falsifier — `0x07d7`
  printed as 2026 — is checked by `0x07d7 is 2007: got '07d7' want '07d7'`.

### 4. Scope

The committed `rom` scan covers the raw bytes of two 256 MiB image heads
(US `70d945d8…a04da`, EU `92aa525d…007f9`; 115 encoding forms each) plus the
synthetic fixtures. It **does not** include the decompressed sections of the
six ROMs that §F8 also searched, so its `exact_hits=0` / `tolerance_pairs=0`
are narrower than §F8's full-scope results (exact 0, tolerance 5). The
tolerance pass is deliberately box-free (the original §F8 found mixed-sign
pairs outside the US box), but a raw-head-only pass finds no tolerance pair
here; §F8's "5 pairs" is therefore marked `measured on the original host, not
reproduced` and the EXP does not claim the committed tool reproduces it. The `vflash` `1600` pairs / `4` copies /
`15 m` / `2007-04-04` are *measured on the unit host* (M6.18 dump, not
reproduced here); the committed tool replays only a synthetic pair and a
synthetic date. Excluded: any new unit measurement, any vendor dump in the
repo. The tool is static/offline and stdlib-only; no vendor bytes are
committed. Scratch: `~/projekty/leafre-work/scratch/m1.56/`.

### 5. Falsifier

The planted pair found at a wrong offset, the `pair+8` date read as 2026, a
planted positive control not found by `rom`, or a negative control passing
would refute the tool and leave the C-8245/C-8249 evidence unreproducible —
the task would be `blocked`, not `done`. The M1.50 verdict itself is falsified
only by the unit measurements in U1–U3 being wrong.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** F1–F9 are code/string facts (commands in Method 2). The committed

  heads over `115` encoding forms. Its `tolerance_pairs=0` is a scope limit,
  not a refutation of F8's host-measured "5 pairs" (§4 Scope).
- **OBSERVED:** U1–U3 (unit photos and the M6.18 VFlash dump). The M6.18 dump's
  VFlash pair count/date span is measured on the unit host, not reproduced on
  this host; the committed `vflash` replay shows the same rule on a synthetic
  pair only (`0` pairs on the anon zeros file).
- **HYPOTHESIS:** H2a/H2b/H2c (date / distance / map) are not separated.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the real-dump `1600` count and `4` copies reprint
  identically on another host: the M6.18 dump is user data and not committed.
  The `pair+8` date offset is derived from §U3's record description, not from

### Revised verdict

- **H1 (no fix, held position) is falsified** by U1: the receiver works and
  knows it is in Wrocław.
- **H3 (receiver region lock) is falsified** by U1.
- **The block is in the WinCE navigation (`Navi.exe` car-position logic), not
  in the receiver**: the raw fix is right (U1), the navigation's car position
  (`OBJ_CarPos`, which `Vehicle.exe` sends to the TCU → OpenCARWINGS, F1) stays
  in Kentucky (U3). This is H2, now the main hypothesis. Candidate reasons, not
  yet separated:
  - **H2a — date:** fixes are timestamped 2007, older than the stored
    position/history (2011–2019 dates); a "fix older than the last known state"
    check would drop every fix. The rollover is a fact (U2); its effect on
    `Navi.exe` is a hypothesis.
  - **H2b — distance:** a fix 8 000 km from the dead-reckoned position is
    treated as a GPS error (jump filter), forever.
  - **H2c — map:** the US card has no data at Wrocław, so map matching cannot
    place the car and keeps the last matched position.
- The rollover also explains a wrong date in trip/probe records and in
  anything that uses GPS time.
