## Question

Can a section 2 written only from OSM (nodes, links, one-way, class, length,
cost, tile-edge links) make the firmware route, and draw the route, on a
from-zero London card?

## Hypothesis

With the M4.98 field meanings and the factory's own statistics for the words
not named (type nibble, `w1`, `0x10`), the firmware routes on our graph.
Falsifier: New Destination gives `CALC_ERR_NBR` / `CALC_ERR_OTHER` on our card
for a destination that routes on the factory card.

## Method

1. Build the tile (London IMF and areas as in the from-zero recipe of M5.88 /
   M5.89):

   ```
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py split.jsonl --template B48R068R.DAT --cell B48R068R \
     --class-table class.json --country 826 --areas areas.jsonl --urban-fill 0.1 \
     --places --level2 --sec2 --max-plain 80000 --json -o s6.DAT
   python3 tools/sec2_fields.py s6.DAT                      # structure checks
   python3 tools/sec2_fields.py s6.DAT --s1-direction --limit 40
   python3 tools/sec2_fields.py B48R068R.DAT --census       # factory rules
   python3 tools/sec2_fields.py s6.DAT --census
   python3 tools/sec2_fields.py B48R068R.DAT --sense s6.DAT --limit 300
   ```

   `--max-plain 80000` is needed: above it a dense central parcel's
   section-1 or section-2 record passes 0x10000 words (`GraphError`).
   24 parcels are trimmed (14,592 runs dropped, lowest class first).
2. Card: `imgpatch` the tile into the EU 2011 image, `card_mapcode set
   --from-nk1` (unit flash), boot NK1 with `ze0_launch --ui-start`; the M9.75
   route recipe (consent taps, drag 300,240→100,300, Map Menu, New
   Destination). Verdict from the serial log.
3. Controls. Positive: the factory card routes the same destination
   (`DISPLAY_ROUTE`, C-9939). Negative: our card with one-ways before the
   trap rule fails (below); freeing the one-way links in a 150-unit radius
   around the destination only (section 2 only) makes it route.
4. Scope: one London tile (602 level-1 section-2 records, 559,523 nodes,
   1,228,254 link ends); one start (car position `a6a0cde3` 1395,980) and one
   destination (parcel `a6ac cdef` about 1578,1431). Level-2 section 2 is not
   written. Long routes (step 5) use the factory card only.
5. Long routes on the factory card: zoom out ten steps (tap 765,200), drag,
   Map Menu, New Destination; destination mesh from `(start route
   pos)[SIDED]`.

## Interpretation

- The writer's structure is right: our graph routes as soon as no one-way
  trap sits between start and destination, and the route is drawn on our
  section-1 links, so the (class, id) join holds.
- The failure was graph connectivity, not field encoding: the `0x10` bit
  and the class-8 rule did not change it, freeing only the destination area
  did. The factory has almost no one-way dead ends; the London OSM build had
  1,165, mostly where trimming dropped a way.
- The search is hierarchical: beyond a few miles it starts at level 4 and
  descends at the destination side through level 2 to level 1. The origin
  side is pre-searched separately (`PRESRCH OSIDE`). A from-zero card that
  should route beyond the tile needs section 2 at levels 2 and 4 (and the
  level-2 → level-1 map of C-9938).

## Conclusion

A section 2 written from OSM routes and draws in L4 on the London card once
one-way traps are removed. Next: level-2 and level-4 section 2 (and unit 1)
from OSM, so that routes longer than the local search work; then the
factory re-emit of M5.57 done-when 1.
