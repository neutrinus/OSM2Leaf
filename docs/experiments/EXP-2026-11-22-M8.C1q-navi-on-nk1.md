## Question

On NK1 + A (M8.C1r) `CreateProcessW` returns 0 for Navi.exe and Telema.exe
with LastError 14 (ERROR_OUTOFMEMORY), on both cards, while kernel B starts
Navi.exe. Which kernel step fails, and why?

## Hypothesis

The loader fails while allocating the big `.data` sections (Navi.exe
16.7 MB, Telema.exe 6.6 MB; the other processes have small ones).
**Falsifier:** the error comes from a step that does not depend on size, or
memory is plentiful at the moment of the failure.

## Method

All runs: `-M ze0,dbgmsg=0x880404c4,nk=NK1,ipl-patch=IPL,flash=NAND`, the
`ze0_ipl.py --nk` patch, the US card unless noted. Serial logs are not kept
(NK1 prints the card password).

| Run | What | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | breakpoint on AppLaunch's `CreateProcessW` call (`0x03e4bef4`, name in r4); for Navi/Telema only, breakpoints on all 57 `mov #14,Rn` in nk.exe | gdbstub script (now `ze0_gdb.py break`) |
| Q2 | breakpoints in the section loader around the one hit | same |
| Q4 | single-step through the failing `VirtualAlloc` for Telema.exe's `.data` | same |
| Q5, Q6 | page-free count (KData `+0x310`, phys `0x09e41b10`) every 3 s / 0.2 s next to the serial line count | HMP `xp` (`ze0_gdb.py xp`) |

## Observations

O1. **Where the 14 comes from (Q1, Q2).** For both Navi.exe and Telema.exe
exactly one `mov #14` site is hit between the call and the return: the error
exit of nk.exe's section loader (the function at `0x88026b80`, exit at
`0x88026c26`). The first section (code, mapped from ROM through
`0x88020164`) succeeds; the second, the compressed `.data`, fails at the
`VirtualAlloc(realaddr, vsize, MEM_COMMIT, PAGE_EXECUTE_READWRITE)` call at
`0x88026c90` (Telema: size `0x6a4fe1`).

O2. **Why VirtualAlloc fails (Q4).** The commit path reaches the page
reservation (`0x8802c344`), which compares the request with the kernel's
page-free count at KData `+0x310`. At that moment the count is **795 pages**
(3.1 MB) while Telema.exe needs 1,701 and Navi.exe 4,082.

O3. **Memory appears later (Q5, Q6).** Free pages over the boot, sampled
next to the serial log: 1,439 while NK1 loads image A, 544 right after the
launcher prints `Process Create!!! -> \windows\Navi.exe`, then 40,402 a
few hundred log lines later, settling at ~28,300 when everything runs. The
launcher's failures (`Launch Process Not Create`) come right after the
creates, inside the low window.

O4. **Who adds it (Q8).** After the Navi.exe create, the kernel adds runs of
8,192 pages (32 MB) from the secondary bank (`0x90008000`–`0x98000000`),
then 7,183 and 1,026 pages of the primary bank, all from the kernel routine
at `0x8804a714`. That routine takes pages from a deferred list
(`0x89e6489c`), clears them and frees them. It is reached through
`KernelIoControl` codes in nk.exe's OEM table:

| Code | Handler | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x01013060` | `0x8804a360` | number of deferred pages (minus `0x402`) |
| `0x0101305C` | `0x8804a338` | clear and free N deferred pages |
| `0x01013050` | `0x8804a3d4` | with input 0: clear and free all |

O5. **Why RAM is deferred.** At memory init (`0x8804a878`) NK1 moves the
kernel's whole free list onto the deferred list and asks a boot-type test
(`0x88048a90`: the low nibble of the boot type at `0x89e43c8c` is 0). If the
test is true it keeps everything deferred; else it frees all but `0x402`
pages at once. On `-M ze0` the boot type is 0, so all RAM is deferred.

O6. **Who releases it.** NK1's AppLaunch.dll has `MemClearThread` (its
exception message is `AppLch Driver MemClearThread Exception!!`). At
priority 90 it loops: `Sleep(5)`, query `0x01013060`, release 8,192 pages
with `0x0101305C`, until none are left; then it releases the rest with
`0x01013050`. In parallel the launch thread loads image A, prints `Sum Check
NK2` and creates the processes. On `-M ze0` the creates win the race.

O7. **The fix (Q9, USn, EUn).** `ze0_ipl.py patch --nk` now adds one line:
the `jsr` to the boot-type test in the memory-init hook (`0x8804a88a`,
phys `0x0804a88a`) becomes `mov #0,r0`, so the hook takes the "free now"
branch. The other caller of the test (`0x88049d9e`) is left alone. With it
the page-free count is 41,390 from the start; Navi.exe and Telema.exe are
created (no `Not Create` line); the screen-state log shows
`NAVIMAP_CC_NTF_OWNFIRSTMAPDRAW_END`; the screen shows the map:

| Run | Card | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USn | US 2011 | Los Angeles, LAX area (`AIRPORT BLVD`, `W CENTURY BLVD`) |
| EUn | EU 2011, N2 overlay | London (`PICCADILLY CIRCUS`, `SOHO`) |

The screenshots are vendor map data and stay outside the repo.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis holds in part. The loader does fail while committing the big
`.data`, but the reason is not the size limit of a slot or the loader. All
RAM is parked on a deferred list at that moment. Small `.data` sections fit
in the few hundred pages left; 6.6 MB and 16.7 MB do not.

On the real unit the same race exists. It is most likely hidden by timing:
the module waits before the Navi/Telema creates (`Wait modules before
Process Create`) and slower SD reads give `MemClearThread` time. The boot
type may also be non-zero there. This is a hypothesis (C-8415): nothing here
measures the unit. The fix belongs in the emulator setup (rule: no card
changes), not on the card.

## Conclusion

Navi.exe runs on NK1 + image A in QEMU, with Marek's unit flash, on both the
US card and the EU card prepared with `card_mapcode.py`. The one extra IPL
line is generated by `ze0_ipl.py --nk` (off with `--keep-deferred-ram`).
