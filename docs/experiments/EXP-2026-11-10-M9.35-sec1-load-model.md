## Question

Can a guarded module load model (CE-loader-like for compressed sections: zero BSS, bound IAT trampolines, `INIT` read guard) let the M9.17/M9.14 Navi.exe module probes get past their prior compressed-sec1 UNMAPPED faults at `0x0055d040`/`0x0055d8fc`, while failing loudly and specifically on unbound imports and `INIT` bytes?

## Hypothesis

H49 (C-3440–C-3445): cited `0x0055dxxx` blockers are import slots/BSS and only 5.5% of build-A compressed bytes are stored (`INIT`). Mapping compressed sections as full-vsize zero-filled blocks with `init_len` from `cerom comphdr`, binding 846 IAT slots to named stub trampolines, and guarding non-IAT `INIT` reads should move probes past `0x0055d040`/`0x0055d8fc` to the next real stop (stub name or other fault/class), without fabricating `INIT` bytes. **Falsifier:** any synthetic case where BSS read is non-zero, bound IAT call does not reach named stub, unbound import does not stop as `BLOCKED STUB <DLL!name>`, or non-IAT `INIT` read is not `BLOCKED INIT ... (M1.2f)`; or IMG256 probes still stop at `0x0055d040`/`0x0055d8fc` as UNMAPPED-in-compressed-sec1.
