## Question

Can a field of the `LCTBL001` record (the road-number records that are the
same 12-column layout as the TMC record, C-882) be tied to the road class of
numbered roads whose class is known from OSM, with at least five roads of
different classes and a control?

## Hypothesis

Probe, with a falsifier. Hypothesis: one record field (class code `u16` at
`+14`, record size, slot lengths, the seven attribute bytes, footer length or
its leading bytes) predicts the OSM `highway` class of the road named in slot 1
better than a shuffled-label control. Falsifier: a field whose
leave-one-road-out (LORO) accuracy does not exceed the shuffled-road-label
null, or whose in-sample agreement is explained by the road identity alone (a
per-road value), is not a road-class field. A positive control must pass: a
field that equals the class must score 1.000 LORO on synthetic data.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** The two `LCTBL001.DAT` files and the two OSM files
   above, hashed before use (`sha256sum`). The IMF is the M5.1b output of
   `tools/osm_ingest.py`, whose `imf_check` result is C-910.
2. **Commands.**

   ```bash
   python3 tools/road_class_probe.py --selftest
   python3 tools/road_class_probe.py \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m422/eu2011/RDSTM001/LCTBL001.DAT \
     --imf /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/osm/dolnoslaskie.imf.jsonl --perms 200
   python3 tools/road_class_probe.py \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m422/eu2014/RDSTM001/LCTBL001.DAT \
     --imf /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/osm/dolnoslaskie.imf.jsonl --perms 200
   python3 tools/lctbl_records.py verify /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m422/eu2011/RDSTM001/LCTBL001.DAT
   ```

   The probe (a) joins each numbered record to OSM ways whose normalised `ref`
   (spaces and hyphens removed) equals the road number, and keeps refs whose
   ways agree on one `highway` value for at least 90 % of the ways; (b) scores
   each candidate field by in-sample majority vote and by LORO (a record is
   predicted from the other roads that share its field value, one vote per
   road); (c) computes the in-sample null and the LORO null by shuffling the
   labels (200 permutations, seed 42).
3. **Controls.** *Positive (selftest):* a synthetic `class_code` equal to the
   class gives LORO 1.000 and beats its shuffled null by more than 3 sd.
   *Confound (selftest):* a per-road footer value has no LORO votes (it names
   the road; it cannot generalise). *Negative (selftest):* a random attribute
   byte gives LORO < 0.6 on 90 synthetic rows. *Negative (real, unnumbered
   streets):* 207,722 records with a primary name and no road number are
   counted, and their `class_code` histogram is compared with the joined
   road rows (Observations, table C).
4. **Scope.** Whole EU 2011 and EU 2014 LCTBL streams (all 517 and 329,296
   records). The OSM side is the Dolnośląskie extract only. Excluded: records
   whose road number matches no OSM ref (EU 2011: 36,461 − 420 = 36,041
   numbered records are unjoined); RDSTM rows (no decoded road key, C-884); the
   footer beyond its first two bytes (opaque). Note that the road numbers are
   EU-wide and the records carry no country, so a number can match a
   same-numbered road in another country (C-1827).
5. **Falsifier.** Stated above: a field counts as a road-class candidate only if
   its LORO accuracy exceeds the LORO shuffled-road-label null by at least
   3 sd, and its in-sample agreement is not a per-road identifier. Otherwise the
   result is negative for that field.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (tool, selftest):** the probe returns LORO 1.000 for a field equal to
  the class and does not reward a per-road identifier, so it can separate a
  real class field from a road-identity field.
- **OBSERVED (Table A):** for every candidate field, the LORO accuracy on the
  real data is at or below its shuffled-label null (LORO 0.245–0.481 against
  0.46–0.56 nulls). No single candidate field gives a road-class prediction
  from other roads. The in-sample gains (`class_code` 0.500 vs 0.316,
  `footer[:2]` 0.845 vs 0.580) do not survive LORO: `footer[:2]` takes 161
  distinct values over 420 rows, so it mostly names the road.
- **OBSERVED (Table B):** the most common joined value `0x283c` holds all seven
  OSM classes (it is the default for numbered roads, not a class). Four
  codes are class-pure or nearly pure: `0x2775` is motorway-only in both years
  (23/23 and 30/31 rows, roads A8, A4, A18, D11, S3); `0x4e8e`, `0x4e91` are
  motorway-only in 2014 (10/10, 11/11); `0x2777` is secondary-dominated
  (74 of 94 in 2011, 75 of 103 in 2014). These pure codes cover a small share
  of the joined rows (23 of 118 motorway rows in 2011).
- **OBSERVED (Table C):** `0x283c` is almost absent from unnumbered streets
  (431 of 207,722) while it is 53.6 % of the joined road rows. The `class_code`
  histogram therefore separates numbered roads from streets; it does not, by
  itself, separate classes.
- **OBSERVED (Table A2):** the EU 2014 replication does not confirm Table A. The
  in-sample `class_code` agreement (0.606 vs 0.472) recurs, but its LORO is
  0.554 against a null of 0.550, so the field does not generalise across roads.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** `class_code` (u16 at `+14`) may hold a partial road-class
  code: some values are class-pure in both years (motorway: `0x2775`, `0x4e8e`,
  `0x4e91`), with a default code for mixed numbered roads (`0x283c`). This is
  not established, because LORO fails in both years over all joined rows. The
  pure codes are small, and the control is a single numbering sample. A test
  with a second country and a non-motorway control set is needed before this is
  a claim about classes.
- **UNKNOWN:** the country of each record; whether the pure codes are a
  class or a page/region code that happens to hold motorways (the 2014 replication
  shows the same codes for the same roads, which fits either reading); the footer
  bytes beyond the first two; and the RDSTM rows.
- **Consequence for M4.2:** this gives a road key for the `LCTBL` records
  (road number ↔ record), not for the `RDSTM001` rows. The RDSTM gate of
  EXP-2026-11-08-M4.2 (C-901) is unchanged: no decoded `RDSTM` row carries a
  road number, because the per-record directory (C-884) is undecoded.

## Conclusion

M4.17's Done-when is met: the EXP table maps 96 numbered roads of 7 OSM classes
(420 record rows) to the record fields, with an unnumbered-street control
(207,722 rows), in two EU samples (Tables A, A2). The road-class field is
**not established**. In both years no candidate field beats its shuffled null
under leave-one-road-out. `class_code` shows class-pure codes (Table B) that
recur in EU 2014, but its LORO does not exceed the null, so it is a hypothesis
for a follow-up with a second country. The `LCTBL001` road-number key does not
by itself unblock M4.2, which needs an RDSTM row key (C-884); M4.2 is set back
to `open` per rule 15 and will likely re-block on the RDSTM key. Claims
C-1820–C-1828 record this; `docs/format/` is unchanged.
