## Question

Does the +2.8 km median latitude residual observed by M4.8b (C-1903, C-1904;
16 POIs, all `dlat > 0`, median +2763 m) track a POI's public entrance, its
building/feature centroid, or neither — tested on POIs whose true entrance and
centroid differ by a known, checkable amount from allowed (non-OSM) sources?

## Hypothesis

Exploratory. H1 (entrance): decoded POI positions are stored at street/entrance
points, so residuals vs centroid/Wikipedia references shrink when measured
against the true entrance. H2 (centroid): residuals track the centroid/reference
point, implying a transform or storage bias. H3 (neither): residuals do not
consistently track either. Falsifier for H1: ≥ 3 POIs with a known, checkable
entrance-vs-centroid separation show decoded positions no closer to the cited
entrance than to the cited centroid/reference (or the opposite of the observed
residual direction). Falsifier for H2: decoded positions are systematically
closer to cited entrances than to centroids on those POIs.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED (dep):** M4.27's wider (bit-packed) POI sample was unavailable in
  this session (C-2680). M4.8d is done and rules out a 0..2047-class in-record
  sub-unit position field in zlib-framed POINT/ADDRE (C-2934).
- **OBSERVED (precheck/Stop):** The precheck — ≥ 3 POIs with a known, checkable
  entrance-vs-centroid difference cited from allowed non-OSM sources — could not
  be satisfied on the 16 M4.8b POIs (C-2681). Therefore the Done-when test
  (residual tracks entrance, centroid, or neither, with evidence) cannot be
  completed honestly in this session.
- **OBSERVED (residual pattern, refines C-1903/C-1904):** The latitude residual
  is northward for all 16 POIs but varies by 48× in magnitude (132–6401 m). This
  is inconsistent with a single fixed latitude transform/storage offset as the
  sole cause, and is consistent with (but does not prove) per-POI stored-point
  semantics (entrance/street/access point vs centroid) and/or grid quantization
  interacting with feature placement (C-2682). It does **not** by itself
  distinguish entrance from centroid because no entrance coordinate is cited.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether decoded POI positions track public entrances, centroids,
  or neither remains **unresolved** (C-1904's interpretation not confirmed,
  narrowed, or retracted). A decisive test requires either (a) POIs with
  citable entrance and centroid coordinates from allowed sources, or (b) a
  wider sample including bit-packed pages (M4.27) that yields such POIs, or
  (c) an independent method (e.g. ADDRE street-point comparison, or firmware
  evidence) outside this task's precheck form.
- **HYPOTHESIS (carried, not resolved):** POI points may be stored as access/
  entrance/street points rather than centroids (M4.8b). The directional and
  magnitude variance is compatible with this, but evidence is insufficient.

## Conclusion

M4.28 attempted the entrance-vs-centroid test on M4.8b's 16 POIs using M4.8d's
completed findings and without fabricating M4.27 results (M4.27 unavailable).
The precheck could not be met: no POI with a reliably known, checkable
entrance-vs-centroid coordinate separation from allowed non-OSM sources was
identified (airports share the ARP reference; buildings differ by far less than
the km-scale residual). The Stop condition is triggered and the task is
**blocked** (rule 6). Analysis of the existing residuals narrows C-1903: the
latitude residual is universally northward but not a single constant offset
(48× magnitude spread), arguing against a pure fixed-lat transform error as the
sole cause while leaving entrance-vs-centroid unresolved. C-1904 is **not**
confirmed, narrowed to a verdict, or retracted for lack of the required
entrance evidence. Claims C-2680–C-2682 recorded; C-2683–C-2689 reserved.
