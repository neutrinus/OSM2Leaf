## Interpretation

- **VERIFIED (precheck/parity):** M4.30 count-mismatch diagnostics reproduced (L1 492, L2 737) and M4.34 enumerates the same `osm!=tile2` population (C-3000).
- **OBSERVED (dominant cause):** Most count mismatches are `osm_more` (361/492 L1, 622/737 L2): OSM reports more incident bearings than the tile has non-edge in-record entries. In all `osm_more` cases the extras include non-from/to bearings (`extra_non_from_to_bearings` 361/622). This is consistent with OSM/IMF having additional incident ways at the junction that are absent or represented differently in the tile's routing graph at that level, not with a single pass-through overcount. Pass-through way bearings (`has_pass_through_way_bearings`) are rare (7/3) (C-3001).
- **OBSERVED (not tile far-node filtering within this scope):** "Tile far-node-in-other-record filtering" does **not** explain these 492/737 cases by construction: rd_restrictions excludes far-node-not-in-record entries as `tile bearing missing (far node in another record)` (`len(tb)!=len(nonedge)`) before the `osm!=tile2` count-mismatch branch. The 492/737 all have `len(tb)==len(nonedge)`. Cross-record/far-node filtering may exist as a separate exclusion bucket, but it is not the mechanism for the count-mismatch class analyzed here (C-3002).
- **OBSERVED (junction mismatch indicator, weak):** `junction_dist_gt4` (of tol=8, 2048-units) occurs in 174/492 (35.4%) L1 and 205/737 (27.8%) L2. This is a possible indicator of imperfect junction alignment but is not sufficient to call "genuine junction mismatch" without per-case geometry; many count mismatches occur at close junctions (`<=4`: 318/532) (C-3003).
- **OBSERVED (recoverability, no outcome fitting):** A geometric subset rule that maps only the restriction's `from`/`to` bearings to distinct tile entries within 25° (ignoring extra/deficit incident bearings) is feasible for 161/492 L1 (32.7%) and 173/737 L2 (23.5%) of count-mismatch exclusions (96/361 and 143/622 of `osm_more`; 65/131 and 30/115 of `osm_fewer`). These are *potential* additional bearing-mapped cases for from/to only; adopting such a rule changes mapping semantics and was **not** validated against restriction outcomes (outcome fitting forbidden). Non-recoverable cases are dominated by no feasible from+to geometric assignment (329/563) (C-3004).
- **UNKNOWN:** Per-case attribution between "OSM has minor/service ways not in routing graph", "tile generalization omits entries", and "genuine different junction/representation" requires manual/automated geometry audit beyond this classification. Firmware-side behavior for count-mismatched junctions unknown (rule 13).

## Conclusion

M4.34 reproduced M4.30's count-mismatch diagnostics (L1 492, L2 737 of 851/1317 bearing mismatches) and classified the `osm!=tile2` cases without outcome fitting (`tools/m434_bearing_mismatch.py`, 11 selftest checks). Most are `osm_more` (361/492, 622/737) with extra non-from/to bearings; pass-through way overcounts are rare (7/3). Tile far-node-in-other-record filtering is not the cause of this class by construction (separate exclusion branch). Junction-distance>4 is present but not a decisive mismatch proof (174/205). A geometric from/to subset mapping could recover 161 L1 + 173 L2 count-mismatch cases (32.7%/23.5%) as bearing-mapped for from/to, but this requires a deliberate mapping-rule change and outcome-independent validation before use. Claims C-3000–C-3005 added.
