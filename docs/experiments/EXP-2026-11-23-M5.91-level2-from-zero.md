## Question

Zooming out leaves level 1. Can the from-zero tile carry level-2 records
built from OSM, and does the firmware's map client read them?

## Hypothesis

`tilewriter.build_road_record(2, …)` and `clarion_bg.build_record` with the
level-2 header (w3/w4 `0xffff`) give records the map client reads like the
factory's. Falsifier: a level-2 request on our card gets reply sub 9, reads
no record, or reads bytes that are not our record.

## Inputs

- Factory tile.
- Our Z5b tile inputs.
- Factory level-2 census of `B48R068R`, done with `s1_wide_dir` on section 1
  level 2:
  - Background: layer 0 `0x502b` urban, layer 1 `0x5012` sea, line types
    `0x5001`/`0x500a`. There are no park or water polygons at level 2.
    **Corrected by M5.100 O3:** that census was incomplete. Level 2 has the
    level-1 scheme with `0x50xx` types, parks and water included.
  - Roads: layer 6 classes 4–7 and layer 7 classes 1/2.
  - Empty level-2 pairs are `0xffffffff` for even and odd.

## Method

1. Sample and hash: above.
2. Build. Level 2 differs from level 1 as follows:
   - **Highways:** only motorway to tertiary, with their links.
   - **Classes:** the `fa_encode` level-2 class: motorway 1, trunk 3,
     primary 4, secondary 5, tertiary 7.
   - **Layers:** layer 7 for classes 1/2, else layer 6.
   - **Geometry:** Douglas-Peucker simplification at 10 m.
   - **Background:** urban only (`0x502b`, `--urban-fill 0.1`).
   - **Labels:** road names go into the odd record. There are no place points.

   ```bash
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py --selftest
   python3 tools/tile_from_zero.py $W/z3/split.jsonl --template $FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT --cell B48R068R \
       --class-table $W/z3/class.json --country 826 --areas $W/z5/areas.jsonl --urban-fill 0.1 --places \
       --level2 -o $W/z6/l2.DAT
   python3 tools/tile_provenance.py $FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT $W/z6/l2.DAT --require-zero
   ```

3. Gates:
   - **L1:** `cardcheck`.
   - **L3:** `datask_l3.py mount archive/head256.img --card-image CARD
     --position 51.5098,-0.1342 --request 0xCODE --bytes` for level-2
     parcel codes. Two are small (`0xa67fcdb2`, `0xa67fcdc2`) and two are our
     largest (`0xa6afcde2`, `0xa6afcdf2`). The bytes of each `0x0900` read
     are inflated from the tile.
   - **L4:** the start screen at level 1, as a regression check.
4. Controls:
   - **Positive:** factory card, request `0xa6afcdf2`.
   - **Negative:** our card, request `0xa40fcc02`. This level-2 parcel is
     outside the London bbox, so our tile leaves it empty.
5. Scope:
   - Section 1 levels 1 and 2. Section 4 stays empty, as `tile_relayout.empty_model`
     leaves it.
   - L4 at the zoomed-out screen is not reachable in this sandbox. The zoom
     buttons need the NK1 kernel with `ze0_launch.py --ui-start`
     (`docs/emulator/running.md` §2). That path takes the unit flash dump
     (`nand.bin`), which is never in the repository or the sandbox. The
     card-only B+A path (`run.sh`) boots to the map with no touch input.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the map client reads our level-2 road records. A request for one
  of our parcels reads exactly our zlib stream, which inflates to our code,
  and is answered with sub 3. The factory record gets the same answer through
  the same readers. A parcel we left empty gets sub 9 and no read.
- **FACT:** a 230,708-byte level-2 record (85,441 B compressed) is read and
  inflated whole at L3. It is 3.7× the factory's record for the same parcel.
- **FACT:** adding level 2 leaves the level-1 start screen and its error set
  unchanged.
- **OBSERVED:** with every highway, one London level-2 parcel needs more than
  65,535 dwords in its unit. The 16-bit offset word overflows, so level 2
  must be generalised. Tertiary-and-up plus 10 m simplification fits.
- **OBSERVED:** section 4 is not what draws place labels on the level-1
  screen. Our tile's section 4 is empty, yet the Z5 labels draw from the
  level-1 odd records (layer 25 + `0xc068` names). This agrees with C-2393:
  the traced render paths read no section 4.
- **UNKNOWN:**
  - Whether the zoomed-out screen draws our level-2 roads and urban area.
    Resolved by M5.100: it does.
  - Whether a record 3.7× the factory size draws without a slowdown or a
    `MeshDtChk` line.
  - Whether level-2 odd records are ever requested. The default request kind
    reads only the even record.

## Conclusion

Level 2 of the from-zero tile now comes from OSM (`tile_from_zero.py
--level2`) and passes L1 and L3. The zoomed-out L4 screen needs the NK1 +
flash-dump run on Marek's side, so it is follow-up M5.100.
