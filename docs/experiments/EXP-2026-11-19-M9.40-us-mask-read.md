## Question

Does the chunk-engine run take each build's `inflate_mask[17]` from the
image, or does it write zlib's public table at the build's VA — and does the
image actually hold that table at the US VA `0x005987b0`?

## Hypothesis

Before: the `zero-mask` run writes `2^n - 1` at the build's slot and reads
nothing (code at `run_firmware`, "Not read from the image"). Both VAs are in
compressed `Navi.exe` sec1; if they are `INIT`, `nk_decomp` can decode them
(H51) and the 17 words can be read.
**Falsifier:** the decoded US sec1 at `0x005987b0` is not `2^n - 1`; or a run
that reads the mask changes either build's decode verdict or counters; or a
planted wrong word at the US VA still passes the read.

## Interpretation

Before this task the run **wrote** the public table at both VAs; it never
read the image. Both slots are `INIT`, and the kernel's own decompressor
yields 17 words equal to zlib's `2^n - 1` at **both** the EU and the US VA,
so the US slot named by the `shll2` site really holds the table (EU was
already read once by H51, C-3486; the US one had not been). A run that loads
the mask from the image gives exactly the counters and verdicts of the
written-mask run on all three pairs, so no verdict depended on the write. The
read path is falsifiable: one changed word or the other build's VA fails it
before the engine starts.

C-3535 already says the harness *writes* the values (corrected after
`3d79bcc`), so its row is left as printed; C-4181 adds the read. The
runner's `cmp_ind_reader_us2011` target keeps its declared `sec1_table`
(scope: no target change); its "not read from the image" provenance is now
backed by C-4181 and can switch to `read-mask` in a follow-up.

## Conclusion

The `zero-mask` log now prints `WRITTEN … OBSERVED (not read)` per build, and
`--sec1 read-mask` reads the table from the decoded image and refuses any
other series. US2011 `0x005987b0` and EU2011 `0x00574de0` both hold
`inflate_mask[17]`; decode verdicts are unchanged.
