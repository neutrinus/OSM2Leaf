## Question

Does the +2.8 km median POI latitude residual of M4.8b track a POI's public
entrance, its building/feature centroid, or neither — now that M4.27 has
decoded the fine position held in the POINT name record?

## Hypothesis

Neither. M4.27 (C-2679) showed that copy A's low nibble is `11xx` marker bits
plus a 4-unit sub-cell index, not a 1-unit coordinate, and that two fine fields
in the same record give metre-level position. The residual is therefore a
decoding error (marker bits read as position), not a physical entrance/centroid
effect; M4.8b's 1-unit reading inflates latitude by up to 15 units.
**Falsifier:** if the fine position were not dramatically closer to the
reference than the 1-unit reading, or were no better with the two fine fields
exchanged, the residual would not be explained as marker bits read as position
and an entrance/centroid cause would remain live.

`refs` decodes each record's fine position
(`u(v, f) = (v & ~15) + 4·(v & 3) + f/512`; lat origin 32768/192, lon origin
52736/128), the legacy 1-unit reading (lon origin 52742), and the fine position
with the two fields exchanged, and prints the haversine distance of each to the
reference. It also prints the median, max and count within 100 m for each model.

### 3. Controls

*Positive:* `point_census --selftest` (`point_census selftest: OK`, 32 checks,
including the position round trip within 2 m at four world locations).
*Negative (must be worse):* the legacy 1-unit reading and the fine fields
exchanged are computed on the same 16 records; the earlier r2 shifted-reference
control (median 444.5 km, C-1905) shows the reference matching is not trivial.

### 4. Scope

The 16 reference POIs of the M4.8b TSV (13 US, 3 EU), the same corpus as
M4.27's `refs` fit. No new POIs, no firmware search, no vendor bytes emitted.
`coord.py` constants and code are unchanged (the formula lives there since
M4.61). Not covered: the firmware reader (M3.34, rule 13 — this is OBSERVED,
not accepted/confirmed), and the r2 entrance hunt, which is overtaken by C-2679.

### 5. Falsifier

As in Hypothesis: fine model not ≈5× closer than the 1-unit reading, fewer than
80 % of POIs closer, or the swapped-field control nearly as good, would refute
the marker-bit explanation.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** On the 16 POIs, the fine model gives median 114.6 m (max 2159.1 m,
  8 within 100 m) versus the 1-unit reading's median 3557.7 m (max 6790.0 m,
  0 within 100 m), closer in 16/16; the swapped-field control is 1567.9 m
  median with 0 within 100 m.
- **OBSERVED:** The residual does not track the entrance or the centroid. It
  tracks the choice of decoding: copy A's low nibble is `11xx` marker bits plus
  a 2-bit sub-cell index (C-2679), so the 1-unit reading treats marker bits as
  position and is off by up to 15 units; the in-record fine fields restore
  metre-level position.
- **OBSERVED:** C-1903's latitude bias is explained, not fitted: the median
  +2763 m of M4.8b is the same marker-bit error seen as a northward shift; the
  48× magnitude spread (132–6401 m, C-2682) is the varying low-nibble offset,
  consistent with a per-POI sub-cell index rather than a single fixed offset.
- **OBSERVED:** C-1904's "more than 1.5 km from reference, no metre-level
  position" is overturned: the position was in the same name record all along,
  just not in the fields M4.8b read.
- **UNKNOWN:** The firmware code that converts (copy A, fine) to map units is
  not located (M3.34); rule 13 keeps this OBSERVED, not accepted/confirmed.

## Conclusion

The +2.8 km latitude residual tracks **neither** a POI entrance nor its
centroid: it is the `11xx` marker bits of copy A read as a 1-unit coordinate.
M4.27's fine-position formula (C-2679, now in `tools/coord.py`) reduces the
median error from 3557.7 m to 114.6 m on the 16 references, closer for 16/16,
with the swapped-field control at 1567.9 m. The r2 entrance hunt and its
precheck/Stop are overtaken by that result; no entrance coordinates, no OSM and
no `coord.py` change are needed. C-1903 is retracted (bias explained) and
C-1904 is retracted (metre-level position does exist); new C-2683/C-2684 carry
the corrected OBSERVED interpretation with `Supersedes:`. C-1902 (the 52742
longitude origin) is left untouched. Falsifier did not fire.
