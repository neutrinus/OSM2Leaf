## Question

Can the factory's generalised level-1 built-up fill (`0xc12b`) be derived from
OSM data by a defensible building-footprint density rule, and does that rule
agree with the factory cover better than M5.89's guessed `--urban-fill 0.1`
threshold?

## Hypothesis

A parcel whose summed `building=*` footprint area (with the landuse cover on
the same 0..1 scale as a fallback) reaches a threshold chosen by a sensitivity
sweep reproduces the factory's built-up cover per parcel. Falsifier: the
derived rule's agreement does not beat the 0.1 baseline, or L4 black-screens /
logs `MeshDtChk`.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** per-parcel built-up cover can be derived from OSM building
  footprints plus landuse on one share metric, and the resulting tile mounts
  and draws at L4 with 0 factory records.
- **FACT:** in the London bbox the OSM landuse cover share is ≥ the building
  footprint share in every parcel that qualifies, so building density adds no
  new filled parcel at any tested threshold; the "derived from buildings"
  claim is only supported as an alternative signal that agrees with landuse,
  not as an independent driver of the fill.
- **OBSERVED:** on F1 the data-derived T=0.20–0.25 slightly beats the M5.89
  guessed T=0.1 (0.2437 vs 0.2396), but recall stays ~0.145: OSM landuse and
  footprints cover far fewer parcels than the factory's generalised 0xc12b
  (27,122 factory urban parcels vs 606 generated).
- **OBSERVED:** `agree_at_tol` alone is a misleading score because of the
  61,772 true negatives; precision/recall/F1 were added to the comparison tool
  for this reason.
- **UNKNOWN:** why the factory generalises built-up cover over ~27k parcels
  while OSM gives 588 building parcels / 606 filled; a buffer-and-merge or
  per-parcel cell grid (as the task suggests) could close that gap but was not
  implemented in one session.

## Conclusion

`tools/osm_areas.py --buildings` now counts footprints; `tile_from_zero.py
--building-density` fills on the max of building and landuse share;
`tools/builtup_compare.py` measures the factory/ours cover per parcel and sweeps
thresholds. The derived threshold 0.20–0.25 gives a marginally better
filled-parcel F1 than the guessed 0.1, but the building signal does not change
any parcel's decision in this bbox, so a defensible *building-density-driven*
fill is not yet demonstrated. Claims C-8770–C-8777 record the measured facts;
C-8778–C-8779 are reserved. Next: a buffer-and-merge / cell-grid rule that
lets buildings drive the fill (follow-up), and M5.96 line layers.
