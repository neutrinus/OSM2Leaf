## Question

Why do most `no_*` restrictions matched by `rd_restrictions.py match` carry no `0xff` regulation word naming the from-entry on the to-entry (152/411 at level 1, 128/235 at level 2 on B48R058R, C-2627), and what is the `only_*` encoding (C-2628)?

## Hypothesis

none / exploratory. Candidate explanations for `no_*` misses: prohibition stored on the from-entry (`reverse`), word present with byte1 != `0xff`, word naming F present on a different entry, nibble/position mismatch, OSM/graph mapping differences (two-way entries, `no_u_turn` geometry), or the prohibition not encoded as an `0xff` word naming F at that node. Candidate `only_*` encoding: "all other exits carry an `0xff` word naming F" (C-2628, previously not separated from shuffle). **Falsifier (per M4.25):** the pre-stated PASS (n >= 20, hit >= 0.80, shuffle <= 0.30, gap >= 0.50) would refute the "encoding absent / not the simple form" conclusion if met on the audited mapping or on a second tile; a miss class left unnamed/uncounted would mean the audit did not satisfy done-when.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED (named partition):** Every `no_*` miss in the counted B48R058R L1/L2 corpus falls into at least one named class, and the exclusive first-match partition covers 259/259 (L1) and 107/107 (L2) misses. The dominant class is `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere` (208/259 L1, 90/107 L2 exclusive), meaning the matched junction carries no `0xff` prohibition word naming the OSM from-entry anywhere on a non-edge entry. This explains the miss as *absence of that word form*, not as a nibble/position reversal in most cases.
- **OBSERVED (small mapping/encoding variants):** `reverse_ff_on_F_names_T` (19 L1, 7 L2), `ff_naming_F_on_other_entries` (21, 9) and `other_byte1_on_T_names_F` (11, 1; byte1 values 1/2/3 observed) are real but small named classes. They do not account for the majority of misses.
- **OBSERVED (directionality pattern):** The simple `0xff`-on-T-naming-F convention is strongest where directionality is explicit (`arr->lv` 64.7%/70.7%, `two->lv` 67.7%/81.8%) and weakest for `two->two` (23.0%/39.0%) and `no_u_turn` misses (97/116 L1 misses have no ff naming F). This is consistent with M4.23's class breakdown but now attached to named miss causes.
- **HYPOTHESIS (not proven):** For misses with `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere`, the factory may encode the restriction by a different word form (byte1/nibble scheme not in `{F,0xff}` on T), at a different graph element/level, with different from/to way mapping at split/two-way geometry, or not at all in section-2 for that junction. The audit cannot distinguish these from outside this encoding.
- **OBSERVED / NEGATIVE (`only_*`):** The C-2628 convention (`all_other_exits_ff_naming_F`) has hit rates 61.7% (L1) and 71.5% (L2), indistinguishable from or below the measured forbidden-slot density (61.8%, 74.5%) and random-exit baseline (64.1%, 74.7%, seed 42). No tested alternative candidate separates positively: `T_has_ff_naming_F` 4/240, `T_has_any_naming_F` 21/240 (mostly byte1 1), `F_has_ff_naming_T` 22/240, no `0xf` all-entries marker on T/F. `only_*` encoding remains **UNKNOWN** (C-2916).
- **UNKNOWN:** True encoding for the absent-word `no_*` majority; `only_*` encoding; meaning of bits 11-8 and byte1 1/2/3 in this context (consistent with M4.23 unknowns); whether way-via/split geometry or level selection explains specific two-way misses; firmware consumer semantics (rule 13).

## Conclusion

M4.25's precheck reproduces C-2627 (152/411 L1, 128/235 L2, FAIL). The mapping audit partitions every `no_*` miss into named, counted classes: most misses (208/259 L1, 90/107 L2 exclusive) have **no `0xff` regulation word naming the from-entry anywhere** on the matched node, so the simple M4.23 convention is absent rather than mis-stored in those cases; smaller named classes are reverse storage (19/7), 0xff naming F on a different entry (21/9), and other byte1 on T naming F (11/1, byte1 1/2/3). The pre-stated PASS does not pass. A second-tile attempt found no accessible B48R tile with n>=20 dolnoslaskie via coverage. For `only_*`, named candidates were counted and the prior "all other exits barred" convention is indistinguishable from background ff-naming-F density (~62-75%); `only_*` encoding is not found. `docs/research/claims.md` gains C-2910–C-2917 (audit findings / negative `only_*`). No format doc is changed (encoding remains unconfirmed for firmware, rule 13).
