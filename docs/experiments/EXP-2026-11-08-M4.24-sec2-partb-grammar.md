## Question

What is the unit-1 (part-b, odd record) grammar of section-2 kinds `0x2046` (level 2) and `0x03e4` (level 5), such that every odd record of `B28R070R.DAT` (76 pairs, level 2) and `LEV3456R.DAT` (34 pairs, level 5) is reproduced word for word and the mark and side words are explained by the parent record?

## Hypothesis

The writer's part-b structure (`rd_encode.build_record_edges`: `[nn, 0xa100]` + per-node `[0x2000, k, 0, k]` + per-entry `[other, SIDE_B if edge else 0, other]` + parcel-code tail) holds for factory records with node/entry words derived from the parent record (node index order for grid tiles; edge side from `cls & 0xe000` via `SIDE_B`). Falsifier: any odd record whose words cannot be produced from the parent record by a single grammar (structural mismatch, unexplained field, or ordering not derivable from the parent).

## Observations

Precheck (reproduced): `B28R070R` L2 `0x2046` 76 pairs, 16 strict-ok / 60 strict-bad, factory length 76/76, marks hi-nibble 2,4,6,8,a,c (values `0x2100,0x4100,0x6100,0x8100,0xa100,0xc100`, counts 8,15,16,15,15,7); `LEV3456R` L5 `0x03e4` 34 pairs, 34 strict-bad, factory length 34/34.

**Structural grammar (OBSERVED, 110/110 pairs parseable):**
- Unit 1 header: `w[0]=nn` (node count), `w[1]=mark` (`mark & 0xff == 0x00` in all 110), length `n = 2 + 4*nn + 3*E + 2` words (E = parent total entries). Tail `w[n-2],w[n-1]` = parcel code of the record, on all 110.
- Body is a sequence of `nn` items `(nb[4], entries[3]*deg)`: `nb[0] == deg<<12` for every node (295/295 L2, 34,362/34,362 L5), `nb[1]` is a parent node index and the sequence is a **permutation of all parent nodes** (all 110). Each item is followed by exactly `deg = len(parent.nodes[nb[1]].entries)` entry triples.
- Entry first word `e[0]`: equals `parent entry.other` for all 590/590 L2 entries. For L5, equals `parent entry.other` in 83,806 cases and equals `other & 0x3fff` in 8,166 cases; all 8,166 differing cases are cross-parcel references (`other & 0x4000` or `other >= nn`) and satisfy `e[0] == other & 0x3fff` (8,166/8,166). (183 L5 cross-parcel refs where `e[0]==other` also observed.) Cross-parcel mask rule is **OBSERVED for L5**, not established as the full rule.
- Node ordering: **L2 `0x2046` (B28R070R)** — the 76 pairs parse with `nb[1]==i` in index order (295/295 nodes). **L5 `0x03e4`** — `nb[1]` is a permutation, not index order and not `(x,y)` sort, `(y,x)` sort, or edge-first+index (tested on slot 2, order `[17,10,11,12,18,0,2,3,...,39]`). The ordering rule is **UNKNOWN**.

**Mark (OBSERVED, not explained by parent):** L2 values `0x2100,0x4100,0x6100,0x8100,0xa100,0xc100` (step `0x2000`, high byte `0x21+0x20*k`). **Side word `nb[2]` (OBSERVED, not fully explained):** 16-value vocabulary shared with entry middle words on L2: `0x0000,0x0040,0x0080,0x00c0,0x4000,0x4040,0x4080,0x40c0,0x8000,0x8040,0x8080,0x80c0,0xc000,0xc040,0xc080,0xc0c0`. Derivations **UNKNOWN**.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED (structural, 110/110):** part-b unit 1 is `[nn, mark, (nb[4], entries[3]*deg(nb[1]))*nn, parcel_code]` with `nb[0]=deg<<12`, `nb[1]` naming a parent node (a full permutation), `e[0]=other` (L2, all) or `other & 0x3fff` for cross-parcel refs (L5, 8,166/8,166 diffs). L2 node order is index order (76/76); L5 node order is a parent-derivable-in-principle but **unknown** permutation (34/34). This refines C-2622/C-2623's "not decoded": the skeleton is now parseable, but not word-for-word reproducible.
- **UNKNOWN / done-when not met:** mark derivation (from parent), `nb[2]` (side) derivation, `nb[3]`, `e[1]`, `e[2]`, and the L5/L4 node ordering rule. Because mark and side are not explained by the parent record and the record cannot be reproduced word for word, the M4.24 done-when is **not** met. No C-288x claim is registered (insufficient to claim "established"); the structural observations above may support future claims once explained.
- **Writer disagreement (context, M4.23):** factory mark varies (not fixed `0xa100`), and factory node/entry fields differ from `rd_encode.build_record_edges`' fixed `[0x2000,k,0,k]`/`[other,SIDE_B,other]` form.

## Conclusion

M4.24 is **blocked**. The part-b skeleton is structurally parsed for all 76 `0x2046` L2 and 34 `0x03e4` L5 pairs (length/tail/`nb[0]`/permutation/`e[0]` cross-parcel mask for L5), extending M4.23, but the fields required by the done-when — mark and side explained by the parent record — and full word-for-word reproduction (including `nb[3]`, `e[1]`, `e[2]`, and the L5 permutation order) are not solved. A word-for-word parser is not delivered. Re-opening requires deriving mark/side/`nb[3]`/`e[1]`/`e[2]` and the node ordering as deterministic functions of the parent record (no fitting), then reproducing all 76+34 odd records.
