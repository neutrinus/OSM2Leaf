## Question

In `TestMode.exe` (image C, US2011 then EU2011), what caller chain reaches
`CTestModeGPSInit::WriteGpsData` (`fn 0x0003aa80`), what text does the
technician enter, what are the six frame grammars (`GpsDataSetDate`
`0x00043da8`, `GpsDataSetLatLon` `0x00043f70`, and `GpsDataSetB0_A3`
`0x0004443c` / `B0_A0` `0x00044494` / `B3` `0x000444ec` / `B4`
`0x0004453c`), and what example frame seeds 52.23 N 21.01 E?

## Hypothesis

`0x0003aa80` is screen-reachable (not dead code), its two text fields are
`DDMM.mmmm`, and the four A0/A2 frames are 7-bit-sum + `0xF0` binary frames
that can be encoded from the disassembly alone. **Falsifier:** no caller of
`0x0003aa80` is reachable from a screen/menu table (dead code), or the derived
frame fails its own checksum/7-bit validation.

### 3. Controls

- **Positive:** `sh4xref --to 0x198f0` must find the literal-pool load in
  `fn 0x3aa80`; it printed `pool 0x0003ac18 holds 0x000198f0 / load
  0x0003aaa8 (r4) fn 0x0003aa80 [pdata]`. `--calls 0x3aa80` must list
  `jsr @r3 -> 0x43da8` and `jsr @r3 -> 0x43f70`; the test file's own
  checksum must validate (`OK (10 checks)`).
- **Negative:** `--selftest` deliberately XORs the checksum byte and sets bit
  7 of a 7-bit payload byte; both must raise `FrameError` (the negative
  function is inlined in the tool, no image needed). A big-endian reading of
  `sh4dis`'s pin case (`e62f` → `mov #47,r6`) is documented nonsense in
  `sh4dis.py --help` and was not used.

### 4. Scope

Static analysis of the uncompressed `TestMode.exe` section (rva `0x1000`,
size `0x4ca00` bytes) in two builds, US2011 and EU2011 (2 images, 1 module,
6 key functions + 4 constant setters + 1 helper + 1 caller chain). The two
compressed sections (`rva 0x4f000` and `0x5b000`) were not decoded; they are
`.data`/resources, not executable code (C-2128 in `AGENTS.md`). No frame was
sent to a real receiver; nothing here is L3/L4/L5 evidence (rule 13).

### 5. Falsifier

If no caller of `0x3aa80` were reachable from a screen table, the function is
dead code and the physical steps are unfounded; if the derived lat/lon frame
failed its own checksum or 7-bit check, the byte grammar is wrong. The
caller chain (`0x5c3e0 → 0x2c04c → 0x3aae8 → 0x3aa80`) is the positive
result that keeps the falsifier from firing; the hardware reachability of the
specific screen remains unproven.

- `fn 0x3aa80` disassembly: `mov.w @(0x3aacc,pc),r9` loads `0x193c`; `mov.l

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** `0x0003aa80` is reached by `fn 0x3aae8` ← `fn 0x2c04c`, whose VA
  sits in the screen/dispatch table at `0x0005c3e0`; the falsifier "no caller
  reachable from a screen" did not fire. The physical button path to that
  screen is still UNKNOWN (no hardware).
- **FACT:** The six frame grammars are derived from the disassembly, not from
  EXP-M1.50 F6; `GpsDataSetLatLon` is the position writer (`0x43f70`), the
  date is `0x43da8`, and the four `0x4443c…` setters are B0_A3, B0_A0, B3,
  B4 respectively.
- **OBSERVED:** The example frame for 52.23 N / 21.01 E is
  `A000013F2269004C7839000048F0`; the date frame for 2026-11-22 11:36 UTC
  is `A21A0A160B24000069F0`.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Writing the date frame first fixes the receiver's
  1024-week rollover (EXP-M1.50 U2), and seeding the Wrocław position helps
  H2a; neither has been tried on a unit.
- **UNKNOWN:** What the four B0_A3/B0_A0/B3/B4 commands do inside the
  receiver; whether the exact text field format on the screen matches the
  internal `DDMM.mmmm` parse; whether the technician can reach `fn 0x2c04c`
  from the retail UI.
