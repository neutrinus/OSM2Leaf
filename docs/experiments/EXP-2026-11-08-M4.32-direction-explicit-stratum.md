## Interpretation

- **VERIFIED (precheck):** M4.30 `deepen` arr->lv/two->lv numbers reproduced exactly (C-3010).
- **VERIFIED (full pre-specified union):** The task's pre-specified combined rule `base OR v1(reverse_ff_on_F_names_T) OR v2(other_byte1_on_T_names_F) OR v3(ff_naming_F_on_other_entries)` does **not** reach pre-stated PASS on held-out test (n>=20) at L1, L2, or pooled L1+L2, because v3's shuffle analog is structurally non-discriminating (74.5–76.5% shuffle; fires when F has 0xff words on entries other than U, independent of U). Hit rate crosses 80% on some held-out tests but shuffle/gap fail the pre-stated criterion (C-3011).
- **VERIFIED (discriminating decomposition):** `base+v1+v2` (variants with destination-discriminating shuffle analogs) reaches pre-stated PASS on L2 held-out (n=51, 42/51=82.4%, shuffle 1/51=2.0%) and on pooled L1+L2 held-out (n=102, 82/102=80.4%, shuffle 5/102=4.9%). Pooled result is **not independent** (57 overlapping relation IDs across levels). L1 held-out misses (40/51=78.4%, hit<0.80). This is reported as decomposition evidence, not as a replacement pre-specified rule (C-3012).
- **VERIFIED (residual word-form/position):** Union residuals (41 = 20 L1 + 21 L2; 19 test) are dominated by T-empty (14) or T-has-0xff-naming-other-entries (26) with no word naming F in any tested byte1 on T and no ff-naming-F on other entries/reverse — consistent with M4.30's "no ff word naming F anywhere" but now audited for position/form: no second-word-on-T naming F found except non-hit structural absence; one train case shows other-b1 naming F on a non-T entry (position/form outside named set). This supports "alternate encoding/position not covered by named variants, or prohibition genuinely absent at node" over "simple reverse/byte1-on-T" for these residuals (C-3013).
- **VERIFIED (junction-mapping audit):** Residuals show no `osm_from_eq_to`, no duplicate OSM way bearing, and low ambiguous margins (7/41 <15°). Junction-mapping errors of the tested kinds do not explain the dominant residual class (C-3014). Count-mismatch bearing exclusions remain M4.34 scope.
- **HYPOTHESIS (not firmware-confirmed):** The direction-explicit prohibition encoding is plausibly `base+v1+v2`-class (0xff-on-T-naming-F, reverse ff-on-F-naming-T, or other byte1 on T naming F) for `arr->lv`/`two->lv`, with remaining residuals requiring wider position/form search or alternate storage; firmware consumer VA confirmation required (rule 13, M4.26 C-2805 context) (C-3015).

## Conclusion

M4.32 reproduced M4.30 precheck and tested the pre-specified combined direction-explicit stratum (`arr->lv`+`two->lv`, base plus named M4.25 variants v1/v2/v3) with held-out (sha256 relation id mod2, bucket1=test) and symmetric shuffle analogs. The full pre-specified union does **not** reach pre-stated PASS on held-out n>=20 because v3's shuffle analog is structurally non-discriminating (73–77% shuffle), despite high hit rates. The discriminating decomposition `base+v1+v2` reaches pre-stated PASS on pooled L1+L2 held-out (n=102, 80.4%, shuffle 4.9%, 57 overlapping IDs — not independent) and on L2 held-out (n=51, 82.4%, shuffle 2.0%), but L1 held-out is 78.4% (miss). Residual `no_ff_word_naming_F_anywhere` misses (41 total, 19 test) are dominated by T-empty or T-has-ff-naming-other entries; junction-mapping signals (dup/from==to/amb<15°) are rare and do not explain them. No firmware-confirmed format claim (rule 13). Follow-ups remain M4.33 (`two->two`/u-turn/from==to), M4.34 (count-mismatch exclusions), and M4.26 consumer VA.
