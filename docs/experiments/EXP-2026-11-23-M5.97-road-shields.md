## Question

M5.90 writes `1=NAME;2=REF` road names; the labels draw but no shield does
(C-8704). What makes the factory draw the A4/A400/A401/A3201 shields?

## Hypothesis

Candidates: (a) the odd record's `points` section (w15/w16), (b) the `2=REF`
text itself, (c) the road shape's class. Falsifier per candidate: removing
it from the factory tile leaves the shields, or adding it to ours draws none.

## Inputs

Factory tile; our M5.90 tile; London start position 51.5098 N, 0.1342 W.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the `points` section does not draw anything on this screen
  (A, B pixel-identical to the factory).
- **FACT:** the shield text is the `2=REF` part of the road name (C).
- **FACT:** a shield is drawn only on roads of class 4 or lower; class 5
  (`provisional-v0` primary/secondary) draws the name instead (M5.90, F vs
  M5.90). Class 4 keeps the brown main-road style of the factory screen.
- **OBSERVED:** where a shape shows a shield the firmware does not draw its
  street name (C: names appear when the refs go).

## Conclusion

Shields are now drawn in the from-zero tile: `tile_from_zero.py` defaults to
`--class-override primary=4,…,secondary=4`. The full measured class table
stays M5.98.
