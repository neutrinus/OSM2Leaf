## Question

Can `tools/tilewriter.py`'s in-place `0x2046` splice write an even (road) record
and its odd (background) partner so that `verify_factory` passes on a real
`B50R058R` level-1 build without skipping its semantic record check and without
breaking the M5.53/M5.54 wide-row kinds?

## Hypothesis

Writing the pair as one unit (or appending the whole slot block and repointing
the tag whenever the in-place span could touch a partner or the reader's walk)
makes the real `B50R058R` L1 build pass `verify_factory`; the prior failure is a
writer/container defect, not a card-format limit. The `0x10000` bound is a
**reader-derived** constraint, not a guessed constant:
`clarion_tile.read_dir` lines 100–104 stops the pair table on `ln > 0x10000 or
X > 0x10000` (from the real B30R048 slot-21 raw tail), and EXP-2026-11-23-M5.70
O5 / C-6811 / C-6812 record it for real `B50R050R` parcels of 105,288 /
96,684 / 67,380 inflated words. It is **not** a firmware reader VA or an L3
verdict (rule 13); the EXP must label it "checked-in reader + real tile", not
"firmware-confirmed".
**Falsifier (task M5.69, quoted from TODO.md):** "a fix that makes
`verify_factory` skip the check, or one that passes `B50R058R` but breaks a
M5.53/M5.54 wide-row kind." Operational form: plant a real byte change at an
*unregistered* odd partner pair and show the fix's `verify_factory` still FAILs;
plant a wide-row kind and show its selftest/census still PASSes.
