## Question

Can the `ICADM0nn` field decode that matrix row 14 called "claim-only" be made
reproducible by a committed tool that censuses every shard on both cards and
counts how much of each file it explains — and which part, if any, stays
unexplained?

## Hypothesis

The 462-byte-stride ISO table and the term-record run in `docs/format/refer.md`
§ICADM account for most bytes of every shard.  **Falsifier:** the walk explains
under half the bytes of a shard; then the claimed layout is wrong or partial,
and the unexplained part must be named with a value histogram instead of being
guessed.  A second prediction: the `0x00e7` tag is actually the stride stored
as 231 halfwords, and the firmware reader for it is still unlocated (M3.18 r3),
so no VA may be asserted.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** The two `.cmp` files above with their SHA-256; all 33
   EU and 11 US shards under the same roots are the counted corpus.
2. **Commands.** Runnable as written (paths as on this machine):

   ```bash
   python3 tools/icadm_decode.py --selftest
   python3 tools/selftest.py --only icadm_decode
   python3 tools/icadm_decode.py census \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m4.8b-eu/REFER001 \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory/REFER001
   python3 tools/icadm_decode.py census \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m4.8b-eu/REFER001/ICADM001.DAT.cmp \
     --mutate-byte 0x1005
   python3 tools/icadm_decode.py census \
     /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory/REFER001/ICADM001.DAT.cmp \
     --mutate-byte 0x1503
   ```
3. **Controls.** *Positive:* the synthetic selftest builds a two-entry ISO
   table (`stride=0x00e7`, ISO 20/840, Latin-1 labels), five term records of
   both observed forms plus a marker, and checks that the census decodes all
   of them (100 % explained, 5 records, `stride_hist={231:2}`, first term
   `COUNTRY`).  Factory shards are a second positive control: every shard has a
   run of `0x00e7`-tagged entries and printable terminology.  *Negative:*
   flipping one bit of the first stride tag at `0x1005` (`0xe7→0x18`) must
   change the stride histogram; flipping the first term length at `0x1503`
   (`0x07→0xf8`) must change the record count; in the synthetic file a mutated
   length must lower coverage.
4. **Scope.** All 33 EU2011 and 11 US2011 `ICADM0nn.DAT.cmp/.ind` pairs
   (113,561,600 + 6,274,816 decoded bytes); no other REFER family, no 2014 or
  2016 card, no firmware code.  The `m5.12c/decoded` tree mentioned in the task
   input was checked and set aside: its ICADM001 is byte-identical to the US
   factory shard (SHA-256 `91f6b0cc…`), i.e. a generated card, not EU factory
   data.  The census therefore uses the two factory roots above.
5. **Falsifier.** Under 50 % explained on any shard, or no detection of the
   mutated byte, refutes the walk.  The unexplained bytes are printed as a
   per-shard histogram, not assigned a guessed meaning.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the 4096-byte CLARION header, the `0x1000` table with `count`
  462-byte `0x00e7`-tagged entries and the fixed-offset term blob are present
  on every one of the 44 shards; the census explains 88.98 % (EU) / 98.06 %
  (US) of all bytes, with no shard below 74.964 %.  The falsifier (>50 %) is
  not met on any shard.
- **FACT:** the stride value is `0x00e7` on every entry (histogram
  `{231:33}` EU / `{231:2}` US).  No firmware VA for that reader was found
  (M3.18 r3, negative), so the result is a histogram, not an attribution.
- **FACT:** the term blob's dominant record is a 12-byte prefix + `2*len`
  bytes of Latin-1 text; 174/10,819 US records carry a `u16 value` trailer,
  10,582 do not, and `code=0x0006, len=0` is a 14-byte marker.  The text
  decodes to real terminology (`ADMINISTRATION`, `AUTOHÄNDLER`, `PARROÒUIA`).
- **OBSERVED:** the unexplained bytes are concentrated in a small pre-blob
  index region and in the second record family (`flags != ffffffff`); their
  byte histogram is printed per shard, e.g. 1,388 B / 84 runs on EU ICADM001.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the second family is a different terminology section (region
  names such as `NORD-PAS-DE-CALAIS`, `LIMOUSIN` were seen in spot dumps), but
  no reader or field dictionary confirms it, so the tool does not decode it.
- **UNKNOWN:** the firmware reader VA for the 462-byte stride and the term
  blob; the meaning of the second family's `flags`/`value` fields; the
  `code`→terminology mapping.  These are left open (G2 / M3.18 follow-up).

## Conclusion

`docs/format/refer.md` §ICADM now records the reproducible header/ISO table,
the stride histogram, the term-record forms and the full two-card census;
matrix row 14's Offline cell changed from `claim` to `tool` and its evidence
column cites C-8360–C-8369 and this EXP.  C-8360–C-8369 were registered via
`tools/board.py add-claims`; the originally reserved C-7840–C-7849 belong to
M4.84.  What stays open is the firmware-side reader and the second record
family's field semantics, reported as a histogram rather than guessed.
