## Question

Marek (2026-11-23): on every emulator screenshot, water and parks are made
only of rectangles. Is this rendering or data?

## Hypothesis

The 2DG interpreter (`hw/sh4/ze0_2dg.c`) draws some area command as its
bounding box. Falsifier: the area commands of the start screen are plain
`0x80` polygon covers, which are already filled even-odd.

## Inputs

The factory start screen (B+A, London, `Pow Off 1st Detect`) and the
zoomed-out NK1 screens of M5.100.

## Observations

O1. **Op census, factory start screen.** `0xe0` 170, `0xd0` 169, `0x82`
160, `0x80` 9, `0xa0` 5, `0xb3` 5. The dump file was cut at 120 KB, so
these counts are a lower bound.

O2. **Areas are drawn as `0xe0`, `0xd0`, `0x82`, then `0xb3`.** In this
sequence:
- `0xe0` gives the polygon box.
- `0xd0` gives N vertices, for example 38 for St James's Park.
- `0x82` has op `0x820032e0`, a 32×32 texture, and the four corners of
  the box.
- `0xb3` draws the outline from the same vertex array.

Icons use op `0x82003ae0` with bit `0x800` set (colour key). Their quad is
the size of the texture (for example 24×22), and they follow a 4-vertex
rectangle.

O3. **Before the fix.** `texquad()` stretched the 32×32 pattern over the
whole box and ignored the polygon. The result:
- every area is a pixelated rectangle;
- its `0xb3` outline has the right shape, drawn over the rectangle.

O4. **After the fix.** A `0x82` that follows a `0xd0` now repeats the
pattern from the quad corner and writes only pixels inside the polygon
(even-odd). Without a pending polygon the old stretch is kept.
- **Factory start screen:** `3ccbb525…`. St James's Park and its lake
  follow their outlines, and no rectangles are left. The result is the
  same over two boots.
- **NK1, zoom steps 2–3:** on both cards the Thames is a river, not
  blocks.
  - Factory screens: `fd762beb…`, `c16563e6…`.
  - From-zero screens: `22bb957f…`, `1bd83624…`.
- No `MeshDtChk` line.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the rectangles were an emulator defect, not map data. Areas
  are a stencil polygon plus a repeated pattern (`0x82`), and
  `texquad()` drew only the box.
- **FACT:** with the fix, water and parks follow their polygons on the
  factory card and on the from-zero card.
- **OBSERVED:** `0x82` is masked by the preceding `0xd0` polygon. That
  the hardware masks it this way, and repeats the pattern from the quad
  corner, is our reading: it matches the outlines, but no hardware
  register says so.
- **Consequence:** every L4 screen hash taken before this patch was made
  with the box fill. That includes the factory reference `7e23cc73…` and
  the from-zero references `806d6ce8…`, `fa771a60…`, `a1b95d9a…` and
  `c5bae8aa…`. Comparisons stay valid within one QEMU build. New runs need
  new references, such as the factory `3ccbb525…`.

## Conclusion

Water and parks are now drawn with their real shapes. The remaining
blocky look is gone on both cards. What is left is our data: label
density and small parks (M5.101).
