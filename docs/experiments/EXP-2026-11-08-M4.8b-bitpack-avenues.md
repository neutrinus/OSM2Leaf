## Question

Do the remaining avenues named by M4.8d — bit-packed (non-name-record, `00 xx
00 yy` / non-`0x60/0x68` zlib pages) POINT pages and "other methods" — yield a
0..2047-class geographic sub-unit position field that lets M4.8b test
`coord.py` local scale 1 vs 2 on ≥ 10 real POIs, or otherwise meet M4.8b's
done-when (origin/local scale stated, error in metres, `coord.py` self-test
pinning the chosen constants)?

## Hypothesis

Exploratory (none confirmed). H1: a bit-packed POINT page contains a 0..2047-
class local coordinate pair linkable to a named POI, distinguishing scale 1
from scale 2. H2 (other method): a systematic latitude correction fits the 16
M4.8b reference POIs well enough to pin a transform constant.

**Falsifiers.** H1 refuted for the tested scope if the bit-packed pages remain
undecoded and no raw packed copy-A reference of the M4.8b POIs, and no
identifiable 0..2047-class *geographic* field linked to those POIs, is found by
structural probe. H2 refuted (as a transform fact) if the per-POI latitude
residual varies widely across references so a fitted constant is a fit to
Wikipedia centroids / POI storage points, not a pinned transform.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED:** POINT bit-packed (non-name-record) zlib pages are numerous and
  structured (headers with `0xffff` sentinels, shard/region-varying `w0`) but
  their record grammar and field meanings are undecoded (C-2936). The raw packed
  copy-A pair of a reference POI does not appear as a plain `>HH` in that
  shard's bit-packed pages (0/1796, P01/POINT002), so no simple cross-reference
  to a sub-unit field was found by this probe.
- **OBSERVED:** ~49% of sampled `u16` values in bit-packed page prefixes are
  ≤ 2047. Because field boundaries are unknown, this is not evidence of
  identified 0..2047-class *geographic coordinate* fields (it reflects mixed
  small integers/indices/flags in an undecoded structure), and no such field
  was linked to any of the 16 M4.8b POIs (C-2937).
- **OBSERVED (negative, H2):** The 16-POI latitude residual varies over ~11
  packed units across Wikipedia-centroid references; a best-fit −3 offset
  reduces median error but is a reference-dependent fit, not an independently
  pinned transform constant. `coord.py` constants are unchanged (C-2938).
- **UNKNOWN:** Local scale 1 vs 2 remains untestable. No 0..2047-class
  geographic sub-unit position field linkable to real POIs was found in
  bit-packed pages by the structural probe performed, and bit-packed grammar
  decoding is out of scope here (C-2939). Origin 52742 for POINT/ADDRE longitude
  remains supported (C-1902, unchanged).

## Conclusion

M4.8b's remaining avenues (bit-packed POINT pages, other methods) were
attempted but do not meet the done-when. Bit-packed pages
(`POINT002/008/029`, 5405 pages total characterized) are undecoded; a raw-A
cross-reference test and a prefix `u16` density probe found no decodable
0..2047-class geographic sub-unit position field linked to the 16 reference
POIs. The latitude-offset "other method" fits the 16 Wikipedia-centroid refs
only as a non-pinnable fit (wide spread), so it cannot set local scale or pin a
constant. The 16-POI baseline and `coord.py` 19/19 checks are unchanged.
`docs/research/claims.md` gains C-2936–C-2939 (replacing M4.8d's RESERVED
placeholders) and TODO M4.8b remains **blocked** with a refined blocker.
