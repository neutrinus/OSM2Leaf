## Question

Is there a sub-unit position field in `POINT`/`ADDRE` records — a
`0..2047`-class local value — that would let M4.8b test local scale 1 vs 2
(`coord.py` `--local-scale`, C-960's hypothesis: 2 local units per packed
unit, 0..2047, vs 1 per packed unit, 0..1023)?

## Hypothesis

Exploratory. H1: a POINT name record body contains an 11-bit (0..2047) local
coordinate pair refining or replacing the packed copy-A position, which would
distinguish scale 1 from scale 2. Falsifier for H1: no 0..2047-class field in
the tested record scope correlates with geographic local position (matches
`local(A)` at scale 1 or 2 above noise, or yields a plausible position when
interpreted as tile-local coordinates), and ADDRE has no 2047-class sub-unit
position field.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** The EU/US 2011 images and extracted trees above (same
   as M4.8b). The 16-POI reference table is the M4.8b TSV (Wikipedia coords,
   rule 12); records addressed by `(shard, stream_offset, record_index)`.
2. **Commands** (all run from the worktree root; real-image scans read vendor
   data outside the repo and commit no bytes):

```bash
python3 tools/poi_subunit.py --selftest                  # expected: OK (10 checks)
python3 tools/poi_subunit.py scan /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory POINT002 POINT008 POINT010 --limit 30000
python3 tools/poi_subunit.py scan /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m4.8b-eu POINT017 POINT029 --limit 20000
python3 tools/poi_subunit.py refs --us /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory --eu /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m4.8b-eu /tmp/opencode/m4.8b-refs.tsv
python3 tools/coord.py --selftest                         # expected: 19/19
python3 tools/addre.py --selftest                         # expected: selftest OK
```

   The scan classifies every aligned BE `u16` in the first 88 bytes of each
   record by its 0..2047 membership and tests 2047-class candidates against
   `local(A)` at scale 1 and 2 (`coord.latlon_to_local` of copy A in A's cell).
   A separate bit-scan (inline, method below) checked all 11-bit windows in the
   first 48 bytes (BE and LE bit extraction, excluding A bits 16–47 and B bits
   96–127) against `lx2,ly2,lx1,ly1` over 2,000 records.

3. **Controls.** *Positive (tool):* `poi_subunit --selftest` builds a synthetic
   0x60 record with A = the M4.8 fixture `(41167,53693)` (B40R060R, local_s2
   `(414,878)`) and body fields `v6=0x0800|100`, `v8=200`, `v16=576`, `v20=832`;
   it verifies `v6` has bit 11 set with low 11 bits = 100, `v8/v16/v20` are
   2047-class, `local_of_a` reproduces the fixture, and the correlation test
   reports 0 false matches for `v8=200` vs `lx2=414`. *Positive (data):* copy A
   decodes to the known M4.8b positions and `coord.py` 19/19 passes (the
   transform under test is sound). *Negative (geographic):* interpreting the
   2047-class pair `(v6 & 0x7ff, v8)` as tile-local scale-2 coordinates in A's
   cell yields 43–681 km errors on the 16 M4.8b refs (median-scale hundreds of
   km), i.e. it is not a local geographic position.
4. **Scope.** 30,000 US zlib-framed 0x60/0x68 name records
   (`POINT002,008,010`, `iter_streams`/`iter_point_records`; the first 30k
   yielded in order) plus 20,000 EU (`POINT017,029`) — 50,000 records total.
   Excluded: bit-packed POINT pages (`00 xx 00 yy`, a different page type not
   decoded by `iter_point_records`, the same exclusion as M4.8b); POINT index
   records (24-byte, 12 u16, f11 is a page/slot id per M4.8b, not a geographic
   local coordinate); ADDRE record bodies beyond the M4.19/C-1960–1969 `TT 40
   XX YY` token analysis and a confirming note that XX/YY are not 2047-class.
   Corpus counted, not "all".
5. **Falsifier.** H1 is refuted for the tested scope if (a) no 2047-class field
   matches `local(A)` at scale 1 or 2 at a rate distinguishable from noise
   (11-bit random ≈ 1/2048 per axis per position) and no candidate yields a
   plausible local position on the M4.8b refs, and (b) no other 11-bit
   bit-aligned field in the 48-byte prefix matches above noise. Stated before
   the result.

## Observations

### 2047-class `u16` fields in POINT name records (aligned BE, first 88 bytes)

US 30,000 records (POINT002,008,010) — EU 20,000 (POINT017,029) show the same
pattern:

| offset | US min..max | US <=2047 | class | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +06 | 2048..4094 | 0.0% | wide (flagged 11-bit) | `v6 >> 11 == 1` for 30,000/30,000; `v6 & 0x7ff` spans 0..2046 (2047 distinct) |
| +08 | 0..2046 | 100.0% | 2047-class | 2047 distinct values (US n=30k) |
| +10 | 1536..51730 | 56.9% | mixed | high bits vary (`v10>>11` takes 0,1,2,3,…) |
| +16 | 576..1088 | 100.0% | 2047-class (discrete) | values {576,1088} only |
| +18 | 33..12938 | 42.7% | wide | — |
| +20 | 512..1344 | 100.0% | 2047-class (discrete) | values {512,576,832,1024,1088,1344} only |
| +22 | 33..12944 | 42.6% | wide | — |

`+02,+04` = copy A (packed x,y), `+12,+14` = copy B. EU confirms: `+06` low
11 bits 0..2046 with bit 11 set 20,000/20,000, `+08` 0..2046 100%, `+16` {576,
1088}, `+20` {512,832,1024,1344}.

### Correlation with `local(A)` (tile-local of copy A, same cell as A)

US n=30,000, EU n=20,000 (matches = field value equals `lx` or `ly` of that axis):

| field | US ==lx2 | US ==ly2 | US ==lx1 | US ==ly1 | EU ==lx2 | EU ==ly2 |
| --- | ---:| ---:| ---:| ---:| ---:| ---:|
| `+08` | 18 | 28 | 11 | 11 | 11 | 26 |
| `+06 & 0x7ff` | 13 | 18 | 19 | 8 | 12 | 13 |
| `+16` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `+20` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Pair `( +06 & 0x7ff , +08 ) == (lx2,ly2)` or swapped, scale 1 or 2: 0 / 30,000
(US).

### 11-bit bit-scan (first 48 bytes, 2,000 US records)

All 11-bit windows, BE and LE, excluding A (bits 16–47) and B (96–127),
tested for equality to `lx2,ly2,lx1,ly1`. Top hits: BE 9/2000 (0.4%) at bit
199 for `ly2`; LE 24/2000 (1.2%) at bit 198 for `ly2`. No position above noise
(2000 records × many windows × 2048 values; 1% aggregate noise is expected).

### M4.8b refs: `(v6 & 0x7ff, v8)` interpreted as local scale-2 in A's cell

`as_local(p6,v8,s2): d_fromA` ranges 41,603 m (P12) to 679,855 m (P11),
`d_ref` 38,722 m to 680,833 m across 16 POIs (all far outside the tile cell;
P01–P16 per `poi_subunit.py refs` output). Example P14 (closest to ref):
A `(39710,52060)` B30R070R, `localA_s2=(1596,1708)`, `p6=1733 v8=1757`,
interpreted pos 36.5130/−5.1367, `d_ref=45270 m`, `d_fromA=43218 m`.

### ADDRE

M4.19 (C-1960–C-1969, `tools/addre.py assess_point_coords` / `--selftest`) on
1.62M `TT 40 XX YY` points (ADDRE001/002/003/017): `YY` full 0–255 (256
distinct), `XX` shard-capped and dense (max 23/50/58). A 2D geographic
`(lat,lon)` is not derivable from `(coord,XX,YY,tag)` alone; whole-unit
addition is a wrong hypothesis (14.0% in-box on Andorra, control), the 1/256
fractional candidate is in-box but not a verified 2D geographic mapping.
Neither `XX` nor `YY` is 0..2047-class. ADDRE's packed coord id is the same
`(x,y)` `u16` pair form as POINT copy A.

## Interpretation

- **OBSERVED:** POINT name records (0x60/0x68, zlib-framed, US+EU, n=50,000)
  carry two 11-bit 0..2047-class body fields at fixed positions: `p6 = v6 & 0x7ff`
  with `v6` always `0x0800|p6` (bit 11 set, n=50,000/50,000) and `v8 = rec[8..9]`
  spanning 0..2046. `v16`/`v20` are 2047-class by value but discrete structural
  constants ({576,1088} and 6 values), not continuous positions (C-2930, C-2932).
- **VERIFIED (negative):** `(p6,v8)` does not encode the tile-local geographic
  position of copy A at scale 1 or 2 and is not a plausible tile-local position
  of the M4.8b POIs when interpreted in A's cell (0/30,000 pair matches to
  `local(A)`; 43–681 km on 16 refs). No other 11-bit bit-aligned field in the
  48-byte prefix matches `local(A)` above noise (C-2931, C-2933).
- **OBSERVED (ADDRE):** ADDRE sub-cell tokens are 0..255/0..58 (not 2047-class)
  and yield no verified 2D geographic sub-unit mapping (C-1960–C-1969).
- **UNKNOWN:** A sub-unit *position* field (0..2047-class local value) for POI
  coordinates is **not found** in the zlib-framed POINT name records or in
  ADDRE records examined. The meaning of `(p6,v8)` and the high bits of `v6/v10`
  remains undecoded (they may be bit-packed attributes; the field dictionary
  gives bit offsets such as area code 32/44, category 58 — see refer.md).
  Bit-packed (non-zlib, `00 xx 00 yy`) POINT pages are out of scope and not
  decoded; index records carry no geographic local coordinate. Local scale 1 vs
  2 therefore remains untestable from the record fields examined (C-2934).

## Conclusion

M4.8d searched POINT/ADDRE records for a 0..2047-class sub-unit position field
to unblock M4.8b's local-scale test. It identified 2047-class body fields in
POINT name records (`v6` low 11 bits + bit-11 flag, `v8`; and discrete
`v16/v20`) but showed, with positive and negative controls, that none encode a
geographic tile-local position of copy A (50k records, bit-scan, 16 M4.8b refs).
ADDRE has only 0..255-class sub-cell tokens with no verified 2D mapping
(M4.19). The sub-unit position field is **UNKNOWN** in the tested scope, so
M4.8b's local-scale clause is not answered by this method; M4.8b is re-opened
with its blocker refined (a 0..2047-class position field was not found in
zlib-framed POINT name records or ADDRE). `docs/research/claims.md` gains
C-2930–C-2935; `docs/format/refer.md` gains a short note on the 2047-class body
fields (OBSERVED, meaning unknown). Bit-packed POINT pages remain a follow-up.
