- **Status:** done (M8.H35 positive; M8.H39 tap reaches `kepdrv` but is locked by the UI state)
- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Sample:** NK1 `firmware/unit-flash/nk1-QY7200ND.bin` SHA-256

  card `LEAF_2011-2012_US.img` SHA-256 `53342430…c5383` (ROM image A);

- **Tools:** QEMU 11.1.3 with the `ze0` series plus `ze0-h01` and the new

## Question

What does `lcddrv.dll` exchange with the display unit on `SCI3:`, what
answer makes the link come up, and does a touch frame from a host peer reach
the UI on the running NK1 + A system?

## Hypothesis

The display unit speaks the same DLE framing as the micon line, the driver
waits for a handshake, and touch arrives as one command whose payload is the
6-byte record of `docs/firmware/touch.md`. **Falsifier:** the driver never
reads the line, or the link drops / crashes with a correct peer.

## Method

1. Static: `RcvAnalysisData` (`0x03d1664c`) switch table, the phase
   handlers and the slot writers (`tools/sh4xref.py --to`, disassembly).
2. Dynamic: boot NK1 + A with the US card, `-icount shift=0,sleep=off`,
   a peer on `sci3`. Runs R16–R25 used a scratch peer; L1 used the committed
   launcher: `python3 tools/ze0_launch.py --card US.img --flash nand.bin
   --gdb 1242`, then `tap 400 240` on `panel.ctl`.
3. Breakpoints with `tools/ze0_gdb.py break` on the queue writer, the IOCTL
   handler and the kepdrv path.
4. `ZE0_SCIF_TRACE=scif3` and `hpbdmac-trace=on` for the serial layer.

## Observations

**O1 — serial layer.** Before `ze0-h02` no byte from a peer reached a driver:
the FIFO accepted only `16 - rx_head` bytes, DMA stopped after two pages,
SCLSR.TO (the receive timeout the driver waits for when DMA receives) did not
exist, and TXI was not wired, so every `WriteFile` of the LCD driver returned
`Write size disagreement. (6:0)` (36 times in R20, 0 in R21 after wiring
TXI/TEI).

**O2 — handshake.** With a peer answering `23` → ACK + `24` frame,
`25` → ACK, `71` → ACK + `71` frame, the serial log shows
`LCD: Error history registration [SYS_DIAGLOG_F_DISP_CNCT(SYS_DIAGLOG_REG_OK) ACC-ON]`
(R16–R25, L1). The driver then sends `71` and `42 31 31 31 09 31` and polls
`71` about once per guest second (`scr` writes at 22.495, 23.505, 24.515 s
guest time in R22).

**O3 — crash and its cause.** R17–R22 crashed about 3 s of guest time after
the link came up: `Exception 0e0 … PC=03d18602 … TEA=00a7996a` in
`Scif3RcvThread` and `PC=03d18708` in `TimerThread`. The link object pointer
at driver state `+0x434` (only writer: `0x03d14f4a`) held `0x00a79406`
instead of `0x00a794e0` (object from a breakpoint at `0x03d180e4`). The frame
table at `+0x1b4` is 20 × 32 bytes and ends at `+0x434`. Just before the
crash the driver sent 8 ACKs at once (R21 peer log 11.05 s), i.e. one read
held many stale frames. `serial_scif.dll`'s receive handler (`0x03c99ed4`)
computes the DMA write position as `DTCRn − DTCSR`, plus one page when
`DSTSR & 0x20`; the model set bit 1 and left DTCSR at 0 after a page
switch, so the driver re-read a whole page. With bit 5 and live
DTCSR/DDASR (R24) the link ran 718 polls with no NAK, no reconnect and no
exception; L1 likewise (0 exceptions over 6000+ log lines).

**O4 — touch command.** `sh4xref --to 0x03d16300` → loaded only by the
`51` case (`0x03d167ac`); `--to 0x03d161dc` → the `34/36/37/3f` case.
Frame `10 02 51 01 90 00 f0 31 10 03 12` (press 400,240) and the release
`… 30 10 03 13` were ACKed by the driver (R24) and hit `0x03d16300` once each
and `0x03d17258` (IOCTL `0x800b205c`) twice each (R25).

**O5 — kepdrv.** In R25 a press at (200,200) hit `InputLcdTouch`
`0x03d284e4` → `NotifyKeyTouchCommon` `0x03d27d2c` → `0x03d27190` →
`0x03d27a7c` → `IsKeyControlNotify` `0x03d27344`, and none of
`0x03d27450`, `0x03d26020`, `0x03d27528` (the posting steps). The release
stopped after `0x03d27a7c` (`CheckKeyStatus`). Static reading of
`0x03d273d2`–`0x03d273f2`: for touch a nonzero `(obj[+0xc4] | obj[+0xc5]) & 0x0f`
drops the event. A 10-sample drag across the map left the screenshot
byte-identical (SHA-256 equal, 384 000 non-black pixels both).

**O6 — launcher.** `ze0_launch.py` starts QEMU and `panel.py --sci3`;
`tap 400 240` on `panel.ctl` answered `OK tap 400 240 2 events`, the peer
logged `sent 51019000f031` / `sent 51019000f030`, and the breakpoints at
`0x03d16300` (2) and `0x03d284e4` (4) were hit.

## Result

The hypothesis holds: same framing as `anwdrv`, a three-phase handshake,
touch = command `51` with big-endian x, y and an ASCII status. The full
grammar and command table are in `docs/firmware/lcddrv.md`. The tap is
delivered to `kepdrv` but locked there by the key-control state the UI sets
while it stays in `INI_STARTUP`; the screen does not change. M8.H39's stop
condition ("event arrives but the UI does not react: record the UI state")
is reached.

## Controls

- Negative (no answer reaches the driver): runs R1 and R3 of this session,
  before the receive path worked, log
  `SYS_DIAGLOG_F_DISP_CNCT(SYS_DIAGLOG_REG_ERROR) ACC-ON` instead of
  `REG_OK`; the same build with the answering peer logs `REG_OK`.
- Negative (bad BCC): `panel.py --selftest` answers NAK and does not record
  the frame.
- Positive: breakpoints on the queue writer fire exactly once per frame (2
  frames, 2 hits), so the hits are not background traffic.
