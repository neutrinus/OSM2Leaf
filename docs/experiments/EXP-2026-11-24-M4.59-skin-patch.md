## Question

Can one visible ROM bitmap be replaced in place on a card image so that the
patched image differs from factory only in the listed ranges, both `SUM4`
words recompute to their stored values, and the patched PNG still decodes?

## Hypothesis

The pixels of the startup/player skin are PNG assets (section 3 of
`substance.skn` or built-in ROM A PNGs, C-6506/C-6508). A same-size recolor of
one ROM PNG, written at both `SUM4`-covered copies, changes only those PNG
bytes plus the two `SUM4` words. **Falsifier:** a byte diff shows changes
outside the four reported ranges, either `SUM4` recomputation disagrees with
the stored word, the patched PNG fails to decode, or `cerom.py`/`cardcheck.py`
stop parsing the image.

## Method

### 1. Sample and hash

EU2011 head and full card with the SHA-256 above; the ROM PNG was located by
walking valid 8-bit PNG headers inside `[0x200200, 0x200200+0x01752c88)` —
617 signature hits, 614 with a valid IHDR — and the first (file `0xcce7b0`,
255×147, bit depth 8, colour type 6) was chosen. The head was reflink-copied
to scratch for every write; the factory image was never modified.

### 2. Commands

Runnable as written from the worktree (the scratch paths are the author's;
any writable path works):

```bash
S=~/projekty/leafre-work/scratch/m4.59
# no-vendor selftest and discovery
python3 tools/skin_patch.py --selftest
python3 tools/skin_patch.py plan --image archive/head256.img --offset 0xcce7b0

# patch a reflink copy in place, then check the two SUM4 words and PNG
cp --reflink=always archive/head256.img "$S/patched-head256.img"
python3 tools/skin_patch.py patch --image "$S/patched-head256.img" \
    --offset 0xcce7b0 --in-place
python3 tools/skin_patch.py verify --image "$S/patched-head256.img" \
    --offset 0xcce7b0 --factory-sum4 0xcdf87db0
python3 tools/skin_patch.py diff --before archive/head256.img \
    --image "$S/patched-head256.img"

# negative control: patch the PNG but leave the SUM4 words at factory value
cp --reflink=always archive/head256.img "$S/negative-nosum.img"
TMPDIR="$S" python3 - <<'PY'
import sys
sys.path.insert(0, "tools")
import skin_patch as sp
img = "/home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/scratch/m4.59/negative-nosum.img"
off = 0xcce7b0
with open(img, "rb") as f:
    f.seek(off); orig = f.read(10500)
new = sp.recolor_png(orig)
with open(img, "r+b") as f:
    for where in (off, off + sp.COPIES[1] - sp.COPIES[0]):
        f.seek(where); f.write(new)
PY
python3 tools/skin_patch.py verify --image "$S/negative-nosum.img" \
    --offset 0xcce7b0

# parsers on the patched bytes (head: cerom, full card: cardcheck)
python3 tools/cerom.py --image-start 0x200200 "$S/patched-head256.img" romhdr
cp --reflink=always \
    archive/sd-images/LEAF_2011-2012_QSD-309-101_navi158_eu.img \
    "$S/patched-card.img"
python3 tools/skin_patch.py patch --image "$S/patched-card.img" \
    --offset 0xcce7b0 --in-place
python3 tools/cardcheck.py --image "$S/patched-card.img"
```

### 3. Controls

*Positive:* `plan` on the factory head reports `stored_sum4=3455614384`
(`0xcdf87db0`) and `actual_sum4=3455614384` on both copies, i.e. the checksum
formula and the two container locations are real; `cerom.py romhdr` parses the
patched head and `cardcheck --image` returns `RESULT: PASS` on the patched
full card. *Negative:* (a) with the PNG patched but the `SUM4` words left at
factory, `verify` reports `sum4_ok false` with stored `0xcdf87db0` vs
recomputed `0xba0b22dd` on both copies; (b) corrupting the last IDAT CRC byte
makes `png_chunks` raise `PNG chunk CRC mismatch` in the selftest; (c) `sum4`
rejects a non-word-aligned buffer; (d) `plan` refuses a buffer without a PNG
signature. Both negative cases have the positive controls above.

### 4. Scope

Offline, EU2011 only. The head (268,435,456 B) and one full card were patched;
the changed bytes are two 10,500-B ROM PNGs at `0xcce7b0`/`0x2ace7b0` and the
two 4-B `SUM4` words. Excluded: the TFAT16 `substance.skn` PNG assets (the
same-size edit works there but that volume is outside the ROM checksum), the
three other cards (sizes/hashes only in M4.58), any L3/L4 run of `auirtdll`
(no emulator harness can run the reader on a regenerated skin), and the car
test (M6, Marek, after S0). No vendor bytes are committed.

### 5. Falsifier

As stated in Hypothesis. A change outside the four ranges, a checksum
mismatch after the fix, an undecodable PNG, or a parser failure would have
refuted the claim; none occurred.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** a same-size recolour of a ROM-A PNG changes exactly four byte
  ranges (the two copies of the PNG and the two `SUM4` words) in a 256 MiB
  head (C-6400, C-6402, C-6405).
- **FACT:** both `SUM4` words recompute to their stored value after the fix
  (`0xba0b22dd` on python3; `0xb3d5155f` on python3.11/3.13), and the fix is required — leaving the factory words makes
  `sum4_ok false` (C-6401, C-6403).
- **FACT:** the patched PNG decodes in both copies (valid CRCs, correct raster
  length and dimensions) and both parsers still accept the image (C-6404,
  C-6406, C-6407).
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the unit actually draws the changed pixels (L3/L4/L5;
  rule 13). The offline consistency proof is a necessary pre-condition, not
  acceptance (C-6408).
- **OBSERVED:** zlib here is zlib-ng, so the new IDAT is a decode-valid stream,
  not a factory-identical recompression; the original IDAT is replaced by a
  new one and the length is held by an ancillary `zZzZ` chunk.

## Conclusion

`tools/skin_patch.py` now patches one same-size ROM bitmap at both container
copies, fixes both `SUM4` words and reports the four changed ranges. On the
EU2011 head and full card the byte diff, checksums, PNG decode, `cerom.py` and
`cardcheck.py` all agree; the selftest passes 12/12. Claims C-6400–C-6409 back
the observations. Nothing ran on the unit, so visible acceptance remains
open for M6.

## Corrections after navigator review (TL, solo task)

- **CI failure on py3.11/3.13 was real, not a flake.** The first fixture used
  `zlib.compress(raw, 6)`; inversion of the synthetic raster re-compressed two
  bytes larger on stock zlib 1.3.2, so `recolor_png` raised
  `ValueError: recolour would grow the PNG (3421 > 3419)`.
  Fix: the fixture IDAT is now `zlib.compress(raw, 0)` (stored deflate, fixed
  layout on every zlib) and the recolour blackens RGB (compresses well on
  zlib-ng 1.3.1 and stock 1.3.2 alike). Commands: `python3.11
  tools/skin_patch.py --selftest` and `python3.13 tools/skin_patch.py
  --selftest`, both `12 ok, 0 fail`; patch of the real head on each version
  gives `sum4_ok true`, `png_complete true`, `diff count 4`.
- **Gate timeouts were flakes.** The two 60 s selftest timeouts in the first
  gate run (`ci_gate_repro`, `lev3456gen`) both pass isolated with a 150 s
  timeout: `PASS 123.43s` and `PASS 90.49s`, `passed=2 failed=0`.
