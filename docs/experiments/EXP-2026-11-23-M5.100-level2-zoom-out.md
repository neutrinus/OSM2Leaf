## Question

M5.91 left the zoomed-out screen unchecked (C-8716). Does the firmware draw
our level-2 records when the map is zoomed out?

## Hypothesis

After enough ZOOM OUT taps the map leaves level 1 and draws section 1
level 2 of `B48R068R`. If so, the from-zero card shows OSM roads where the
factory card shows factory ones. Falsifier: either of these on our card at
the zoom steps where the factory shows level 2:
- a blank or `MeshDtChk` screen,
- the factory picture.

## Inputs

- Card F: factory.
- Card A: M5.91 tile `7ffabd97…62ade372`, with level-2 roads and urban only.
- Card B: M5.91 tile rebuilt after the census fix in O3, `0717a913…d878b49d`.
  - Level-2 background: forest, heath, park, river area, water and urban,
    with types `0x50xx`.
  - Place labels: city, town and suburb (`0x5068` / `0x500c`).
  - Ring simplification at 15 m.
  - Card `14ce9edf…` (before the map-code patch). `cardcheck` PASS. Its
    level-1 B+A screen is still `c5bae8aa…49550ba`, with the factory's 1,043
    error lines.

## Method

1. Sample and hash: above.
2. Each card goes through the same steps (`zoomseq.sh`, scratch, not in the
   repo):
   1. Start: `ze0_launch.py --card CARD --flash nand.bin --gdb 1240 --ui-start`.
   2. When `SRT_COLD_I_AGREE` shows in the serial log (50–60 s wall time),
      tap panel (277, 98) twice, then (403, 98). (403, 98) is the EU card's
      consent "OK", at screen (403, 382).
      Result: State `MAP_MAPOPERATION_NORMAL_MAP`.
   3. Six times: tap ZOOM OUT at panel (765, 200), which is screen (765, 280)
      from the plane-1 capture. Wait 30 s, then take a `screen` and a
      `plane -n 1` capture.
3. Controls:
   - **Positive:** card F at the same steps.
   - **Negative:** card A has no level-2 background. If B's water and parks
     appear, they come from the new records.
4. Scope: start position (London), zoom steps 0–6. The serial logs and the
   flash dump stay outside the repository.

## Observations

O1. **NK1 runs with the dump.** The EU card boots to the London map. Touch
works after `--ui-start`, and the map controls (Map Menu, ZOOM OUT, scale
"1/16mi", ZOOM IN) are in DU plane 1. No run has a `MeshDtChk` line.

O2. **The zoom ladder (card F).**
- Steps 0–1 are level 1.
- Steps 2–3 are a coarser map with parks, the Thames, postal-district labels
  (WC2, SE11, N1) and road shields. This is level 2.
- Steps 4–6 are the overview, from `LEV3456R` and other tiles.

Screens: `3728e5be`, `d92cd684`, `71a35e2b`, `7b0b2926`, `8256c8b4`,
`47cdc154`, `cddb9e93` (SHA-256 prefixes; factory screens are not kept).

O3. **Card A.**
- Steps 0–1 are our level 1.
- Steps 2–3 draw **our level-2 roads**: tertiary and up, with A-road shields
  (`c4cfaa98…`, `01d9da18…`). They have no water, no parks and no place
  labels. The background is plain.
- Steps 4–6 match the factory overview:
  - Steps 4 and 6 differ by 3,072 px and 219 px (overlays).
  - Step 5 is the same overview with a shifted centre.

This contradicts the M5.91 census, which listed only urban and sea at level
2. A full parse of the 4,008 factory level-2 odd records that parse finds the
level-1 scheme with type `0x50xx` for `0xc1xx`:
- layer 3: forest `0x501a` 6,673, heath `0x501b` 192, park `0x501d` 1,789;
- layer 7: river area `0x5014` 337, water `0x5015` 3,728;
- layer 1: sea `0x5012` 4,084;
- layer 0: urban `0x502b` 25,045;
- lines: layers 8–11;
- place labels: names entries `0x5068`/`0x4800` 28,833, and layer-25 points
  `0x500c`.

The M5.91 census had stopped at the records it parsed first.

O4. **Card B.** Steps 2–3 draw from OSM: the Thames, parks, the roads with
shields, and place labels (KING'S CROSS, SOHO, COVENT GARDEN, LAMBETH,
VICTORIA, …). Screens `03585279…`, `e6858bd3…`. Compared by eye with the
factory:
- **Labels:** many more, because suburbs are kept. They overlap at step 3.
  The factory uses fewer labels, such as postal districts.
- **Small parks:** many draw as red outlines, as at level 1 (M5.96).
- **View:** the visible area is not the same as the factory's at the same
  step. The scale label in plane 1 is overdrawn, so the scale could not be
  read from the capture.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** zooming out on NK1 with the unit flash draws section 1 level 2
  of the London tile. Our OSM level-2 records are drawn there: roads,
  shields, water, parks and place labels, with no new error line.
- **FACT:** level 2 uses the level-1 layer scheme with type `0x50xx` for
  `0xc1xx` / `0xc0xx` (background `0x501a`…`0x5015`, places
  `0x5068`/`0x500c`). This corrects C-8710.
- **FACT:** a 230,708-byte level-2 road record (M5.91) draws.
- **OBSERVED:** label density and small polygons need level-2 generalisation.
- **UNKNOWN:**
  - Why the visible area differs between runs at the same tap count.
  - Which zoom step uses which level, by scale value.

## Conclusion

Z6 passes at L4: the zoomed-out London map from OSM is drawn on the
emulated unit. Generalisation of level 2 (labels, minimum area) is
follow-up M5.101.
