## Question

"No factory record in the tile" is the acceptance of rungs Z3+ of
`docs/research/osm-from-scratch.md`. Can it be counted, independent of where
records sit in the file?

## Hypothesis

Matching records by the SHA-256 of their inflated payload separates factory
from generated records; a re-laid factory tile must count as 100 % factory,
an empty tile as 0 records. Falsifier: the re-laid tile (same records, other
offsets) counts less than 100 %.

## Inputs

The three tiles above.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** Sample and hash: above.
2. **Commands.** Commands:

   ```bash
   python3 tools/tile_provenance.py --selftest
   python3 tools/tile_provenance.py FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT W/z1/B48R068R.DAT
   python3 tools/tile_provenance.py FAC/MAPAL001/B48R068R.DAT W/z2/B48R068R.DAT --require-zero
   ```

3. **Controls.** positive — factory vs itself 3/3 (selftest) and the re-laid
   factory tile; negative — a synthetic tile with one kept and two new
   records gives 1/3, an all-new tile 0/1 (selftest).
4. **Scope.** Scope: records of all sections and levels the `tile_relayout` model reads;
   zero-length sub-records are not counted; the container is measured as
   header bytes equal to the factory header.
5. **Falsifier.** Falsifier: as in Hypothesis.

## Observations

Selftest: `tile_provenance selftest: OK (0 failure(s))` (5 checks).

Re-laid tile (M5.85), 9.5 s:

```

section 1 level 2: factory_records=7222/7222

section 2 level 2: factory_records=6428/6428

```

Empty tile (M5.87): `total: factory_records=0/0
container_constant_bytes=4088/4096`, `--require-zero` exit 0 (the 8 differing
header bytes are the section-table addresses).

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the count is position-independent: 172,319 of 172,319 records of
  the re-laid tile are found in the factory tile although 5,379 components
  moved. The falsifier did not fire.
- **FACT:** the from-zero container still takes 4,088 of 4,096 header bytes
  from the factory (strings, level table, box table); these are spec
  constants (C-5820: the kind bytes must stay), now visible as a number.
- **OBSERVED:** the M5.85 relayout counted 172,863 records; 544 of them are
  zero-length sub-record entries, which provenance does not count.

## Conclusion

`tools/tile_provenance.py` gives the Z3+ acceptance number; `--require-zero`
is the gate.
