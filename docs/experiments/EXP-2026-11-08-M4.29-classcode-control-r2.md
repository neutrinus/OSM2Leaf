## Question

Does `LCTBL001` `class_code` (u16 at +14) behave as a road-class field on a second country and on a non-motorway control set, under the same road-number→OSM-ref join and LORO-vs-shuffled-null test M4.17 used (C-1820–C-1825)?

## Hypothesis

Probe, with falsifier (inherits M4.17). Hypothesis: if `class_code` is a partial road-class key (C-1825), its leave-one-road-out (LORO) accuracy should exceed the shuffled-road-label null on (a) a non-EU-2011/second-country numbered-road sample joined by the same method and (b) a non-motorway control set. Falsifier (same as M4.17): LORO not exceeding the LORO shuffled-road-label null by ≥ 3 sd means the field does not generalise as a road-class predictor in that scope. A result below 3 sd is negative for that scope, not a pass.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (tool controls):** `road_class_probe.py --selftest` and `lctbl_records.py --selftest` pass in this worktree; the probe's positive/negative/confound semantics are intact (LORO 1.000 for a true class field; no LORO votes for a per-road identifier).
- **OBSERVED (EU 2016, same Dolnośląskie join):** EU 2016 reproduces M4.17's pattern on a third EU year: `class_code` LORO 0.563 vs null 0.546 (sd 0.136, +0.13 sd), below the 3 sd falsifier. Motorway-only codes `0x4e8e`/`0x4e91` recur (11/11 each), consistent with C-1824; mixed/default codes remain mixed.
- **OBSERVED (non-motorway control, Dolnośląskie join):** In all six stratified non-motorway scopes (EU 2011/2014/2016 × exclude motorway / exclude motorway+trunk), `class_code` LORO does not exceed the shuffled-road-label null by ≥ 3 sd. EU 2011 is below null (-3.29/-2.86 sd); EU 2014/2016 are above null by < 1 sd (+0.46 to +0.85). The M4.17 motorway-pure codes do not create a generalisable non-motorway class predictor; mixed codes (`0x283c`, `0x2777`, `0x2776`) remain class-mixed.
- **OBSERVED (US second-country candidate, structural):** US `LCTBL001` contains 157,218 numbered records (303,023 total) with US route refs (I-75/I-85/I-20/US-19 etc., Georgia-area refs visible) and a `class_code` distribution dominated by `0x4e8f` (118,973, 75.7% of numbered) unlike EU samples. This confirms a large second-country numbered-road corpus exists in the archive, but **LORO cannot be computed** because no matching US OSM `ref`+`highway` IMF/PBF is available in `leafre-work/osm/` (only Dolnośląskie). The M4.17 join is therefore not reproducible for the US sample with available inputs.
- **HYPOTHESIS (narrowed, C-1825):** `class_code` remains a hypothesis for partial road-class coding (small recurring motorway-pure codes in EU samples) but is **not** a general road-class field. The non-motorway control does not support it (no scope reaches the 3 sd falsifier). The second-country LORO test is not performed due to missing US OSM join data, so C-1825 is neither promoted nor retracted on a true second-country class-label join.

## Conclusion

M4.29 partially satisfies its Done-when: the EXP reports LORO vs null on a **non-motorway control set** across EU 2011/2014/2016 Dolnośląskie joins (six scopes), and no scope meets the 3 sd falsifier; EU 2011 non-motorway is below null, EU 2014/2016 are < 1 sd above null. This narrows C-1825: motorway-pure `class_code` recurrences do not generalise to a non-motorway class signal, and the hypothesis remains unestablished.

The EXP **does not** report LORO vs null on a **second country** because no US/second-country OSM IMF or PBF with `ref`+`highway` data is present in the accessible archive (`leafre-work/osm/` contains only Dolnośląskie). US `LCTBL001` (157,218 numbered records, Georgia-area route refs) exists but cannot be joined by the M4.17 method without that OSM input. Per the task Stop condition, the missing join is recorded and M4.29 is `blocked` (not `done`).
