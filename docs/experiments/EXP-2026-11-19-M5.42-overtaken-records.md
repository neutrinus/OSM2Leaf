## Interpretation

- **FACT:** merge `280cd74` (#217) set M8.A2 `[x]` / `Status: done` and
  withdrew the access-list clause; C-1240 ("so M8.A2 stays `blocked`") and the
  M8.A2 EXP Status header, §Verdict and navigator note ("Task status is
  `blocked`", "M8.A2 stays `blocked`") were written before it and were stale.
- **FACT:** merge `f5df583` (#219, M8.A10) moved the `build.sh` default to
  8.2.10; C-1240's "`build.sh --force` builds 8.2.7" and the M8.A2
  Reproduction step 2 now need `--version 8.2.7` to reproduce what they say.
- **FACT:** the factory `MAPAL001` value equals the sum of its 46 tile values
  (3,521,119,232 B) — a recursive subtree total (C-036, `docs/format/drstr.md`),
  **not** a structure offset as the M5.42 task text guessed. The
  `0xd1e00400 → 0xd2274b76` change (+0x474776 = +4,671,350) is that total
  growing by the appended tail.
- **FACT:** the two words change by 3 + 4 = 7 bytes, which accounts for the
  whole 7-byte precheck diff; nothing else in `DRSTR001.DAT` needs to change
  for a one-tile size change.
