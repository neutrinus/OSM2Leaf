## Question

Can the 366,814 B M4.86 left unexplained in `B48R058R` be modelled as the
section-4 member of the wide-row family, and does adding those spans drive
`tile_unknown.py`'s residual to zero (or to a set of blocks with an explicit
shape)?

## Hypothesis

Section-4 level-1 directories use the same wide-row idea as `0x2046` section 1
but with base kind `0x1046`, 256-byte narrow bodies and **768-byte wide bodies**:
a wide row carries four sub-record size pairs per column. Adding its row-block
spans to `tile_unknown.py` shrinks the residual.
**Falsifier:** the spans overlap records already modelled by `s1_wide_dir`, or
the unexplained figure does not move from 366,814 B, or no size/record rule fits
the 36 gaps.

## Method

A **spec**, not a narrative. The format source is the **directory layout read
from a command**: scratch probes on the named sample (not committed) dumped
row strides (`+0x000c`, `+0x0110`, … = 260 B for narrow; `+0x000c`, `+0x0310`,
… = 772 B for a wide row), the first body words (bit 31 of the first word marks
a wide row) and the per-column pairs; a generalized parser then counted 64/64
directories with `size_ok` and the wide-row rule.

1. **Sample and hash.** The single file above, full length; hash quoted from
   `sha256sum /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.4/card-factory/MAPAL001/B48R058R.DAT`.
2. **Commands.** Runnable as written (all paths absolute):

   ```bash
   python3 tools/tile_unknown.py /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.4/card-factory/MAPAL001/B48R058R.DAT --top 12
   python3 tools/section4_wide.py --selftest
   python3 tools/section4_wide.py census /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.4/card-factory/MAPAL001/B48R058R.DAT
   python3 tools/tile_unknown.py /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.4/card-factory/MAPAL001/B48R058R.DAT --top 12
   python3 tools/selftest.py --only section4_wide
   python3 tools/selftest.py --only tile_unknown
   ```

3. **Controls.** *Positive*: the known `0x2046` row model stays intact — its
   2,176 section-1 row blocks must still be reported by `tile_unknown.py`, and
   its 15.3-MB top group must stay in the "modelled" column; every section-4
   directory must satisfy `end - off == 2*kind`, `n_wide == (kind-0x1046)/0x100`
   and `encode(parse(dir)) == dir bytes`, with every record a zlib stream of
   exactly `X` inflated words. *Negative*: (a) flipping a byte inside a
   section-4 record must be reported as a bad record; (b) setting bit 31 on a
   narrow row's first word must break `size == 2*kind`; (c) a corrupted span
   that extends past its record must be rejected by the size/record checks.
4. **Scope.** EU2011 `B48R058R` section 4: 64 level-1 directories — 30 `0x1046`,
   13 `0x1146`, 6 `0x1246`, 6 `0x1346`, 3 `0x1446`, 2 `0x1546`, 1 `0x1646`,
   2 `0x1746`, 1 `0x1946` — and the 36 residual gaps from M4.86. No firmware
   run, no writer, no other tile or card year.
5. **Falsifier.** Any overlap between the new row-block spans and the
   already-modelled section-1/slot spans, a residual that stays at 366,814 B,
   or a directory whose size/record check fails would refute the model.
   Expected result: 64/64 directories pass, residual falls below 2 kB, and every
   remaining range is printed with its own shape.

## Plan

Written before the tool was coded:

1. Derive the section-4 row grammar from the directory layout with the scratch
   probes above (done: narrow 32×8 B, wide 32×24 B = one `(ln, X)` pair plus four
   `u16` compressed and four `u16` inflated sub-record sizes per column; wide body
   768 B; `kind = 0x1046 + 0x100·n_wide`).
2. Add `tools/section4_wide.py` with `parse_dir`, `encode_dir`, `row_block` and a
   `census` subcommand; check size, wide-row rule, zlib records, disjoint blocks
   and `encode(parse)` byte identity; give it `--selftest` with synthetic narrow
   and wide rows plus the three negative controls.
3. Extend `tools/tile_unknown.py` with a third pass that adds section-4
   row-block spans and relabels the remaining gaps, keeping the baseline and
   s1-wide figures unchanged.
4. Update only the section-4 text of `docs/format/map-tiles.md` with the observed
   grammar; register claim rows; write the observations below.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** all 64 section-4 level-1 directories satisfy the observed row
  grammar: size, wide-row rule, `encode(parse)` identity and zlib records pass;
  1,044 row blocks are pairwise disjoint (0 overlaps).
- **FACT:** adding those spans moves `tile_unknown.py` from 366,814 B / 36 ranges
  to 1,326 B / 2 ranges; the section-1 2,176 blocks and the 76.286 % baseline
  are unchanged, so the new spans do not overlap the already-modelled records.
- **OBSERVED:** the two remaining ranges are section tables (section 2 at
  `0xaec5f00`, section 4 at `0x1128cb00`), each printed with its own byte shape;
  they are not section-4 wide rows and are outside this task's model.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the same 256/768-byte row grammar applies to section 4 on the
  other EU tiles; only `B48R058R` was measured here.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether a section-4 sub-record's parcel code follows the
  section-1 `sub_code` rule (the check incremented no counter); no firmware
  reader VA is claimed (rule 13).

The falsifier is not triggered: the spans do not overlap modelled records and
the unexplained figure drops by 365,488 B. The Done-when's second branch holds —
every range that cannot be modelled is printed with its own shape.

## Conclusion

`tools/section4_wide.py` (new, `--selftest`-covered) parses every section-4
level-1 directory of `B48R058R` with size, wide-row, record and round-trip
checks: 64/64 pass, 94 wide rows, 3,091 records, 0 bad, 1,044 disjoint blocks.
`tools/tile_unknown.py` now adds those spans as a third pass: the residual drops
from 366,814 B / 36 ranges to 1,326 B / 2 ranges and each remaining block is
printed with its shape (both are section tables). `docs/format/map-tiles.md`
gains the observed row grammar in the section-4 section only. Ten claim rows
C-8420–C-8429 record the numbers. No firmware claim is made.
