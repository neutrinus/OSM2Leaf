## Question

Is KIWI a shortcut for reading or writing our ZE0 map files — i.e. does any KIWI structure decode our bytes well enough to adopt its names and layout, and if so, which follow-up decoder work does that imply?

## Hypothesis

After M4.63's "common concepts, different container" result the expected outcome is: at most one byte-level adoption (the ch.1.2 `SWS` size word, already found by M4.66), several concept/name-only families and several unrelated families. **Falsifier (task):** every family ends as `unrelated`; then the page must record the negative with its controls and close the lead in Retired leads. **Falsifier (check):** a verdict inconsistent with its own evidence, or a zero/sentinel-only raw-fit row promoted to `adopt`, must be rejected by the new checker.

### 3. Controls

*Positive (checker):* `kiwi_verdict_check.py --selftest` parses a canonical 10-row table and must print `canonical table OK (1 adopt, 3 partial, 6 unrelated)`; real-file mode `docs/format/kiwi-glossary.md` must print `OK (10 rows: 1 adopt, 3 partial, 6 unrelated)`. `selftest.py --only kiwi_verdict_check` must print `tool:kiwi_verdict_check  PASS`.

*Negative (mandatory mutation control, navigator M4.78 round 1):* the same selftest flips one cell at a time and asserts the check FAILS: (1) SWS row `adopt`→`unrelated` (must reject because EU=4854, US=4292 are informative); (2) SWS EU informative `4854/4854`→`0/4854` (must reject because adopt requires both cards); (3) D/SA row `unrelated`→`adopt` (must reject because 0/300 and 0/269 are zero/sentinel-only raw fits). All 3 must be rejected, printed as `3/3 mutations rejected`.

*Source-positive controls of the synthesis:* each cited probe's own `--selftest` builds a synthetic KIWI frame that must fit (M4.63: 4 header positives + 4 negatives + road/shape ±1; M4.70: synthetic 7.3 frame FIT with 3 mutations REJECT, ZE0 table rejected; M4.69/M4.72: synthetic Pearson +1/−1/0 controls pass). *ZE0-side control:* `harness.py verify B30R048R` rebuilds 1,358,592 B byte-identically with `background rebuild ok 2958 bad 0`, so the negative probes read genuine ZE0 records. *Cross-card control:* every "unrelated" verdict is observed on both EU2011 and US2011.

### 4. Scope

Covered: the ten family rows of the glossary decision table and the fifteen-row term → our name → file/offset → status mapping, derived from eleven committed EXPs and the claim ledger rows C-7630–C-7779. The checker reads only `docs/format/kiwi-glossary.md` (real mode) or a synthetic in-memory table (`--selftest`). Excluded: a real KIWI disc (`ALLDATA.KWI`, M4.77 — unavailable; no synthetic control can refute a negative on real KIWI bytes), the GPL KIWI source code (names and chapter numbers only), firmware reader VAs (rule 13), any L3/L4 run, and any change to `docs/format/*.md` field names beyond the three SWS tables already named in M4.66. No new decoder, no new `board.py add-task`, and no recompression were part of this task.

### 5. Falsifier

As stated: "every family unrelated" would have forced the negative-with-controls branch and a Retired-leads row; it did not fire because SWS is adopted. For the checker: any mutation accepted, or any mismatch between a verdict and its quoted counts, would have invalidated the page. `map_signals.py` `tile_bytes` must remain unchanged before/after this docs-only change — a movement would mean a decoder accidentally landed here.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (tool):** the decision table is internally consistent under the stated rule: 1 adopt / 3 partial / 6 unrelated, and the adopted row's informative counts are 4,854 (EU) and 4,292 (US) for slot pairs plus 24/16 for directory headers. A zero/sentinel-only raw fit cannot be promoted to adopt — mutation (3) proves the checker enforces this.
- **FACT (synthesis):** the KIWI container is not a ZE0 reader. Road, background, volume/management, address types, level/block, parcel-ID and route-planning families all fail on real bytes across two cards; only the ch.1.2 `SWS` size word decodes informative ZE0 fields, and the two remaining families give concept names only.
- **OBSERVED:** the task's falsifier did not fire (SWS is adopted), but the page still records the six-family negative with synthetic KIWI positives, a ZE0 rebuild control and a two-card corpus, so the absence of a real KIWI disc does not turn the negative into "no evidence".
- **HYPOTHESIS (not tested here):** the shared size-word idiom and guidance vocabulary come from a common Clarion/Xanavi design heritage; no causal or firmware-reader claim follows.
- **UNKNOWN:** a real KIWI volume (M4.77) is still the only positive control that could re-open the unrelated verdicts on actual KIWI bytes; no KIWI chapter was newly fetched in this task.
