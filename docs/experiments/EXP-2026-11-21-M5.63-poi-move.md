- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Sample:** EU 2011 card `archive/sd-images/LEAF_2011-2012_QSD-309-101_navi158_eu.img`, SHA-256 `105e2032e480e8ba162a2a9d4b6624cb1dafee380379a9908f3f101b15076307`, the pair `REFER001/POINT010.DAT.cmp` (SHA-256 `d02a8d87fa0c87b9a1c1cc84aa0a5e76bd97a7010e17656427f6ae46bfaf9ec3`) and its `.ind` (SHA-256 `3eaa47bb653dce9950c2e375801f175b537915dab84ce2ca6e9a3af82381e3ec`). ## Question

Can one existing POI of a factory `POINTnnn.DAT` pair be moved to a given position inside its own copy-A cell by rewriting only its name record's fine words and its spatial-tree leaf, with `point_census` census and joins still passing on the output and every other byte unchanged?

## Hypothesis

M4.27 (C-2676–C-2679) and M3.34 (C-6540–C-6545) show the position lives in two places only: the name record (`rec[6..7]` longitude fine, `rec[8..9]` latitude fine, both 11 bits) and the spatial-tree leaf (the same two words with the flag bits on top). Everything else — trie, index chain, phone tree, 400/2000 tables — carries no position. So a move within one copy-A cell needs exactly those two edits and the name chain re-packed in place.

**Falsifier:** a census or joins count below its factory number on the output; a changed byte outside the touched page's 4-byte slot and the leaf; a no-op run that is not byte-identical; or a pair with the record moved and the leaf left that `joins` accepts.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** As above; `point_census.py extract` copies the 33 pairs to a scratch tree outside the repo.
2. **Commands.** Runnable as written from the repo root with `S` a scratch directory and `Z` the stock `libz.so.1.3.1`.

   ```sh
   S=~/projekty/leafre-work/scratch/m5.63
   EU=archive/sd-images/LEAF_2011-2012_QSD-309-101_navi158_eu.img
   python3 tools/point_census.py extract --card-image "$EU" "$S/eu"
   cp "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.CMP" "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.cmp"; cp "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.IND" "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.ind"
   curl -fsSL -o "$S/zlib-1.3.1.tar.gz" https://zlib.net/fossils/zlib-1.3.1.tar.gz
   (cd "$S" && tar xzf zlib-1.3.1.tar.gz && cd zlib-1.3.1 && ./configure --prefix="$S/zlib131" && make && make install)
   Z=$S/zlib131/lib/libz.so.1.3.1
   python3 tools/point_move_poi.py --selftest
   # baseline
   python3 tools/point_census.py census "$S/eu" --files POINT010
   python3 tools/point_census.py joins  "$S/eu" --files POINT010
   # no-op control: the pair comes back byte-identical
   python3 tools/point_move_poi.py "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.cmp" "$S/eu-noop" --noop --libz "$Z"
   # the move: record 0 (name "ADAMS OF GLIN"), ~230 m east of its factory spot
   python3 tools/point_move_poi.py "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.cmp" "$S/eu-final" \
       --select "ADAMS OF GLIN" --position 52.5722928 -9.2824474 --libz "$Z" --json "$S/final.json"
   # independent readers on the output
   python3 tools/cmpind.py verify "$S/eu-final"
   python3 tools/point_census.py census "$S/eu-final"
   python3 tools/point_census.py joins  "$S/eu-final"
   python3 tools/point_census.py position "$S/eu-final" POINT010 --stream 0x6a7f00 --record 0
   # negative control: the record moved, the leaf left -> joins refuse
   python3 tools/point_move_poi.py "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.cmp" "$S/ctl1" \
       --select "ADAMS OF GLIN" --position 52.5722928 -9.2824474 --libz "$Z" \
       --control leave-leaf --json "$S/ctl1.json"
   python3 tools/point_census.py joins "$S/ctl1"
   # mirror control: the leaf moved, the record left -> the record does not reach the target
   python3 tools/point_move_poi.py "$S/eu/POINT010.DAT.cmp" "$S/c2" --record 0 \
       --position 52.5722928 -9.2824474 --libz "$Z" --control leave-record
   # sample of 39 records every 400, each moved ~13 m at --span 40
   python3 "$S/batch2.py"    # loops point_move_poi.py with --record N --span 40
   ```

   The tool walks the name chain through its locator (`point_pair_regen.chain_via_locator`), builds the record table from the page record walk (`walk_records`), converts the target with `point_census.encode_position`, refuses a target in another copy-A cell, then searches fine pairs closest-first around the request (`--span`, default 6) for one whose page re-encodes into its **original** 4-byte slot. Only the edited page is re-encoded (`regen_chain(..., force=False)`); unchanged pages keep their factory streams. The leaf's fine word is rewritten with its original flag bits (`hi = old & 0xF800`, `lo = old & 0xF800`). Verification re-runs `point_census.census(want_joins=True)`, decodes the moved record, and compares every byte of the file against the factory one, allowing only the page's slot and the leaf's four fine bytes.
3. **Controls.** *Positive*: the synthetic file of `point_census.synth_blob` (both POIs, census + joins green) moved by the tool; on the factory page, re-encoding the unchanged page with stock 1.3.1 reproduces all 74 pages of the pair (`check_factory_encoder` 0 not reproduced for chains 1000/400/2000). *Negative (must fail)*: the same move with `--control leave-leaf` (record moved, leaf left) must drop the spatial join; with `--control leave-record` (leaf moved, record left) the record must not decode to the target; a target in another copy-A cell must be refused; an encoder that is not stock 1.3.1 must be refused; a record with no leaf must be refused.
4. **Scope.** One pair, `POINT010`, 9,864,704 decoded bytes, 15,469 name records, 53 spatial trees with 15,378 leaves, 107 tries, 35,526 index records, 13,999 `INFORMATION` records. Step 1 only (move within one copy-A cell). Excluded: a move to another cell (step 2); a new POI (trie/index/phone records); the US card; the fallback page-growth path (a move whose page does not fit its slot is refused, not relocated); the firmware reader (M3.34/M3.35).
5. **Falsifier.** As in Hypothesis.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** a POI moves inside its copy-A cell with two edits (name record `rec[6..9]`, the leaf fine word) and an in-place re-pack of its name page; on `POINT010` record 0 this lands 1.95 m from the request, census `unaccounted=0`, joins 15,378/15,378 (same as factory), 0 bytes changed outside the page slot and the leaf, the chain table unchanged, and the output pair is accepted by `cmpind.py verify` and both `point_census` readers.
- **FACT:** the negative controls fail as required: a moved record with the leaf left drops the spatial join to 15,377/15,378 (`point_census joins` `VERDICT: FAIL`); a moved leaf with the record left decodes 75.84 m away; a target in another copy-A cell, a non-1.3.1 encoder and a record with no leaf are all refused.
- **OBSERVED:** the fine search is not free: accepted places are on a sparse grid because the page must re-encode into its original slot, so a requested position can be up to ~3 m from the nearest accepted fine pair on a page with no slack (records 0, 700 with `--span 6`); `--span 40` reached within 2 m for 33 of 39 sampled records and refused 6 (2 requested a cell they do not own, 4 had no fine pair within 2 m in 80 steps).
- **OBSERVED:** the accepted positions are within 2 m by construction, not by luck: the tool refuses when the closest accepted fine pair is farther, so a `verify: PASS` line always means ≤ 2 m.
- **HYPOTHESIS (untested):** a page whose factory stream has no 1–2 B slack is the common case; a real writer that must hit an arbitrary OSM coordinate exactly needs the page/chain to grow (relocate, M4.47), which this step deliberately does not do.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the firmware reads the leaf's fine word or the record's (M3.35); this step writes both so the answer does not change the output's consistency, but only an L3/L4 run can confirm the unit displays the new point.

## Conclusion

M5.63's done-when is met for step 1 on a real EU 2011 pair. `tools/point_move_poi.py` (`--selftest`, 21 checks) writes a pair in which the chosen POI (record 0, "ADAMS OF GLIN") decodes 1.95 m from the requested position, `point_census.py census` reports `unaccounted=0` and `joins` reports 15,378/15,378, the changed bytes stay inside the touched page's 4-byte slot and the leaf, a no-op run is byte-identical, and a pair with the record moved and the leaf left is refused by `joins` (15,377/15,378). The move is confined to one copy-A cell; a 39-record sample at `--span 40` moved 33 and refused 6, all without changing anything outside the page slot and leaf. A move to another cell (step 2) and a new POI remain open.

## Corrections after navigator/TL review

TL review (2026-11-21) found the standing gate red on `quality (py3.11/3.13/3.14)`: the selftest's "a non-factory encoder is refused" case depended on the **host's** default zlib (it passed on a machine whose `python3` is zlib 1.3.1 and failed on the CI runners, which carry stock 1.3.2 and zlib-ng). The measured numbers in this record are unchanged; the selftest now builds a *synthetic* foreign encoder (`version = "0.0-host-independent"`, `is_reference = False`, wrapping this host's compressor) and additionally drives `main()` with `--libz` under a monkeypatched `regen.Encoder`, asserting exit `zlibinfo.EXIT_UNVERIFIABLE` before the pair is read. The tool's runtime guard is unchanged: it still refuses a non-`--libz` encoder and one that does not reproduce the factory streams. `--selftest` is 21 checks and passes on `python3` (zlib-ng 1.3.1), `python3.13` and `python3.11` (stock 1.3.2).
