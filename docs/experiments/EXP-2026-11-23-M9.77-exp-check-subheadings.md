## Question

Can `tools/exp_check.py` accept a record whose five Method parts are `### 1. …`
… `### 5. …` subheadings (EXP-M4.96) without weakening the five-part rule, the
full-SHA-256/waiver rule or the elided-command rule, and without editing any EXP?

## Hypothesis

Matching Method parts by content under `## Method*` — accepting both
`1. **Label.**` and `### N. Label`, concatenating multiple `## Method …`
sections and stripping a trailing parenthetical from a label — will PASS
EXP-M4.96 while the missing-part, hash, elision and no-command checks keep
firing on their controls and the corpus FAIL count drops. Falsifier: a record
missing a part PASSes, a sample without a hash/waiver PASSes, `# ...` in Commands
PASSes, M1.50 or M8.H35 becomes PASS, a no-command record PASSes, or the corpus
count does not drop.

### 4. Scope

`tools/exp_check.py` (`check_text`, `section_bodies`, new `part_norm`,
`part_markers`, the `SUBHEADING_PART_RE`/`METHOD_ALIAS_RE`/`PART_SUFFIX_RE`
patterns and the `--selftest` fixtures) plus one sentence in
`docs/experiments/TEMPLATE.md` naming the accepted subheading form. No EXP is
edited and no claim row is changed beyond the task's reserved C-9950–C-9959.
The tool still reads only checked-in prose; it checks presence by name, not
whether a command is true or a Controls section genuinely contains both cases.
The `## Method` alias uses a word boundary (`^method\b`), so `## Methodology`
and `## Approach` are not aliased. Matching normalises case and whitespace; a
trailing parenthetical is stripped only from part labels. The no-command guard
requires a backtick in the Commands body; it adds no reason to a record that
already fails for another reason only if that record also lacks a command, and
0 records fail solely on it.

### 5. Falsifier

The change is wrong if any of the following is observed: a record missing a
part PASSes; a Sample without a full SHA-256 and without a backticked
`none (tool-only task)` waiver PASSes; `# ...` in part 2 PASSes; M1.50 or
M8.H35 becomes PASS; a record with no runnable command PASSes; the corpus FAIL
count does not drop; an EXP is edited to make a check pass; either selftest
fails; or the claim/todo checkers go dirty.
