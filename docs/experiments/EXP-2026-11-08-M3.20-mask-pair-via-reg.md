## Question

What is the correct instruction form and build-A count of the register-AND
mask variant that M3.11 §2 reported as immediate `and #0x70,r0` then
`cmp/eq #0x60`?

## Hypothesis

The site is a register-AND form (EXP-2026-11-08-M3.19-sh4scan §5 identified
`mov #0x70,R3; and R3,R0; cmp/eq #0x60,R0` at Navi.exe `0x3715c8–0x3715cc`).
A `mask-pair --via-reg` scan over build A uncompressed sections should find
exactly that site (count 1) with default `--and 70 --cmp 60`. **Falsifier:**
any count other than 1, or the reported site not being `0x3715c8/ca/cc` R3.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** IMG256 head, SHA-256 as above (printed by sh4scan).
2. **Commands** (run from repository/worktree root):

```bash
IMG=/home/marek/projekty/OSM2Leaf/archive/head256.img
python3 tools/sh4scan.py --selftest
python3 tools/sh4scan.py "$IMG" --image-start 0x200200 dump --module Navi.exe --va 0x3715c0 --count 8
python3 tools/sh4scan.py "$IMG" --image-start 0x200200 mask-pair --via-reg --cmp 60
python3 tools/claims_check.py
```

Pattern definition (LE halfwords, even addresses): `mov #imm,Rn` =
`0xE000 | (n<<8) | imm`; `and Rn,R0` = `0x2009 | (n<<4)` (SH-4 `AND Rm,Rn`,
Rm=n, Rn=R0); `cmp/eq #tag,R0` = `0x8800 | tag`. `mask_pairs_via_reg` matches
the same register `n` across mov and and, then cmp, within `--window-bytes`
(default 14 B) from mov to cmp.

3. **Controls.**
   - *Positive, synthetic (`--selftest`, section 4b):* the exact M3.19 site
     halfwords `E370 2039 8860` are found as `(0,1,2,3)` with `--and 70 --cmp 60`;
     window edge at 14 B found, 16 B rejected; wrong imm/cmp/register pairing
     rejected; `mov #0x70,R0; and R0,R0; cmp/eq #0x60,R0` found (R0 case).
   - *Positive, non-match:* the via-reg sequence is NOT matched by immediate
     `mask_pairs(..., 0x70, 0x60, 14)` (selftest pins this).
   - *Negative/control from M3.19:* immediate `mask-pair --and 70 --cmp 60`
     finds 0 (EXP-2026-11-08-M3.19-sh4scan §3/§5); the via-reg variant explains
     the published count 1.
   - *Real bytes:* precheck dump shows `e370 mov #0x70,R3`, `2039 and R3,R0`,
     `8860 cmp/eq #0x60,R0` at `0x3715c8/ca/cc`.
4. **Scope.** Build A uncompressed o32 sections only (527 sections, 282
   uncompressed, 23,559,542 B psize / 23,513,170 B vsize; 245 compressed not
   scanned), same corpus as M3.19. Build B/C not scanned.
5. **Falsifier.** Stated in Hypothesis: count != 1 or wrong site/register.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** The register-AND form `mov #0x70,R3; and R3,R0; cmp/eq #0x60,R0`
  appears once in build-A uncompressed sections, at Navi.exe sec0
  `0x003715c8–0x003715cc` (R3, +4 B). This reproduces M3.19 §5 and the site
  M3.11 §2 named.
- **FACT:** M3.11 EXP-2026-11-06 §2 table row described this as immediate
  `and #0x70,r0` then `cmp/eq #0x60` — incorrect instruction form for that
  site. Corrected by C-2560 and by a minimal correction in M3.11 EXP §2 (no
  claim deleted; ledger correction via new claim row per M3.20 done-when).
- **FACT:** `tools/sh4scan.py mask-pair --via-reg` pins the variant in its
  synthetic selftest and runs on `--image-start 0x200200`.
- **OBSERVED (scoped):** Count is over 282 uncompressed sections only; 245
  compressed sections (56.24% of vsize) not scanned.

## Conclusion

M3.20 names the register-AND mask variant (`mov #imm,Rn; and Rn,R0;
cmp/eq #tag,R0`, `mask_pairs_via_reg` / `mask-pair --via-reg`) and confirms
the M3.11/M3.19 site as count 1 at Navi.exe `0x3715c8`. M3.11's pattern text
is corrected by C-2560 (and M3.11 EXP §2 text corrected) with no deletion.
M3.19 remains blocked on its original precheck criterion (its stop/discrepancy
is recorded); M3.20 provides the named variant and correction M3.19 asked for.
