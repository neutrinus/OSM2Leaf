## Question

M9.53 makes the data-access task read and inflate our road record and answers
`0x14b6` sub 3 (C-5918). Which client function receives that reply, which of
its copies carry our record's shape bytes, with what byte counts on F and G,
and does one changed link id change the copy?

## Hypothesis

The `0x14b6` sub-3 handler `0x0042613c` is the consumer: it finds the served
record's group, copies the record's shape region sized by the group's own size
word, and copies the record's tail at the header's offset/count words. A
one-link change in our record then changes the copied bytes (DIFFERENT), and a
change outside the copied region leaves them SAME.

**Falsifier (set before the runs):** the handler copies the record but loads none
of its link words. Then the record is consumed by another task that the
handler only notifies, and this EXP must name where the record goes next.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** the dispatcher `0x00427144` tests `+8 == 0x14b6`, then the kind
  word at `+16` (`0x0042718e`, `0x00427184`): kind 64 goes to `0x004268c0`
  (M4.54), kind 4 to `0x0042613c` (`0x00427192`). Sub 9 (no record, C-5918)
  is the branch `0x0042717c` that reads `+20` instead. So `0x0042613c` is the
  sub-3 client handler.
- **FACT:** the first copy is `memmove(shape_buf, rec+0x2c, [group+24])`,
  called at `0x0042636a` (thunk `0x003d13e8`); `[group+24]` is loaded at
  `0x00426360` and holds 11,264 on F and 512 on G — the **inflate output
  window** (`datask_l3`'s "output window `0x2c00` / `0x200`", the value
  `0x003edf5c` hands back), not the record length. The source is just after
  the 44-byte header, so on G only 140 of the 512 copied bytes are our
  record's data (a 184-byte record); the rest of the window is copied as it
  stands. The destination is `group+0x1a0` in the group entry found through
  `*0x00e99698` (`0x30887470` + 0x1a0 = `0x30887610` on F).
- **FACT:** the second copy is `memmove(tail_buf, rec + 4*w17, 4*w18)`, called
  at `0x004263ba`; on F `w17=2515`, `w18=278` give rec+0x274c / 1,112 B, on G
  `w17=41`, `w18=5` give rec+0xa4 / 20 B, and both end exactly at the
  record's end (11,172 / 184 B). The two `mov.w` loads at `0x0042638e` and
  `0x00426392` are the handler's only *instruction-level* reads of the record
  (the two copies go through the Python-modelled `0x003d13e8` thunk, which the
  engine reports as `api-r` at `pc 0x7e00004c`); word 17 must be non-zero and
  not `0xffff`, word 18 non-zero, else the tail copy is skipped
  (`0x00426398`/`0x0042639e`).
- **FACT:** one link id changed (word 59, inside the copied region) changes the 512 B copy
  (`6ffb5d4f…` -> `7fa26f1a…`, DIFFERENT) and leaves the 20 B tail SAME; a
  word outside the copy (word 4) leaves both copies SAME (control).
- **OBSERVED (negative):** no located function reads either copy after it is
  filled. The
  registered draw pass `0x00428200` (registered by `0x00427b4c`) calls
  `0x00427dc8`, `0x00427d64`, `0x00427cd4`, `0x004281a8`, `0x0042810c`; the
  whole pass and the dispatch make 0 guest reads of `b0`/`b1` (and 0 of the
  record). `0x004259bc` then posts the `0x0fa0`/`0x019a` message to `LOC_map`
  (`*0x011fff74`); `0x00421548` receives it and returns without touching the
  record.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the shape walk lives in the `LOC_map`/`LOC_db`/`raw_rec`
  tasks created by the launcher's `Navi_ctrl` section (`0x0032efa8`:
  `LOC_db` `0x111c0`, `LOC_map` `0x111c8`, `RAW_rec` `0x111d0`, with `LOC_map`
  entry `0x004216dc`), not in the renderer init this run drives; the next run
  must start those tasks or model them (follow-up M9.66).
- **UNKNOWN:** which of the three tasks walks the group buffers, and how the
  `0x0fa0` sub `0x019a` message reaches it.

## Conclusion

The Done-when holds: the function that takes our record is `0x0042613c`; it
copies the record's shape region at `rec+0x2c` sized by `[group+24]`
(11,264 B on F, 512 B on G — the inflate window) and its tail at `4*w17` for
`4*w18` (1,112 B on F, 20 B on G), and one changed link word (59) gives
DIFFERENT in the shape copy while an out-of-copy word (4) gives SAME. The Falsifier fires too: the
handler loads none of the link words; it notifies `LOC_map` with the `0x0fa0`
sub `0x019a` message, and the shape walk is not in this run. `reader-coverage.md`
row 17 records the copy and the hand-off; M9.66 (C-6750–C-6759) is the
follow-up that must find the shape walk. The
`GetSystemTime` model is a tool support needed to get the renderer init to its
receive loop.
