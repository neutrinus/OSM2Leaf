## Question

Marek's car test (L5) of the patched EU card on his US unit
(EXP-2026-11-23-M1.57, "Car test", item C5): the audio "works, but with an
odd lag; screen beeps OK, Bluetooth voice OK". What differs in the audio
path between the EU and the US 2011 card, and which difference can reach
the car?

## Hypothesis

Two candidates from the coordinator: (a) the EU `Audio.exe`/AUI layer talks
differently to the unit's audio microcontroller; (b) audio settings were in
`SRAM` area 0, which was erased before the test.
**Falsifier for (a):** a non-radio audio module of ROM A whose code differs
beyond build stamps (PDB GUID, timestamp, path), or an `Audio.exe` that
opens a non-radio device the other build does not.

## Inputs

| Item | Value |

| NK1 | `firmware/unit-flash/nk1-QY7200ND.bin` (the kernel the car runs) |

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** As above; only the system area (ROM A at
   `0x200000`) of each card is read.
2. **Commands.**

   ```bash
   EU=LEAF_2011-2012_QSD-309-101_navi158_eu.sys    # first 100 MiB of the EU image
   US=LEAF_2011-2012_US.sys                        # first 100 MiB of the US image
   python3 tools/audio_diff.py --selftest
   python3 tools/cerom.py modules --census $EU $US
   python3 tools/audio_diff.py modules $EU $US          # -v prints the strings
   python3 tools/audio_diff.py drivers $EU $US
   python3 tools/audio_diff.py tnr firmware/unit-flash/nk1-QY7200ND.bin
   python3 tools/audio_diff.py objtable $EU
   python3 tools/cerom.py --image-start 0x200200 $EU thunks Audio.exe | grep -E 'OBJDLL|Sram|PrivateArea'
   python3 tools/cerom.py --image-start 0x200200 $EU exports OBJDLL.dll | grep -o 'OBJ_Setting_read\w*'
   python3 tools/sh4dis.py $EU Audio.exe --image-start 0x200200 --va 0x35a98 --count 40
   ```

3. **Controls.** *Positive:* `Audio.exe` is known to differ (radio, M1.36)
   and must come out `functional`; the `tnr` table must reproduce C-5216
   (`TNR2:` Euro, `TNR3:`/`TNR4:` USA); `objtable` must start with
   `OBJ_SEM_CARSPEED` at index 0. *Negative:* the selftest feeds two blobs
   that differ only in a PDB path (must be `build-only`) and two that differ
   in one identifier (must not be).
4. **Scope.** 18 modules of ROM A (list in `AUDIO_SET`) plus the census of
   all 148 common modules; strings of ≥ 8 characters (ASCII and UTF-16LE).
   Not covered: code paths after a failed driver open (no emulator run;
   `-M ze0` has no audio microcontroller, M8.H45), the audio
   microcontroller's own firmware and EEPROM.
5. **Falsifier.** Stated under Hypothesis.

shell, `FAST_BUTTON_UPDOWN`, `SEEK_BUTTON_UPDOWN`, `Set/ReqCurrentPICode`,
`CreateAvOsm1/2`) and 4 US lines (error log formats, `RcvUnkownPage`).
Volume, mute, guidance-voice mute, auto volume, Dolby volume, subwoofer,
CD, USB, BT and AUX strings are the same set in both builds.

**O4 — devices each `Audio.exe` opens** (`audio_diff.py drivers`):

| | devices | IOCTL names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU | `AUD1: CEN1: LCD1: MMP1: NAV1: TNR2: VIN1:` | 26 (21 RDS/tuner) |
| US | `AUD1: CEN1: LCD1: MMP1: NAV1: TNR3: TNR4: VIN1:` | 10 |

i.e. the `CreateFile` pattern; on a result of `-1` it logs the RdsLib error (`6, 10, 27`,

**O6 — `SRAM` area 0 and audio.** `OBJDLL.dll` backup table
(`audio_diff.py objtable`): `0x03 DISCINFO`, `0x05 SETTING`,
`0x0A ODOMETER`, `0x0C LOCUS`, `0x10 AVOIDAREA`, `0x19 PLUGINPNT` are the
six objects that M6.24 O6 found in area 0 (log numbers 03 05 0A 0C 10 19).
The ones in other areas fit too: `0x06 CARPOS`, `0x07 CARPOSROAD`,
`0x09 ROUTEPNT` (area 1–3, "car position backup, history"), `0x17 TELSET`
in VFlash with the phone data. `OBJ_Setting` holds the navigation's sound
settings: `readGuideVol`, `readGuideBeep`, `readGuideVoice`,
`readGuide2InterruptAudio`, `readVoiceImpose`, `readStwSound`,
`readOnmapDispAudio`, `readExpLanguage`. `Audio.exe` imports from OBJDLL
only `OBJ_CarSpeed_*` and `OBJ_Odometer_*` (speed-dependent volume); its
own state goes through `commondll!CApi_GetSramVirtualAddress`,
`CApi_CommitSram` and `CApi_Read/WritePrivateArea` (which area was not
resolved: the calls go through a function table).

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** outside the radio, the EU and US 2011 audio applications are the
  same program. The wave drivers, the AUI runtime (`auirtdll`, `auiapp`),
  the audio device manager and the CD/AUX controls differ only in build
  stamps; `AuiMonitoringCC`, `BtAudioCC`, `UsbAudioCC`, `PhoneBTA` and
  `Speech.exe` are relinked with the same strings; `Audio.exe` talks to the
  same `AUD1:` device with the same volume/mute vocabulary (O2–O4). The
  falsifier for (a) did not fire.
- **FACT:** the EU `Audio.exe` drives FM/AM through `TNR2:` (RDS), and the
  unit's NK1 does not start `TNR2:` on a USA unit (O4, O5, C-5216). So with
  the EU card the radio cannot work at all on this unit: `OpenDriver`
  fails. The XM button has no handler in the EU build.
- **FACT:** in the car the audio drivers are the unit's own (NK1); the card
  changes only the applications.
- **OBSERVED:** erasing area 0 reset the navigation's `OBJ_Setting` (guide
  volume, guide beep, guidance voice, "guidance interrupts audio", voice
  impose, steering-wheel sound) and the odometer to firmware defaults (O6;
  the log numbering = table index is consistent for all 11 objects of
  M6.24).
- **HYPOTHESIS (most likely cause of the "lag"):** the EU `Audio.exe`'s
  radio half fails on the missing `TNR2:` and keeps the source manager
  waiting (start-up, source switch, the FM/AM key, mute/un-mute around
  beeps and guidance), which is heard as delays. Supporting: this is the
  only functional difference in the audio path. Not shown: what the caller
  of `OpenDriver` does on failure (retry, timeout, or carry on).
- **HYPOTHESIS (second):** default `OBJ_Setting` sound values (e.g. guidance
  interrupting audio, guide volume, beep) make it sound different from
  before; this would change levels and ducking, not timing.
- **UNKNOWN:** where `Audio.exe` keeps bass/treble/balance/speed-sensitive
  volume (its `SRAM` area id, or the audio microcontroller itself), so
  whether area 0 touched them.

## Conclusion

Claims C-10210–C-10217 added. The EU card's audio stack is the US one
except the radio, and the radio cannot run on a USA unit because its tuner
device `TNR2:` is Euro-only in the unit's kernel. The lag is most likely the
EU `Audio.exe` waiting on that missing tuner; the area-0 reset changed the
navigation's sound settings but is unlikely to cause lag. Open: the failure
path after `OpenDriver` (static) and the car test below.

**Next car test (about 10 minutes, EU card in, engine on, parked):**

1. Note what "lag" is: delay after a key press, delay after a volume
   change, a gap when switching source, or sound that stutters/is late
   all the time.
2. Press FM·AM: expected no station / no tuning (confirms O5). Press XM:
   expected nothing.
3. Play the same source (Bluetooth audio or USB) for one minute, with and
   without touching the screen: is the lag there when the radio is never
   selected?
4. Settings → Audio: note bass/treble/fader/balance/speed-sensitive volume;
   Settings → Navigation → Guidance / Voice: note volume and "interrupt
   audio" (are they defaults?).
5. Same steps with the US card, for comparison.

If the lag is only around the radio or source switching, the cause is the
missing `TNR2:` and the fixes are: (i) a card whose image A has the US
`Audio.exe` (and `RBDS_CC`/`XMCC`) in place of the EU one (task to plan),
or (ii) do not use the radio. If it is the same on BT/USB with the radio
never selected, look at area 0 settings and the audio microcontroller.
