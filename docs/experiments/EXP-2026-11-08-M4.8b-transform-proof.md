## Question

Does the M4.8 transform (`tools/coord.py`, origin 52742 for POINT/ADDRE ids,
origin 52736 for MAPAL001 stream keys) reproduce the real position of named
POIs in the EU and US `POINT` shards, to within the packed-grid tolerance, and
which origin and local scale fit the data?

## Hypothesis

H1: a POI copy-A pair decoded with origin 52742 lands within one packed unit
of a building-level reference point. One unit is 1/192 deg lat (about 580 m)
and 1/128 deg lon (about 730 m at 33 deg N); the rounding half-unit gives
about 290 m and 365 m.

H2: origin 52742 is the correct longitude origin for POINT ids, and 52736
(a 6-unit, 0.046875 deg, shift) is wrong.

H3 (local scale): scale 2 (`0..2047`, C-960) is the local scale, pinned by a
POI sub-unit field.

Falsifier for H1: any reference residual larger than about 1 km in either
axis. Falsifier for H2: origin 52736 gives a lower median error than 52742.
Falsifier for H3: no sub-unit field exists in the record; H3 is then
untestable by this method, not confirmed.

## Method

1. **Sample and hash.** The EU and US images above; the trees are the
   `cmpind`-decompressed `REFER001` files. `cmpind verify` is run on both trees
   before use (C-1907).
2. **Commands.**

```bash

python3 tools/cmpind.py verify /home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/m5.12-us/root-factory/REFER001
```

   The `find` line is the example for P01; the 16 locations (shard, stream
   offset, record index) are in the reference table, and `find` was run on
   each shard to obtain them.
3. **Controls.** *Positive*: the decoder finds the named record at the stored
   position (`find` prints the name for each of the 16 rows), and
   `poi_point_check --selftest` checks copy A/B extraction and the haversine
   metric; `coord.py` check 18 pins the origin. *Negative*: each decoded point
   is compared with the **next** POI's reference (shuffled reference). A
   third control is the copy-A/copy-B agreement rate, which shows the two
   copies are not independent.
4. **Scope.** 11 US `POINT` shards (7,769,349 name records) and 33 EU shards
   (2,657,487 name records), counted from zlib-framed inner pages
   (`iter_streams`, 2,000..400,000 B). Pages that are not zlib-framed
   (bit-packed, `00 xx 00 yy`, C-refer) are excluded and not counted. Only 16
   POIs have a reference point. They are Wikipedia-articled landmarks and
   airports; street-address city halls and civic offices were excluded because
   their Wikipedia coordinates are town-centre points, not building points.
   Crane Point Museum was excluded (no coordinates on its article). The 16 were
   selected before any residual was computed (the full candidate list is in
   the session; no candidate with a coordinate was dropped after the residuals
   were known).
5. **Falsifier.** As stated above; both are fixed before the result.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** copy A of a POI name record is the packed position, at bytes 2..5
  (C-1900). The decoder finds the named records (names printed by `find`).
- **OBSERVED:** longitude under origin 52742 is centred on zero (median 103 m)
  and origin 52736 is about 4.5 km off (C-1902). 52742 is the origin for POINT
  ids, consistent with C-111. H2 is **supported**.
- **OBSERVED:** the transform does **not** place the POIs within one packed
  unit (C-1904). All 16 residuals exceed 1.5 km. H1 is **refuted** under its
  stated falsifier (1 km).
- **OBSERVED:** latitude residuals are positive for all 16 POIs (median
  +2,763 m, about 4.8 x-units). The cause is not known. It is not pinned as a
  constant, because a constant that fits the 16 would be a fit, not a
  transform fact (C-1903).
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the residuals reflect how the POI point is stored (a
  street or entrance point rather than the building centre) rather than an
  error in the transform. The data do not separate the two: the airports
  (P02..P05, 3.0..6.8 km) and the museums (P06..P13) show similar magnitudes.
- **UNKNOWN:** local scale. Packed POI ids cannot pin scale 1 vs scale 2 (both
  decode the same packed pair). H3 is open (C-1906).

## Conclusion

The M4.8 transform is confirmed for the **longitude origin** of POINT ids
(52742), by 16 real POIs across US and EU shards. It is **not** confirmed at
metre level: the packed grid plus POI point positions give median 3.6 km
errors, with a latitude bias that is unexplained. The M4.8 done-when "within a
few metres" is not met (C-1904). The local-scale clause of the M4.8b
done-when cannot be answered from packed POI ids, so the task is marked
`blocked` (the blocker and unblock are in TODO.md). `docs/format/` is unchanged;
the claims are C-1900–C-1907 and C-960 remains a proposed claim.
