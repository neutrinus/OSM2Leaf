## Question

What is the record layout of the section-2 kinds `0x2046` and `0x3e4` that `rd_check` cannot read,
and can one factory regulation word be matched to a known OSM turn restriction?

## Hypothesis

The kinds share the level-1 unit-0 grammar (node triples, entries with regulation words; M4.2 and
`rd_check`), with a larger body. The regulation words of a `no_*` restriction are the entry's word
naming the from-entry, as the writer assumes. Falsifiers: (a) a kind's unit 0 fails the body test;
(b) no regulation word on the to-entry of `r120176` names the from-entry, and no control separates
the junction; (c) the word test on the `no_*` population separates from its shuffle control with hit
rate at least 0.80 (threshold fixed before the run, in `PASS` in the tool).

   `…` is the local path (`/home/marek/projekty/leafre-work/…`). The Wrocław tile is extracted first
   (see Samples). The census runs every section-2 directory kind; `rd_check.check_tile`'s kind
   assertion is not used.
3. **Controls.**
   - *Reader:* kind assertion bypassed; `rd_check.check` (its body test) is the reader's own check on
     unit-0 records; `rd_check.part_a` splits level 2/4/5 raw parts. The `rd_check` header test is
     not used as evidence (see Observations, item 6).
   - *Junction, positive:* `r120176` must find a node at the via (JUNCTION_TOL 8 units), four
     non-edge entries, and four OSM bearings matched one-to-one within 25 degrees.

     matching (the matching uses bearings only).
   - *Word test, negative:* shuffle control: for each restriction the to-entry is replaced by a

   - *Coarse lookup:* the file itself is the control (every level-4/5 code must be reproduced by
     `clarion_bg.parcel_code` over a grid); it failed, so no coarse junction conclusion is drawn.
4. **Scope (counted corpus).**

## Observations

### Census (`census`), counted corpus

Directory kinds and records (levels as the directory's level):

| Level, kind | Tiles | Records | Unit 0 | Body test pass (unit 0) | Note |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1, `0x1046` | pinned + B48R058R | 540 + 65,274 | all | 540/540, 65,273/65,274 | 256-byte body |

| 2, `0x2046` | B48R058R | 8,142 | 4,071 | 4,070/4,071 | |
| 4, `0x3cde` | LEV3456R | 760 | 380 | 379/380 | 704-byte body |
| 5, `0x03e4` | LEV3456R | 68 | 34 | 34/34 | 176-byte body |

Observations:

1. **Kinds.** `0x2046` is the level-2 kind on B28R070R, B30R048R and B48R058R; the level-1 section-2
   kind on those tiles is `0x1046`. In `LEV3456R`, `0x03e4` is the level-5 kind and `0x3cde` the
   level-4 kind. `0x2046` also appears in section 1 (road shapes), which is not in scope.
2. **Grammar.** Unit 0 is read with `clarion_rd.parse_record`: 27 words of header, then a node is
   three words (entry count `<<12 | x`, class `<<12 | y`, flags), an entry is seven words
   (`other, w4, w5, id, cls, a, b`) followed by `(w5>>6)&0x3f` words. Unit 0 is the unit that
   `pairs[0]` names: word 27 on 3,824 of 4,161 level-2 records. The other offsets were not analysed.
3. **Pairing (levels 2, 4, 5).** Records pair by slot index: even k has unit 0 and odd k has
   `pairs[0] = none` and a unit 1 at `pairs[1]`. The unit-1 tail is the parcel code `(w0, w1)` of
   the even partner on all 4,161 level-2 pairs, 380 level-4 pairs and 34 level-5 pairs. Level 1 has
   no pairing (every level-1 record carries unit 0).
4. **Part b is not decoded.** The writer's part b (`rd_encode.build_record_edges`) and the factory
   odd record differ:

   | Check (per odd record) | Factory | Writer |
   | --- | ---: | ---: |
   | unit-1 length `2+4n+3E+2` (n nodes, E entries) | 4,160/4,161 L2, 379/380 L4, 34/34 L5 | 0 of 4,161 L2, 0 of 380 L4, 0 of 34 L5 |
   | unit-1 length `2+4n+3E+3` (writer) | 0 of 4,161 L2, 0 of 380 L4, 0 of 34 L5 | |
   | unit-1 tail | parcel code (w0, w1) on every pair | writer: `[hdr0, hdr1, 0]` |
   | mark word | `0x?100`, high nibble 2, 4, 6, 8, a, c | writer: `0xa100` |

   The counts are the notes printed by `census` (`length = 2+4n+3E+2 (factory)` and
   `length != 2+4n+3E+3`); the one L2 and one L4 pair outside the factory length are not analysed.

   The node block and entry words do not follow one fixed pattern (the first word of a node block
   is `0x2000` or `0x3000`; the side word takes many values; the third word of an edge entry is not
   its `other`). The part-b grammar beyond the length and tail is **UNKNOWN**; `part_b_check` is a
   checker of the observed layout, not a decoder. Its strict form passes 17 of 90 level-2 pairs of
   the pinned tiles and 3 of 4,071 of B48R058R.
5. **Arrivals carry no words (writer disagreement).** An arriving entry is `w5 & 0xc000 == 0x8000`.
   No arrival carries a regulation word: 0 of 213,722 level-1 arrivals (pinned 1,190 + B48R058R
   212,532), 0 of 71,504 level-2, 0 of 335,858 level-4, 0 of 38,657 level-5. Words with high nibble
   `0xf` and byte1 `0xff` (the "every entry" form) occur 0 times on level 1, 2 on level 2, 15 on level
   4 and 4 on level 5, and none of them sits on an arrival. The writer (`rd_encode.oneway_regulations`)
   puts `0xf0ff` on every arrival, so the factory disagrees with the writer on this point.
6. **Carriers.** No `0xff` word sits on an arrival carrier (zero `… -> arr` pairs). Named entries
   come from all classes. The `rd_check` header test is not used here: it fails on most level-1
   records (for example 303 of 303 on `B28R070R`, "units end at 87 of 88 words"); this is not
   investigated.
7. **Second nibble (bits 11-8).** The writer leaves them clear. In the pinned level-1 `0xff` words
   (2,270) bits 11-8 equal the carrier index on 213, equal the high nibble on 173, and are other on
   1,884. Their meaning is **UNKNOWN**.
8. **Other byte1 values.** Words whose byte1 is not `0xff` are common (level 1: 1.21 M in
   B48R058R, 2,063 pinned; e.g. `0x1101`, `0xf101`, `0x0102`). Byte1 `0x01`–`0x06` with bit 7 clear
   are not the writer's form and their meaning is **UNKNOWN**. The w5 low nibble (tail count) is
   nonzero on entries at all four levels (level 1, pinned tiles: 1,185 entries); its meaning is
   **UNKNOWN**. The w4 word is 0 in the writer and `0x1002`/`0x1202` at the via node; its meaning is
   **UNKNOWN**.

### Junction `r120176` (control and positive)

9. **Level-1 location.** The via lies in parcel (row 2452, col 13728) of B48R058R (the
   parcel code of the record is `(42588, 54924)`), at node 96, 5 units from the quantised via in y
   (about 6 m). Its four entries are all non-edge, and they match the four OSM ways at the via (the
   one-way classes agree with the OSM directions, checked by hand from this table):

   | Entry | Far node | Tile bearing | OSM way (bearing) | Error | Class (w5) | Regulation words |
   | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
   | 0 | 224 | 72.2° | `w33644761` Świdnicka, starts at via (72.2°) | 0.0° | leaving (`0x4040`) | `0x33ff` |
   | 1 | 320 | 341.0° | `w372859452` Piłsudskiego, ends at via (341.3°) | 0.3° | arriving (`0x8000`) | none |
   | 2 | 95 | 246.5° | `w16314723` Świdnicka, ends at via (247.9°) | 1.4° | arriving (`0x8000`) | none |
   | 3 | 98 | 158.3° | `w33644762` Piłsudskiego, starts at via (162.6°) | 4.3° | leaving (`0x4080`) | `0x03ff`, `0x23ff` |

   Entry 2 is the from way and entry 3 the to way. Entry 3 carries `0x23ff`: the high nibble is 2
   (the from entry) and byte1 is `0xff`. The restriction is therefore stored on the to-entry,
   naming the from-entry, as the writer's convention would have it, for this one case.
10. **Level-2 location.** The same junction is found in the level-2 graph of B48R058R with the same
    entry numbering (from 2, to 3) and the same word on entry 3.
11. **Controls at the junction.** Entry 0 (the straight continuation of Świdnicka) carries no word
    naming entry 2, so the straight pair is not barred. Entry 3 also carries `0x03ff` (naming entry 0,
    a leaving entry); the leaving entries 0 and 3 bar each other. Arrivals 1 and 2 carry nothing.
12. **Word test over the `no_*` population** (`match`, level 1 and level 2):

13. **Only relations.** The `only_*` convention ("every other exit entry carries the from-word") does

## Interpretation

- **Kinds, unit 0 (established, tool, real data).** `0x2046` (level 2), `0x03e4` (level 5),
  `0x1046` (level 1) and `0x3cde` (level 4) carry the same unit-0 grammar and differ in body size
  (C-2620, C-2621). The census finds 0 of 65,814 level-1 records and 0 of 4,071 level-2 part-a
  records without a unit 0 (the exceptions are body-test failures, one record per tile).
- **Part b (not established).** Odd records have a unit-1 length `2+4n+3E+2` and a parcel-code tail;
  the writer's `+3` and its `0xa100` mark are not the factory bytes (C-2622, C-2623). The rest of the
  record is **UNKNOWN**, so the layout is claimed for part a only.
- **Regulation words (partly established).** Arrivals carry no words; carriers are never arrivals;
  the factory's `0xf0ff` "no entry" word is absent from arrivals, against the writer (C-2624,
  C-2625). Bits 11-8 and byte1 values other than `0xff` are **UNKNOWN**.
- **One restriction matched (established for one junction).** `r120176` on B48R058R at level 1 and
  level 2: the to-entry carries `0x23ff`, the from-entry's nibble and `0xff` (C-2626). One junction
  is one case; it does not establish the convention.
- **Convention test (fails pre-stated threshold).** Over 411 (level 1) and 235 (level 2) `no_*`
  relations the to-entry carries the from-word on 37 % and 55 %, against 4 % and 3 % shuffled. The
  signal is real (far above the shuffle) but well below 0.80. The best-defined subset (arrival to
  exit) reaches 65 % and 71 %. The missing cases are not explained by this EXP; candidates to test
  are mapping errors (way splits at the via, two-way entries), a different encoding for part of the
  population, and the level-1 graph not containing every junction. The status is therefore FAIL, not
  "encoding confirmed" (C-2627).
- **`only_*` (negative for the tested convention).** The "all other exits barred" form does not
  separate from the shuffle (C-2628).
- **Firmware (not established).** Only the LEV3456R section-2 reader VA is in the repo (C-2629).
  The regulation-word consumer VA is UNKNOWN.
- **Done-when.** (i) the layout of both kinds: part a established, part b not; (ii) one regulation
  word matched to `r120176`: established; (iii) VAs where the firmware reads it: not located for the
  grid kinds. (i) and (iii) are not met, and the word test (ii) fails the pre-stated threshold, so the
  task is **blocked**, not done.

## Unknowns (listed, not claimed)

1. Part-b grammar: mark high nibble, node-block first word (`0x2000` vs `0x3000`), side word, third
   word of edge entries, and the reason for the non-`0xa100` marks.
2. Regulation-word bits 11-8 (second nibble) and byte1 values other than `0xff` (`0x01`–`0x06` with
   bit 7 clear, and `0xf101`-style words naming all entries).
3. The w5 low nibble (tail count) and the w4 word (`0x1002`, `0x1202` at the via).
4. `only_*` encoding; way-via relations; `restriction:<mode>` variants.
5. Why 63 % of `no_*` to-entries carry no from-word (two-way entries are the largest group).
6. The `rd_check` header test failure ("units end at 87 of 88 words") on most level-1 records.
7. Coarse (level 4/5) parcel addressing: `clarion_bg.parcel_code` reproduces none of the 414 codes.
8. Firmware: the reader VA of grid-tile section 2 (`0x1046`/`0x2046`), and the consumer of
   regulation words (rule 13: nothing here is firmware-confirmed).
