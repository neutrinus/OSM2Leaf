## Question

Can the 5 POI records M5.12c left unedited (4 × `RAB ORCHARD` on
`POINT006` page `0x12d94cc0`, 1 × `HARDEE'S` on `POINT008` page
`0x152e4a08`) be renamed without overrunning any neighbour, and does the
firmware's page loader read the edited pages?

## Hypothesis

M5.12c measured the edited pages with zlib-ng (33,549 B for a 33,299 B
slot; 58,688 B for 58,616 B). With the factory encoder (stock zlib L6/m8,
M1.13) the edited page grows by a few bytes only, and the name chain's
pooled slack (M5.26 `regen`: pages after the edit shift, the chain table is
rewritten, nothing past the chain slot is touched) absorbs it, so no
relocation (M4.47) is needed. **Falsifier:** the re-packed chain exceeds its
slot (regen refuses), a byte outside the chain slot, its table or its base
reference changes, `cardcheck` fails, the image diff leaves the four files,
or the firmware page loader (US2011 `fn 0x00426480`) requests the edited or
the next page anywhere but at its new table position.

## Interpretation

- **FACT (C-3190):** the 5 records are renamed on a `cardcheck`-passing US
  image; inside each `.DAT` only the name chain slot, its table and the
  base reference's length word change; on the image only the four files'
  clusters and directory-entry size bytes change.
- **FACT (C-3191):** with stock zlib L6/m8 the edited pages grow by 5 and 7
  bytes, not by 250 / 72 as M5.12c measured with zlib-ng; the "unfit" verdict
  was an encoder artefact (M1.13), not a slot limit of the format.
- **FACT (C-3192):** the per-page slot rule (M5.21) refuses both pages
  because they abut their neighbours; the chain-level re-pack (M5.26) with
  196 / 132 B pooled slack fits them without relocation.
- **FACT (C-3193):** before this task an in-place `regen` that changed a
  chain's length left the M4.47 base reference's length word stale (the
  locator check refused it); `regen` now rewrites it.
- **OBSERVED (C-3194, L3):** the US2011 page loader requests the edited page
  with its new length and the shifted next page at its new offset, and the
  walker returns the record carrying `OSM2 RAB 01@`; the factory pair is
  requested at the old positions. Whether the loader uses the base length
  word is not shown (it computes the request from the table).
- **UNKNOWN:** how the renamed records look on screen (no car run).

## Conclusion

The 5 records are no longer unfit: re-packing the name chain with the
factory encoder fits them with 196 / 132 B to spare, the card passes
`cardcheck` with the diff confined to the four files, and the firmware page
loader reads the edited and the shifted pages at L3. `point_pair_regen.py`
now keeps the M4.47 base reference consistent after an in-place re-pack.
Relocation (M4.47) was not needed for these pages.
