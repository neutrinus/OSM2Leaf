## Question

The M1.22 baseline mapped **5/13** cited EU readers to a US address and
reported 8 `NOMATCH`. Are those 8 genuine rewrites, or a matcher weakness?
The M1.31 leaf census fixes the denominator (`635 leaf`, `1` cap-truncated,
`79` no-`rts`), so this task only has to classify the 8.

## Hypothesis

The NOMATCH cluster is the REFER family, which the M1.22 blocker line says
has real differences, so some should be genuine rewrites. **Falsifier:** if a
"rewrite" has an identical instruction stream (opcodes, registers, branch
structure) in the US build and differs only in literal-pool data, then it is
a matcher weakness, not a rewrite — and conversely, a genuine rewrite must
show a differing *instruction*, not just a differing literal.

## Interpretation

- **FACT:** 6 of the 7 NOMATCH are **not** rewrites. Their masked form stream
  (instruction forms with this build's literal slots blanked) is identical and
  unique in the US build; every differing halfword is either a literal-pool
  slot or the `disp` immediate of a `mov.l @(disp,pc)`; no opcode, register or
  branch differs. The old matcher could not say this because
  `fingerprint` hashes literal-pool *values* of the function's own slots and
  keeps the immediate fields of `mov.l @(disp,pc)`; a build that moves a data
  base (EU `sec1` at `0x55d000`, US at `0x581000`) changes those values.
- **FACT:** the seventh, `0x003fbed8` (ADDRE resolve), is a genuine rewrite:
  `bf` vs `mov.l @(pc),r3` at `+0x16c`, with a different resolved-call count (8 EU vs 7 US)
  and no masked-form twin in the US build.
- **OBSERVED:** the 13-VA table is now `5 UNIQUE / 7 DIFFERENT / 1 NOMATCH`.
  Over the whole module the CLI verdict path gives `UNIQUE 2392 / 7596`
  (`31.5 %`), `DIFFERENT 3214`, `CALLORDER 213`, `AMBIGUOUS 518`,
  `NOMATCH 1259`. The real signal is that **12 of 13 cited readers are
  located**, 5 exactly and 7 through a justified same-code/different-constant
  pairing.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether `0x003fbed8`'s US counterpart `0x00424850` is the same
  function (context says likely, the code says rewritten). Recorded as
  `HYPOTHESIS`, not a claim.

## Conclusion

The 8 NOMATCH in the M1.22 baseline were 7 matcher weaknesses + 1 rewrite, not
8 rewrites. `fn_match` now reports a NOMATCH whose masked form stream is
unique in B as `DIFFERENT` with the first differing literal, and the frozen
fixture re-pins those six rows (a DIFFERENT row is *stricter* than a NOMATCH
one: `compare_rows` also checks `us_candidate` and `diff_offset`).

The M1.22 "≥90 % UNIQUE" done-when is not met on the US build (`38 %`), and it
should not be: the EU→US difference is mostly relocated data, so a UNIQUE-only
metric measures data layout, not code. The task closes on the per-function
justification the block allows.
