## Judge (a): one role did everything, and it was the TL

Five TL logs, one PO log, no SE/QA/SM log since 2026-11-21 07:44Z. The TL logs
show why: each batch both launched the drivers *and* did the reviewer's job
("TL self-check" replaces the QA pass on solo tasks) and the SM/SE/QA prompts
have no run at all. This is the failure the fifth retro in a row names in some
form ("the roles that turn the retro into work were silent", 2026-11-21). It is
not a prompt gap — prompt_common tells every role to run and log. It is that the
cycle driver stopped launching the middle three roles and the TL absorbed their
work. A batch that "self-checks" its solo tasks and merges them is exactly the
solo-slippage the pair rules were meant to prevent, and the TL log's own
`M4.88`/`M8.G1` review-placeholder defects are the cost.

The fix is an action (launch SE and QA), not a rewrite of prompt_tl.
