**Six finished PRs are waiting while 21–35 merges passed them.** #338 H100,
#339 H114, #340 M9.55, #341 M3.29, #344 H115 (second TL batch, marked ready

merges cleanly. Two of them are the board's own first priorities:

  the software gate before any car test.
- **M9.53** is `done` on its branch: the firmware reads and inflates a road
  record we wrote. That is the P1 gate the Priorities section names first.

**The cause is mechanical, not human.** Every task PR rewrites the same four
files: `TODO.md`, `claims.md` and the two generated fn_audit snapshots
(`fn-audit-corpus-2026-11-16.json`, `fn-audit-l3-corpus-2026-11-20.json`).
The snapshots are derived from the whole document corpus, so two PRs that
each add one EXP always conflict in them, and the loser must merge `main`,
regenerate and push — after which the next merge makes it stale again. The
window shows the cost: 87 merge-main commits, 25 commits whose only content
is a snapshot re-sync, and the snapshots touched by 85 and 88 commits in eight
hours. With one batch this was noise. With three batches merging at once a PR
that is not merged within minutes of going green does not merge at all.
Action 1 (merge the six first) and action 2 (remove the conflict at its
source).
